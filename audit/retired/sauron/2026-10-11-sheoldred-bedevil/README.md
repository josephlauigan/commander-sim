# Sauron: Sheoldred, the Apocalypse -> Bedevil (2026-10-11)

What Sauron would lose if Sheoldred left to lead her own mono-black deck. The list has Sheoldred; the variant has
Bedevil (from the binder; the card that held Kefka's old slot) instead. Look-ahead AI, loose profile, 1,000 paired games
per tier (same seeds, seats and opponents' draws), code on main 1a9f797. `run.sh`.

| Tier | With Sheoldred | With Bedevil | Delta | Noise |

| T1 | 28.9% | 27.5% | -1.4 | ±3.4 |
| T2 | 34.6% | 30.8% | **-3.8** | ±3.6 |
| T3 | 25.5% | 22.0% | **-3.5** | ±3.1 |
| T4 | 24.2% | 22.3% | -1.9 | ±3.1 |
| T5 | 22.1% | 22.0% | -0.1 | ±2.8 |
| All | 27.1% | 24.9% | **-2.1** | about ±1.4 |

Sheoldred is worth about 2 points to Sauron, a real loss (beyond the noise over all tiers), most of it against
Tiers 2 and 3. For scale, Kefka leaving cost 1.8 points (`../2026-10-11-kefka-bedevil/`). Without her Sauron would
sit at about 25%, still around an even share.
