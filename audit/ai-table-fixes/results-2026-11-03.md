## 4. Verdicts

The deck rows, the within runs and the realism shares all move by 2.1 pts or less, and every move sits inside the printed intervals.

| fix | evidence | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| AI_SACSTEAL (sacrifice choice vs a steal-on-sacrifice opponent) | alone at t3: Korvold -0.3, Tergrid +0.3, nothing over 0.5 | level |
| AI_CMDRISK (commander recast into open removal) | alone at t3: Korvold +1.3 (11.7 -> 13.0), Aurelia -0.9, both inside the intervals | level (a small Korvold gain is possible, not shown) |
| AI_WIPEVALUE (board wipes that cost the caster) | alone at t3: Tergrid -0.7, Atraxa -0.8; the wipe decks in section 3 (cloud, galadriel, karn, sauron, thrasios, yshtola, urdragon) all within 1.2 | level |
| all three | section 1: 36 deck-share moves, largest +2.1 (Giada t0); section 2: Korvold -0.2, Brago -0.1; section 3: no own-tier average below -0.5 | level |

What "level" does and does not say: at 2000 games the fixes change no win rate, seat share or median round by more than the noise, and they do not
hurt any of JD's decks. They also do not show the hoped-for effect: Korvold stays LOW (11.5 against 11.7) and Tergrid stays HIGH. The one hint
(CMDRISK alone, Korvold +1.3) vanishes with all three on, so it is not a result. Wipes and sacrifices are rare per game, so a real but small effect
would need many more games than this to show.
