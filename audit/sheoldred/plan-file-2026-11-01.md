generic policy plus `data/plans/sheoldred.toml` by +1.9 over t3-t4 [+0.3, +3.5]. No sims in this task: the coordinator

her: the draw-punisher plays that make the table draw into her (gated on the deck key, so the generic policy never
made them), Exsanguinate as a finisher, and keeping her alive with Darksteel Plate.
