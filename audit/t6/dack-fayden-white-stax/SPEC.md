## Game plan

The deck is a combo deck with a stax fallback **[primer: S2 "when fast starts fail, the deck falls back on white stax"]**. It tries to resolve Dack Fayden ({4}{W}{W}, 4/6) as early as turn 2 with fast mana **[primer: S3]**. Dack's enters trigger reveals creatures until it has one per opponent (3 in a standard pod), puts them onto the battlefield under the Dack player, then gives one to each opponent **[Oracle]**. The Dack player controls the creatures' enter triggers, so the flicker and venture triggers are theirs while the bodies belong to the table **[primer: S1]**. Radiant Solar ventures into Lost Mine of Phandelver each time a nontoken creature enters under the Dack player; the loop is steered through Lost Mine to the Dark Pool room, which drains each opponent **[primer: S1, S2]**. Stance: race. It wants the win on the Dack turn, or the turn after if Dack is answered. If the first attempt fails or is too risky it sits back on a tax and lock shell (Trinisphere, Chalice, Defense Grid, Tabernacle, Static Prison, Blind Obedience, Deafening Silence, Cursed Totem) while holding protection for the next try **[primer: S2, S3 list these; the "sit back" stance is S2]**. The deck's own cheap spells are hit by its own taxes, which shapes its sequencing (see Turn by turn).

**What the TrafficKone primer (S8) confirms** [primer: TrafficKone]: the deck is "a zero-card combo from the command zone": cast Dack, order the triggers correctly, and hope the table kept no spot removal. Dack flips one creature per opponent, donates and goads them; a second Dack enter trigger (Felidar flicker) flips the rest. The kill is Preston + Felidar Guardian (Felidar's trigger and Preston's copy of it loop the flicker) + Radiant Solar or a Preston token copy of it, each creature entering ventures, and the Lost Mine Dark Pool room drains the table, "even through The One Ring". The second kill is two banishers (Solar + Fiend Hunter / All-Fates Stalker) trading Felidar back and forth. The win "most of the time comes in the end step" because slow flickers are needed to regain the donated pieces; some piles win in the main phase. Weakness: spot removal in response, and buried triggers can restart the loop if the opponent interacts.

**Limit of the primer for our list** [inference]: the primer'"'"'s claim that its creatures "flipped in any order assemble the winning board" holds **only for its six creatures, which include Flickerwisp**. Our list has **seven creatures and no Flickerwisp** (Werefox Bodyguard and Abdel Adrian instead). Flickerwisp can target any permanent and returns it at end step, which is how the primer'"'"'s piles regain control of donated pieces. Our list has no equal, so the "any order wins" guarantee does **not** carry over; see "First-flip classes" below.

Tutors: **Enlightened Tutor** finds only artifacts and enchantments (Cloudshift, Ephemerate are instants, not found) so it fetches *support*: Deafening Silence, Static Prison, Gleaming Splendor, Smothering Tithe, Blind Obedience, Defense Grid, Trinisphere, Chalice, Pithing Needle, Mana Vault, Sol Ring, The Mind Stone (a flicker engine at {5}{W},{T} then each end step). **There is no creature tutor in the list** [inference]: Scroll Rack, The One Ring, Staff of Domination and Karn find cards by dig. **Urza's Saga** chapter III fetches a {0}/{1} artifact (Mana Vault, Sol Ring, Chrome Mox, Lotus Petal, LED, Mox Diamond, Mox Opal, Jeweled Amulet, Vexing Bauble, Portable Hole, Voltaic/Manifold Key, Expedition Map). **Monumental Henge** digs five for a historic card (artifact, legendary or Saga: Preston, Solar are not historic; Mind Stone, Apple of Eden, Abdel Adrian are). **Fomori Vault** digs for artifacts. Because nothing finds the creatures, the line depends on the random flip.

**How likely is the flip to work?** [inference] Dack reveals until it has 3 creature cards from a shuffled library. With all 7 creatures in the library, the chance that Felidar is among the first three is 3/7 (43%), and that Felidar, Preston and Solar are all in it is 1/35 (2.9%). Every creature drawn or cast beforehand shrinks the pool: with 5 left, 1/10; with 4 left, 1/4; with 3 left, certain. This is why S1/S2 call the line "the shortest example" and say "many variations exist depending on which creatures Dack flips". The practical plan is to **deploy Preston (and Felidar/Solar) from hand first when mana allows, so the flip adds free copies**, and to treat Dack as a repeatable engine (Felidar, Cloudshift, Ephemerate, The Mind Stone, Eldrazi Confluence's exile-and-return mode all re-trigger Dack).

Other steal-back tools in the list [inference from Oracle]: Fiend Hunter, All-Fates Stalker and Werefox Bodyguard return the exiled card under its **owner's** control when they leave, and the owner of a donated creature is us. So flickering or killing our own Hunter takes a donated creature back and re-triggers its enter effect. Eldrazi Confluence ({2}{C}{C}, instant, three modes) can exile a donated creature and return it under its owner's control, three times in one spell. Touch the Spirit Realm's channel ({1}{W}) does the same at the next end step.

## Mulligan

No source covers it **[primer: none]**. By inference from the list:

- **Keep:** a hand that can make 6 mana with WW by turn 3–4 and holds either a protected Dack plan (Silence, Orim's Chant, Reprieve, Angel's Grace) or a reason to wait (stax pieces to cast first). Two lands plus Sol Ring or Mana Vault, or Ancient Tomb or City of Traitors with a mox, is a typical keep. A creature from the combo (Preston, Felidar, Solar) in hand is good, because it takes itself out of the random flip and does not need to be found.
- **Ship:** no white source and no way to make one; all-land hands; hands of only free mana (Moxen, LED, Petal) with nothing to spend it on; hands with Trinisphere/Chalice as the only play and no mana to start it.
- **Typical keep:** 2–3 mana sources (one fast), 1–2 stax or protection spells, 1 cheap tutor or draw (Enlightened Tutor, Scroll Rack, The One Ring).
- **Watch:** Mox Diamond needs a land to discard; Chrome Mox exiles a non-land card (use a spare spell, not the combo); Gemstone Caverns is free only if not on the play and costs a card.

## Turn by turn

All **[inference]** except where tagged.

- **Turns 1–2.** Land, then fast mana in this order: Moxen and Sol Ring and Mana Vault first, **before** Vexing Bauble, Void Mirror and Trinisphere, which counter or tax the deck's own free spells (Bauble counters zero-mana spells; Void Mirror counters spells paid without coloured mana, including Sol Ring paid with colourless; Trinisphere makes every cheap spell cost 3). The fast route is turn 2 Dack with 6 mana (S3 says turn 2 is possible with Ancient Tomb, Chrome Mox and the Monoliths **[primer]**). If Dack is protected (Silence or Orim's Chant up behind it, or Cavern of Souls for Human or Advisor), go. If not, deploy a cheap lock piece.
- **Turns 3–4.** Dack with backup, or the stax shell. First stax pieces: Chalice of the Void at 1 (hits Sol Ring, Moxen, Petal, LED, Bauble, Portable Hole) or at 2, Cursed Totem against Kinnan-style activated abilities **[primer: S3]**, Defense Grid and Trinisphere against a table that depends on cheap spells, Deafening Silence (one noncreature spell per turn each, which also caps the Dack deck's own protection), Static Prison on the best opposing permanent, Tabernacle (creatures cost {1} per upkeep, bad for all creature decks, including its own Dack and creatures). Karn at 4 shuts off opposing artifact abilities.
- **Later.** Hold Silence / Orim's Chant / Reprieve for the Dack turn. Cast Dack when the table is tapped or locked. Gleaming Splendor + Smothering Tithe, The One Ring and Scroll Rack refill hands. Sunscour (free) and Vanquish the Horde are reset buttons if the table is ahead; Abdel Adrian can save the deck's own permanents from them.

## Interaction

**Holds** [list]: Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile, Erode, Get Lost (kills creature, enchantment, planeswalker), Portable Hole (MV 2 or less), Static Prison (temporary exile; it needs {E} each turn, 2 turns), Touch the Spirit Realm, Soul Partition (exiles, owner may recast with {2} tax), March of Otherworldly Light (X=MV; exile white cards to cut cost), Razorgrass Ambush (3 to attacker or blocker), Eiganjo's channel (4 damage to attacker or blocker, cheaper with legendary creatures), Kozilek's Command, Eldrazi Confluence (needs {C}{C}); Silence, Orim's Chant (kicker stops attacks), Abeyance (target player no instants, sorceries or non-mana abilities, draws a card), Mandate of Peace (combat only: opponents can't cast, ends combat, exiles the stack), Calamity's Wake (exiles graveyards, no noncreature spells this turn), Reprieve, Bilbo's Gambit (both return a spell to hand; Bilbo's with a Treasure gift stops all spells this turn), Angel's Grace, Defense Grid, Void Mirror, Vexing Bauble, Chalice, Pithing Needle, Disruptor Flute, Damping Sphere, Cursed Totem.

**What it spends them on** [inference]:
- *Protection for Dack* (Silence, Orim's Chant, Abeyance, Reprieve, Bilbo's Gambit, Angel's Grace) on its own turn. Silence and Chant go on the stack **before** Dack is cast, so the table cannot answer the cast or the triggers with a spell (they do not stop abilities already on the stack). The first priority is Dack resolving, then the venture loop.
- *Removal* (Swords, Path, Erode, Get Lost, Fiend Hunter) is spent on the opponent's engine creature or on the creature that is about to make a win. Path and Swords give the target's owner something (a land, life): it spends them only on a real threat.
- *Stax* (Chalice, Trinisphere) is cast to stop **spells** that threaten the win, not ahead of time unless the table is clearly deck-heavy.

**When it lets something resolve** [inference]: anything that cannot win or stop its win within a turn or two (a 3-mana value spell, a mana rock). It saves free and cheap protection for Dack's turn and for the win turn of an opponent that is a turn from winning.

**Who it stops when two players threaten to win:** the one whose win is *sooner and harder to answer later*. Ties go to the combo deck (Kinnan, Rograkh and Silas, Kraum and Tymna with Breach) before the midrange deck [inference]. Stax pieces name or hit the strongest piece: Pithing Needle and Disruptor Flute name the commander or key piece (Needle for a commander ability such as Kinnan's, Flute for a spell); Cursed Totem stops creature abilities; Karn stops artifact abilities.

## What it fears

[inference unless tagged]

- **Counterspells** on Dack (Force of Will, Mana Drain, Counterspell). Answer: protection spells, Cavern of Souls, Silence/Chant before the cast, casting at an opponent's end step. The commander tax (+2) makes a recast cost 8.
- **Graveyard and stifle effects:** Stifle effects (Tishana's Tidebinder, Stifle) hit the trigger: Dack's enter trigger and the venture triggers. Graveyard hate doesn't matter.
- **Targeted removal on Preston / Felidar / Solar** in response to the loop. Answer: Angel's Grace for the player, Abdel Adrian (exile permanents until he leaves) or Cloudshift/Ephemerate to dodge removal.
- **Wipes** (Toxic Deluge, Damnation). They kill the creature package but leave the stax shell, which is mostly artifacts and enchantments.
- **The donated creatures.** An opponent who gets Radiant Solar ventures too, and a donated Felidar can flicker their own permanents. Goad forces them to attack, but not at Dack's player specifically **[Oracle: goaded creatures must attack a player other than the goader if able]** [inference: so goad rarely protects Dack's player]. A table that takes Solar and Felidar gains a flier with lifelink 3/6.
- **Artifact and enchantment hate** (Null Rod, Collector Ouphe, Stony Silence, Rest in Peace, Disenchant) against the stax shell and the artifact mana base (about 30 artifacts) **[S2: list is 31 artifacts]**.
- **Free counterspells** (Force of Will, Fierce Guardianship) are answered by Vexing Bauble (no mana spent) and Void Mirror (no coloured mana). Counterspells paid with mana are not; Silence or Chant must come first.
- **Combo speed.** Turn 1–2 wins from other decks (Rograkh and Silas, Kinnan). The deck's cheap spells are taxed by its own Trinisphere, so it must choose between speed and lock.

## First-flip classes (our seven creatures)

**Method.** Not a trace of the 35 piles. Assume all 7 creatures are in the library, the first flip is 3 of 7 (7 choose 3 = 35 equally likely sets), no opponent interaction, and we want the win **the same turn**. Piles are grouped by whether the first flip holds the two key pieces, Preston and Felidar. All of it is **[inference: rules sketch]**.

Facts the sketch leans on (Oracle text, checked on Scryfall 2026-10-17):
- Flipped creatures enter together, so Preston sees the others enter and triggers for each nontoken, uncast one; the trigger is ours even if Preston is donated right after. Preston'"'"'s copies are 0/1 Illusion tokens under **our** control and their enter triggers are ours.
- A **donated Preston no longer triggers for new creatures** (the "you" is the opponent). Preston only makes tokens on the turn he enters with the others, or after he is back under our control.
- Felidar'"'"'s trigger says "another target permanent **you control**": it can flicker Dack or our tokens, never a donated creature. Fiend Hunter ("another target creature"), Stalker (non-Assassin) and Werefox (non-Fox) can target donated creatures, and when they leave, the card returns under its **owner'"'"'s** control, which is us. So **a banisher we control (a Preston token copy is enough) plus one flicker of that banisher regains a donated piece**; the token ceases to exist and its leave trigger fires. This is the stand-in for Flickerwisp.
- Radiant Solar is the only venture source, so the drain needs Solar (or a token copy) entering repeatedly. A Felidar loop that includes Dack re-flips the library, so Solar usually appears if it is still in the library.
- Our flicker spells (Ephemerate, Cloudshift, Touch the Spirit Realm channel) cost {W}/{W}/{1}{W} on top of Dack'"'"'s six mana, so they usually need spare mana the turn Dack lands.

### Resolved by the lead (2026-10-17, Scryfall Oracle text of Dack Fayden, Helping Hand)

*When Dack Fayden enters, reveal cards from the top of your library until you reveal X creature cards, where X is the
number of opponents you have. Put those creature cards onto the battlefield, then shuffle. They're goaded for the rest
of the game. For each of those permanents, choose a different opponent. Each opponent gains control of the permanent
for which they were chosen.*

- **Every flipped creature is donated:** one per opponent (three in a pod), none kept.
- **They enter under Dack's controller first.** The cards are put onto the battlefield (under your control) and only
  then does control change, within the same ability. So their enter triggers, and Preston's "another nontoken creature
  you control enters, if it wasn't cast" trigger, are yours. The triggered abilities are controlled by you even after
  the permanents change hands. This is what makes the line work at all; the sketch's percentages assume it.
