# What the "unnamed" stops of Tier 6 combo attempts are (Tier 6 fix 2, 2026-11-02)

Branch `t6-stops` (origin/main a58fefb6). Brief: `rust/agents/t6-stops.md`. Source of the question:
`audit/t6-structure/diagnosis-2026-11-01.md`, S1, H2 and "4. What to change next", item 3: 30-34% of the combo attempts of
Kraum/Tymna, Rograkh/Silas and Rograkh/Thrasios are stopped at a t5 table and most name no card ("a cost, a ward or a
lock"). The fix is behind `AI_T6STOPS` (default on, `AI_T6STOPS=0` = origin/main byte for byte). No sim has been run on
the fixes: the queue is written (section 5) and the numbers go in a follow-up.

## 1. Where the label comes from

`t6stats::combo_stop(g, p, combo, by)` writes `stop|<combo>|<by>` and `by` is `t6stats::last_counter_name`: the last
card that countered or removed something (`g.last_counter`), or `?`. The report (`sim-cli/src/t6report.rs` 471-476)
prints `?` as "by an unnamed answer" and the all-`?` case as "no card named: a cost, a ward or a lock". The four
`combo_stop` sites in `impls/combos.rs` are: a cast piece that was not cast or was countered (`attempt_run`), the key
pieces answered at instant speed (`interrupted`), a declared step that returned false (`run_steps`), and a piece
answered on the battlefield (`combos::normal_play_window`). `g.last_counter` is set only by a counterspell resolving
(`stack.rs` 1156, `cast.rs` 922) or by `interrupted`; `attempt_run` and every step of `run_steps` clear it first.
So a step that fails for any other reason, and a stop whose real cause was an earlier step, both read `?`.

## 3. Classification

By stop (6 stops; 6 is too few for shares, take them as an order of magnitude and read the sim for the real shares):

| cause | stops | share | in the rough |
|---|---|---|---|
| a lock the ready test does not see (Void Winnower, Rule of Law): the line can never be cast, and is tried again every turn | 3 (k12 x2, s13) | 50% | the ready test calling a line ready that cannot complete |
| Ad Nauseam "ready" with the pieces not in hand: a dig that most often finds nothing | 2 (k9, s11) | 33% | the ready test again (the attempt fails by itself) |
| a counter war that spent the mana of the next step (k22) | 1 | 17% | real interaction, unlabelled (the label is lost at the next step) |

Real interaction that is just unlabelled: one in six. The rest are the ready test: nothing in the 59 games was a ward or a
cost the table paid (no Thalia or Sphere tax shows in `cost_of` failures; the debug lines showed `can_pay=true` for the
failed casts, `castable=false` for the locks).

Why Ad Nauseam is the biggest source for Kraum/Silas in the diagnosis (114 + 54 and 51 + 92 unnamed Ad Nauseam and
Oracle attempts): `ad_ready` (`impls/t6_combos_extra.rs`) is true when Ad Nauseam is in hand, life is 26 or more, and the
Oracle line (Oracle, and Consultation or Pact) or the Breach line (Breach, LED, Brain Freeze) is "in hand or library" and
payable with Ad Nauseam's five mana on top. Those cards are in the deck, so it is true every turn the deck holds Ad
Nauseam and about eight mana. The attempt then draws as many cards as the life above the threat floor allows (about 8-10)
and wins only if it hits the line: with two cards to find among 55 it mostly does not. It costs Ad Nauseam, 10-16 life,
and the turn. The Oracle line attempts that stop are mostly the locks and counter wars above.

## 4. What was fixed (behind `AI_T6STOPS`, default on; `AI_T6STOPS=0` = origin/main)

1. **A line whose cards cannot be cast is not ready** (`combos::playable`, used wherever `affordable` gated a go:
   `combo_options`, `attempt`, `attempt_run`, the Tier 6 speed measure, `ready_lines`, `audit_ready`; the mana-for-lines
   tutor wish keeps plain `affordable`). For a Tier 6 player the cast pieces must pass `castable` (locks, Void Winnower,
   Chalice/Bauble counters as cast), and each later piece must pass it on a copy of the game in which the earlier ones
   are already this turn's casts (Rule of Law, Deafening Silence, Ethersworn Canonist, Eidolon, Archon). The copy is made
   only when something on the table has a cast hook (`g.hooks` not empty) and the line has two or more casts. Tests:
   `a_void_winnower_makes_an_even_oracle_unplayable...`, `rule_of_law_stops_the_second_spell...`,
   `without_a_lock_the_line_is_ready_either_way`.
2. **Ad Nauseam into a win is ready only when it is likely to find the pieces** (`ad_odds`, `find_all`): the cards it can
   draw (the life above `adnaus_floor` and 7, over the library's average mana value, at most the 15 the loop allows),
   the copies in the library of each missing group, hypergeometric by inclusion-exclusion over the groups; the better of
   the two lines must reach 0.4 (`AD_MIN_ODDS`, a knob). A piece that is not in the library (gone) makes its line
   impossible. Tests: `ad_nauseam_with_the_line_far_from_hand_is_not_ready`, `..._one_piece_in_hand_and_the_other_likely_is_ready`,
   `..._a_piece_gone_from_the_deck_is_not_ready`, `the_odds_of_finding_the_pieces`. Three existing tests of the dig itself
   (`engine_t6_combo3.rs`: draws into the Oracle line, digs past a full hand, finds nothing) construct pieces deep in the
   library, so `ad_table_with` now turns the gate off for them.
3. **Labels** (a labelling change that, to keep `AI_T6STOPS=0` byte-identical in the report, is also behind the switch):
   a stop with no counter or removal now names where it failed, `no card: step 2 (<step label>)` or
   `no card: cast of <card>`, and `, after <card> at an earlier step` when an earlier step of the same attempt met an answer
   (the k22 case). Test: `a_stop_with_no_card_says_which_step_failed`. The report's "unnamed answer" text is unchanged
   for the old `?`.

What it cannot do: the lock cases stop being tries, which spares the mana, the Dark Ritual and the turn the AI wasted (in
k12 the deck then cast Dark Ritual and Tymna with the same mana, so the line's mana was not lost); it does not make the
Oracle castable under a Void Winnower, only stops the repeat. Whether the freed turns win more games, and whether the
Ad Nauseam gate costs wins (an attempt at 40% that would have won), is for the sim.

## 5. What was left

- **Counter wars that spend the next step's mana (k22).** The player's own counterspell, cast during its attempt, may take
  the colour the next piece needs. Reserving the rest of the line's mana before it counters would have saved nothing in
  k22 (the line was dead either way, the Oracle would have been countered or stranded); a real fix is in the payment: pay
  the counter and the Oracle from the lands that leave a black source, which is a payment-planner change. Not done.
- **A Void Winnower or Rule of Law on the table is real interaction.** The ready test now sees it, but nothing plans around
  it (hold the line until the lock is removed, cast the exile spell first under Rule of Law: it cannot be, the Oracle must
  come first).
- **Ad Nauseam as a card-advantage engine** is untouched (the plan's `adnaus_cast_now`); only the "win attempt" is gated.
- Rograkh/Thrasios: one attempt in the traces and no stop. Its 49 unnamed stops of 88 (diagnosis) are not explained by
  these traces; a trace of its stopped games is still open. The board-combo stops (creature lines, Gaea's Cradle) go
  through `run_steps` and `attempt_run` like these, and the labels will now say the step.
- The yield of the trace was low (6 stops in 59 games). The queue's `AI_T6STOPS=1` runs print the new labels in the Tier 6
  report ("stopped by: ... by no card: step 2 (...)"), which gives the real shares.
