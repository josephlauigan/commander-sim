# Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER (sephfs) — AI audit, phase 1

Strategy (decklists/JD/sephiroth-fs-wipe-steal.md): mono-black control. Every creature death drains 1 (Sephiroth, then the emblem after the 4th drain in a turn flips him), so the deck makes creatures die on purpose (outlets, edicts, wipes), takes the dead (Grave Betrayal, Necromantic Selection, Animate Dead, Zombify ...) and wins by drain, Exsanguinate, or the 5/5 angel; one named loop (Phyrexian Altar + Reassembling Skeleton + Pawn of Ulamog).
Priority function: `sephfs_prio` (rust/crates/sim-core/src/impls/sephfs.rs:70), which wraps `pool::generic_prio` and overrides edicts, Massacre Girl, Pestilence, Grave Betrayal, Kaya's Ghostform, outlets, drain creatures, Rewrite Regrets/Zombify/Call of the Ring/Braids. Wipes: `sephfs::wipe_utility` (brain.rs:751). Steals: `sephfs::rean_targets` -> `sephfs_cards::steal_targets`. Options: `sephfs::options` (Presumed Dead, Exsanguinate, Rabid Attack). Plan config: NONE (`plans::config("sephfs")` is empty; no wish list, no key_cards).

Run: `--deck sephfs --pool t5 --analyze --ai adaptive --games 300` (both profiles): 29.0% / 28.0% win. Flips in 25-28% of games (first flip round ~7.5), 3.3-3.5 emblem drains, ~3 creatures sacrificed and ~0.2 steals per game, 0.4 wipes per game. 96-99% of wins are damage/drain; none by "combo". Loses 71%: Kinnan combo 23%, Urza combo 14%, Winota 17%, Zur, Yawgmoth. Sephiroth is removed 0.44x per game. The "game plan timing" section is blank ("Plan: ", online 0% every turn) because there is no plan config.
Evidence limits: the card report listed only Sephiroth (the other cards are below the 100-casts threshold, "often stuck" list empty). To get per-card numbers I traced 60 games (`--trace 0..59`, scratchpad files `tr/fs*.txt`) and counted "Sephiroth FS casts X" and every tutor result. Cast counts below are over those 60 games (a card is drawn in roughly 10-14 of them). (Environment note: `commander-sim`/`cardgen` refuse to start in the shared worktree while `decklists/JD/cloud-midgar-equipment.md` has no `## Import list`; I ran from a private copy with that file taken from HEAD. `cardgen audit --mine`: "sephfs: 69 unique cards, 0 not Full"; `--cards` lists "not modeled" Scryfall text on Braids's Frightful Return, Massacre Girl, Moseo, Presumed Dead, Rewrite Regrets, Dread Drone, but each has Rust code in sephfs_cards.rs / partials.rs / sheoldred.rs, so I judged them from the code.)

## Problems (ranked by likely impact on win rate)
1. **Tutors (Demonic Tutor, Vampiric Tutor, Rune-Scarred Demon) have no deck wish list** — `tutor_pick_named` (ai/decks.rs:700-810) has arms for seph, yshtola, cloud, kraum, veyran, kefka, sauron, not sephfs, and `plans::config("sephfs")` has no `wish` (main decks' `wish_list` is empty, plans.rs:119), so every tutor falls to the generic pick (decks.rs:820: turn <= 4 highest `sephfs_prio`, later `tutor_value` = card_worth or 10 x bomb). In 60 traced games the deck made 25 tutor finds: Sol Ring 6, Wander Off 4, Bolas's Citadel 5, End of the Hunt 3, Fleshbag Marauder 2, one each of Yahenni, Woe Strider, Phyrexian Altar, Grave Titan, Crux of Fate, Massacre Girl, Sephiroth's Intervention, Fellwar Stone. Zero Exsanguinate, Gray Merchant, Pestilence, Kaya's Ghostform or Rune-Scarred Demon, which the doc names as the tutor targets ("Pestilence or Massacre Girl for the wipe turn, Exsanguinate or Gray Merchant to finish, Phyrexian Altar or Kaya's Ghostform to set up the flip"). Late finds were poor: Woe Strider on turn 13 with 8 lands and 14 life, Wander Off/End of the Hunt on turns 9-12 with 7-8 lands, Fleshbag Marauder turn 8 with 7 lands. Fix: add a `"sephfs"` arm: no Sephiroth out -> Phyrexian Altar/Viscera Seer; Sephiroth out unflipped -> the card that reaches 4 deaths (edict, outlet) or Kaya's Ghostform; flipped + opponents have creatures -> Massacre Girl/Pestilence/Crux; lethal or 7+ lands -> Exsanguinate/Gray Merchant; an opponent's combo about to go off -> spot removal.
2. **Gray Merchant of Asphodel and Exsanguinate are cast rarely / at a flat creature priority** — Gray Merchant has no `sephfs_prio` row and no drain tag, so `generic_prio` treats it as a plain creature (`42 + 2*pow` = 46, pool.rs:~245); its ETB (devotion drain, zones.rs:1290, worth 6.0 in the ETB table) is not part of the cast decision, so it loses to Dread Drone/Call of the Ring/Pestilence in the "decides" lines (16%, 40% shares; cast 3 times in 60 games, tutored 0). Exsanguinate (4 casts, only via `exsanguinate_option`, needs lethal or X >= 4 and a quiet board) and Butcher of Malakir (4), Pitiless Plunderer (4), Living Death (4), Blood on the Snow (4) are also cast in under a third of the games where a draw was expected; I cannot separate "not drawn" from "held" without per-card draw data, so this is unproven for all but Gray Merchant. Fix: a `sephfs_prio` row scaling Gray Merchant with devotion x opponents (and emblem), and a drain-lethal check.
3. **The named Altar + Reassembling Skeleton + Pawn of Ulamog loop is never recognised as a win** — the doc (Key lines and Rule 0) calls it the deck's loop. The code has only per-card options: `skeleton` (impls/t3.rs:386) returns the Skeleton for `0.5 + death_value/2` when an outlet exists and `Pawn of Ulamog` has a `dies` hook (common.rs:3400); `impls/combos.rs` has no entry for the pieces (grep: only Altar appears), and "How it wins" shows 0% combo wins in 600 games. With Sephiroth/emblem or Blood Artist out the loop is an infinite drain. Fix: add a loop detector like `mine::loop_*` for seph: with Altar + Skeleton + Pawn + a drain source, run the loop to lethal.
4. **No plan config / milestone for the deck** — `--analyze` prints "Plan: " and "Online by turn" 0% every turn, so none of the timing numbers (flip turn, wipe turn) are tracked in the standard plan report; the `Sephiroth` block (flips, drains) partly replaces them. A plan like "Sephiroth flipped" or "first wipe with emblem" would let swap tests show when the deck comes online. Impact on win rate: none directly; on every future test: yes.
5. **Bolas's Citadel is the default tutor target and hardcast at up to 80** — `citadel_prio` (plans.rs:382) is only `life_margin >= 8 -> min(80, 40+2*margin)`, with no check that creatures/outlets/drain are on board or that Sephiroth is out. It was tutored 5 times in 25 finds (turns 5-14) and cast 6 times in 60 games; the doc says it is good because "the drains refill the life". Whether it helps here is unknown (no win-rate-when-cast number available: Citadel cast in under 100 games); the trace shows `Citadel: cast Demonic Tutor from the top for 2 life`, i.e. it works mechanically. Note as a candidate for a paired test, not a proven bug.
6. **Vampiric Tutor timing on turns 2-3** — 6 of the 10 traced Vampiric Tutors were cast on turns 1-3 with 0-2 lands, putting Sol Ring / Fellwar Stone / Wander Off / Massacre Girl on top (fs24, fs29, fs34, fs45, fs46, fs54): the next draw is the tutored card instead of a land, with 6-7 cards in hand and 1 land (fs19, fs24, fs45, fs46, fs54 show 1 land on turn 2). The pick function never considers the mana situation (`tutor_pick` has no land-count term). Impact unknown. Fix: with fewer than 3 lands and no land in hand, Vampiric/Demonic Tutor for a rock only when a rock gives the mana (Sol Ring yes, Wander Off/Massacre Girl no).
7. **Massacre Girl and edicts depend on one board-state formula** — `massacre_prio` is 88 only when `opp_creatures >= 3 && theirs > 1.2*ours + 3`, otherwise `min(base,20)`; edicts use `times + deaths >= 4` with an unflipped Sephiroth and `66/30` with the emblem. These are deck-specific and plausible; no counter-evidence found, listed because Massacre Girl was cast only 6 times and the deck loses to combo (Kinnan/Urza 37% of games) where boards are small. Unknown.
