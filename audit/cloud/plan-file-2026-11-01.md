# Cloud: a plan file to replace the hand AI (2026-11-01)

Brief: `rust/agents/plan-files-3.md`. Gap to close (`audit/recal/pairs-2026-10-31.md` section 4): hand AI on beats the
generic policy plus `data/plans/cloud.toml` by +6.6 over t1-t3 [+5.3, +7.8]. No sims in this task: the coordinator
runs the on/off paired test next.

## What the game shape says

| tier | off (plan) | on (hand) | plan online off / on (first attack by an equipped Cloud) |
|---|---|---|---|
| t1 | 14.9% | 23.2% | 42.4% / 77.3% |
| t2 | 6.9% | 13.7% | 43.1% / 79.8% |
| t3 | 11.6% | 16.2% | 40.9% / 73.0% |

The whole gap sits in one number: with the generic policy Cloud attacks wearing Equipment in about 42% of games, with
the hand AI in about 77%. Wins are 98-100% by damage either way and the loss turn hardly moves (8.8 vs 8.1 at t1), so
the plan isn't dying faster, it isn't getting the Equipment onto Cloud.

Why 1-5 most plausibly explain the gap: each one keeps Equipment off Cloud or kills him bare, which is exactly the
42% vs 77% plan-online split; 4 alone takes two of the deck's best pieces (the Helm's hexproof, the Feast and Famine
untap) out of the generic deck entirely. 9 and 10 matter in the look-ahead's choice between equipping and casting more;
they are the first thing to add if the paired test still shows a gap (a plan knob read in `search.rs`).

## How it was checked (no sims)

`tests/plan_files_3.rs`, hand AI off (`force_hand(Some(false))`) and on, same hand-built position: the Sword goes on
Cloud over a 3/2 flier (both); a Sword worn by another creature moves onto Cloud (both); the Helm and Feast and Famine
are equipped to Cloud by the generic equip with `cloud_special`'s utility (the hand AI through `cloud_special`); a bare
Cloud stays home against a blocker, goes wearing Hearth and Home past a 0/2, and stays home again once a 3-power
blocker would kill him (both). Default play: the new sections are read only from an active plan, and
`plan_data::active` is None for cloud while its hand AI is on (the default); the test checks no other plan file uses
them.
