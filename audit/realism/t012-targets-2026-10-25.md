## Lists

- **Tier 0:** the official precons stay (real lists already, and fully modelled).
- **Tier 1:** EDHREC's bracket-2 ("core") average decks; all five have no Game Changers, as the tier requires.
- **Tier 2:** EDHREC's bracket-3 ("upgraded") average decks, held to 1-2 Game Changers and no two-card infinite:
  Kaalia's Smothering Tithe became Lyra Dawnbringer, Meren gained Demonic Tutor for Liliana, Dreadhorde General, and
  Brago's Deadeye Navigator (with Peregrine Drake, a two-card infinite) became Skyclave Apparition (EDHREC's
  most-played fits).

## Modelling

Seven agents modelled the lists' 121 Partial or Unmodeled cards (`rust/agents/t12-*.md`); each branch was reviewed
and merged. Found on the way: Champions of the Perfect could never be cast (behold); Sheltered by Ghosts never
returned its card; Shardmage's Rescue was never cast by pool decks; Mossborn Hydra's doubling overflowed an i32 (13
panics in 4,000 games, now capped); compiled tokens now carry their printed colours (about 70 cards); "return all ...
cards" reanimates every match; several compiled abilities that would have run twice next to card code are off. A
practice test that passed by chance (it assumed one answer before the mulligan) now undoes every answer.
