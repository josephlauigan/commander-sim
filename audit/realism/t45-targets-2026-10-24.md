## After replacing the lists and modelling every card (2026-10-25)

All ten lists are EDHREC's bracket-4 ("optimized") average decks (Tier 4 keeps its no-Lotus-Petal rule, JD
2026-10-25: Prosper's Petal became Weftstalker Ardent, Yuriko's Dismember). Their 133 Partial or Unmodeled cards were
modelled by seven agents (`rust/agents/t45-*.md`), each branch reviewed and merged. Engine bugs found on the way:
Impact Tremors and Purphoros never saw tokens made by card code; compiled "whenever you gain life" triggers never

**Heliod (40 traced games: 21 wins, 18 by combo, many in rounds 4-7).** The commander is always available, so Heliod
with Walking Ballista (or Triskelion) is a one-card combo: four mana wins. Opponents get their instant-speed removal
window (`combos::interrupted`) and mostly had nothing. EDHREC's bracket-4 Heliod list is built around it; Playgroup's
Heliod (21% combo wins, average win turn 9) is every Heliod deck logged, mostly casual life gain. The target and the
list describe different decks.

## Opponents respect combo commanders (JD: "Opponents respect combos", 2026-10-25)

200 traced Tier 4 games, at each of Heliod's combo windows (`combos::interrupted`), what the opponents held:

| | before | after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heliod combo attempts / combo wins | 90 / 81 | 82 / 77 |
| an opponent held no instant removal and no counter | 161 of 226 | - |
| held instant removal, tapped out | 39 | 19 |
| held instant removal, locked out (Grand Abolisher) | 2 | 2 |

The change (`ai/brain.rs`, `Situation::cmdr_combo`): an opposing commander on the battlefield that is a catalogued
combo piece makes instant removal worth holding mana for, with the near-combo weight, and value plays don't tap out
past it. It halved the tapped-out answers, but most windows find no answer in hand at all, so Heliod moved only from
62.3% to 61.4% (8,200 games). Two crashes fixed on the way: Magda's five-Treasure cost (sacrificed one at a time, a
trigger between could spend one: 4 panics in 8,200 Tier 4 games) and the Pathway loop above.

Scorecards now: Tier 4 2 of 9 (median round 8 and poison pass; combo 54.5% against 19.9%), Tier 5 3 of 9 (seats 2 and
3 and median round pass; combo 59.7% against 37.7%).
