# Tier 3: combo and alternate-win lines (2026-10-26)

Branch `t3-misses`. The audit checked each Tier 3 list's non-combat wins against Oracle text, the combo catalogue and
200 traced games. No deck list changed. Playgroup data is CC BY 4.0, from playgroup.gg.

## What changed

- **Aurelia:**
  - Sword of Feast and Famine and Aggravated Assault were dead cards. Their code ran only for Sauron's deck. Now the
    Sword is cast and equipped, and Assault gives extra combats while the Sword connects (capped at 12).
  - Aurelia puts Helm of the Host on herself (or on Combat Celebrant) before combat. Helm + Aurelia isn't
    catalogued as a combo: the engine plays it out as real combats, and players log those as combat wins.
    Cataloguing it made Aurelia 61% combo against Playgroup's 4%.
- **Korvold:** new line, Chatterfang + Pitiless Plunderer + an outlet + a payoff. The Gravecrawler line in the deck
  notes isn't in the list.
- **Marwyn:** new line, Staff of Domination + an Elf that taps for five or more, finished by Ezuri, Finale or
  Craterhoof.
- **Tergrid:** Torment of Hailfire was never cast; its option was Sauron-only. Now it's cast.
- **Atraxa:** no combo lines. Norn's Decree is still Partial and never cast.
- **Poison:** no rules bug. Atraxa's poison is a pure infect build (Ichor Rats, Ixhel, Inexorable Tide); Tergrid's
  comes from stolen infect creatures and Vraska, which is legal. The 18% against a 9.3% target is the list against
  Playgroup's all-build average.

## Left open

  two a pass: the Skeleton and the Treasure that returns it. The scorecard is unchanged (combo or alternate 16.4%,
  passes).
- Sword of the Animist: fixed later the same night. The Aurelia deck now casts and equips it, like the Feast and
  Famine Sword. Rescored: combo or alternate 16.7% (passes). Seat 1 went from 31.5% to 30.7% and seat 3 from 22.8%
