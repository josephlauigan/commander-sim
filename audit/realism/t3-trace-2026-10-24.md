# Tier 3 traced for bugs, and a knob fit (2026-10-24)

Branch `trace-fixes`. Adaptive AI, loose profile.

## Tracing (300 games)

**Refused plays: 26 in 300 games** (Tier 6 had 247 before its fixes):
- **Mind Stone (11): fixed.** Its draw was offered on its own mana, the same trap as Deathrite Shaman.
- **Village Rites (33) and Unexpected Windfall (6)** fail their extra cost at cast time. They reach the cast through a
  path other than the main options.
- **Diabolic Intent (5):** the chosen sacrifice was gone by the cast.
- **Korvold and Academy Manufactor:** 3 payment failures.

All of these are small. Nothing broken explains Tier 3's misses.

**Engines checked:**
- Korvold's sacrifice engine fires (`die` with "sac" fires the sacrifice event).
- Land counts by turn are normal (about one land a turn).

**Combo attempts: 3 in 300 games** (2 Korvold, 1 Aurelia). Games end by combat elimination around round 13. The
attacks are small, attackers are often held back, and big wipes are common (Atraxa's Hour of Revelation and Toxic
Deluge).

## Conclusion

As in Tier 6, the Tier 3 gap is structural:
- The decks' play is slow, and their lines rarely assemble.
- Combo wins need combos the lists actually run and the catalogue models. Real casual logs show 20% combo or
  alternate wins.
