# Tier 3 traced for bugs, and a knob fit (2026-10-24)

Branch `trace-fixes`. Adaptive AI, loose profile.

## Tracing (300 games)

**Refused plays: 26 in 300 games** (Tier 6 had 247 before its fixes):
- **Mind Stone (11): fixed.** Its draw was offered on its own mana, the same trap as Deathrite Shaman.
- **Village Rites (33) and Unexpected Windfall (6)** fail their extra cost at cast time. They reach the cast through a
  path other than the main options.
- **Diabolic Intent (5):** the chosen sacrifice was gone by the cast.
- **Korvold and Academy Manufactor:** 3 payment failures.

All of these are small. Nothing broken explains Tier 3's misses.

**Engines checked:**
- Korvold's sacrifice engine fires (`die` with "sac" fires the sacrifice event).
- Land counts by turn are normal (about one land a turn).

**Combo attempts: 3 in 300 games** (2 Korvold, 1 Aurelia). Games end by combat elimination around round 13. The
attacks are small, attackers are often held back, and big wipes are common (Atraxa's Hour of Revelation and Toxic
Deluge).

## Conclusion

As in Tier 6, the Tier 3 gap is structural:
- The decks' play is slow, and their lines rarely assemble.
- Combo wins need combos the lists actually run and the catalogue models. Real casual logs show 20% combo or
  alternate wins.

Next: the Tier 3 lists against EDHREC (plan step 6), including Atraxa, then the combo catalogue for the casual decks.

## After modelling every Tier 3 card (later the same day)

All five decks' Partial and Unmodeled cards were modelled (Atraxa in the main session; Aurelia, Marwyn, and Korvold
with Tergrid by three agents, merged after review). The targets were rebuilt with Atraxa in (`data/realism/t3.toml`:
the five commanders weighted equally). Scorecard, 8,200 games, adaptive, loose:

| statistic | sim | target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| seats 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 | 30.5 / 26.0 / 23.1 / 20.4 | 32.3 / 27.7 / 22.0 / 18.0 |
| median win round | 10 | 8 |
| combo or alternate wins | 8.6% | 16.5% |
| damage wins | 62.0% | 59.8% |
| commander damage wins | 10.3% | 14.4% |
| poison wins | 19.1% | 9.3% |

Decks: Tergrid 41.7%, Atraxa 25.8%, Aurelia 22.8%, Korvold 17.5%, Marwyn 17.4% of the decisive games each sat in.

**Bugs found and fixed:**
- Poison eliminations were recorded as the last life-loss kind, so poison wins read 0%.
- Contaminant Grafter: the compiler dropped its corrupted condition and draw, so it put a land from hand at every end
  step, on top of the hand-written trigger; and its proliferate hook made `toxic_hit` skip its toxic 1.
- Llanowar Elves was a 0/1 tagged Human, Blood Artist tagged Human (data/carddb.txt from the Python port).
- Korvold's sacrifices (`t3::sac_worst_permanent`) took the cheapest nonland permanent before any land, so it gave up
  Ashnod's Altar, Phyrexian Altar and Goblin Bombardment with seven lands out, and handed them to an opposing Tergrid.
  Now: tokens, then a land with five lands or more, then the cheapest card.

**Tergrid (60 traced games, 22 wins).** No rules bug found. It wins by stealing, about 5 permanents a game, mostly
through its own discard and edicts (Liliana, Waker of the Dead and Liliana of the Veil, Smallpox, Mire in Misery,
Oppression, Soul Shatter, Plaguecrafter, Mindslicer). The stolen permanents include opponents' engines: Mazirek
pumped by Korvold's Treasure sacrifices, Gisela doubling the damage, Tainted Observer adding poison. Overkill
attacks (132 and 174 damage) came from those. Checked and correct: Tergrid's steal triggers, Mazirek (any player's
sacrifice of another permanent, tokens included), Liliana's +1 (3 life once), the AI's discard (instants and sorceries
first with an opposing Tergrid), Tergrid's threat value (7; removed 26 times in the 50 games it was cast). Playgroup's
Tergrid wins 26% of its games (all builds, casual pods); this list (EDHREC's bracket 3) stacks symmetric discard and
edicts with it, which is its real strength, and the AI opponents don't race it or hold back their own sacrifices.

## Opponents respect Tergrid (JD: "Try 2")

Tergrid is cast in 50 of the 51 traced games it played, and its wins come with up to 24 stolen permanents. Opponents
fed it little by choice (4 Altar sacrifices in 60 games); the rest came from its own edicts, discard and Smallpox.
Three changes, 8,200 games each:

| change | Tergrid | deck spread | other statistics |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| before | 41.7% | 24.3 | - |
| sacrifice outlets take only tokens, +3 to attack Tergrid's controller | 40.3% | 22.8 | seat 3 passes |
| Tergrid's threat value 7 -> 10 (removal goes to it first) | 36.9% | 19.0 | unchanged |

The threat value is its own commit (94babb12), easy to drop: 10 is the highest in the table (Kinnan went 8 -> 5 when
every removal spell went to him). Scorecard now: seats 30.2 / 26.5 / 22.5 / 20.8, median round 10, combo 8.9%,
damage 61.4%, commander damage 10.5%, poison 19.2%; decks Tergrid 36.9, Atraxa 26.4, Aurelia 24.5, Korvold 19.3,
Marwyn 17.9.
