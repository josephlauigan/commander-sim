## What it means

- **Seat edge.** The style knobs (temperature, aggression, caution per deck; the counter threshold, the instant
  bonus and the Mox Diamond land rule for the table) don't move it on fresh seeds. That's the same finding as the
  2026-10-24 morning fits. Seat 1 sits at 27-28% against 32.5%; the cause is elsewhere.
- **Spread.** Every fit cuts it by about 2 points (17.2 to 15.2-15.4), mostly by raising Rograkh/Thrasios and Kraum/Tymna.
- **Win-type mix.** Fitting it pulls combo wins down (82 to 76) at the cost of a slower table (median 9).

## Decision

Keep the built-in knobs: no fitted file is adopted. The fitted files stay in `fit/` for reference.
