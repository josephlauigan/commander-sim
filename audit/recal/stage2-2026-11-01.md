**The test.** With the refitted model, `cargo test --release --no-fail-fast` gave 3808 passed and 1 failed:
`sim-core/tests/ismcts_b.rs::protection_is_held_for_a_low_value_target` (line 134). The toy duel is Edgar Markov's
10/10 and a 1/1 against Krenko at 8 life with no creatures, with removal aimed at the 1/1. The refitted model spends
Heroic Intervention on the 1/1 instead of holding it: the protection search's playouts are scored by the learned
evaluation, and the refitted model scores the line that keeps the 1/1 higher. Its sibling test
(`protection_saves_the_game_winning_permanent`) and the rest of the suite passed.

**Decision (coordinator, 10/31 20:00): not adopted: no measurable gain, fails ismcts_b
protection_is_held_for_a_low_value_target.** The held-out gain is within noise (log loss -0.0011; the halves' mean
-0.01), and the full suite must pass. The model was committed (9f0b47f1) and reverted; `data/eval/model.toml` stays
the current model (data hash `5c51874b0ca6d03d`). The refitted file is kept, uncommitted, in the scratch folder
(`recal2/model-new.toml`, with `recal2/train.txt` and `recal2/score.txt`) in case it is revisited. Every later step
uses the current model (binary rebuilt from the reverted tree).
