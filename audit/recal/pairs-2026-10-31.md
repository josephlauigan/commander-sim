## How the pairs are read

2000 games an arm, seeds 500000-501999, every arm of a comparison on the same binary and `--jobs 22`. Base = the
stack's default (no env). A half's summed share of the decisive games its decks sat in (`half.sh`), or a `--deck`
run's win rate (`dp.sh`); Wald 95% CIs, the arms' variances summed (conservative). Level = the CI includes 0.
CPU = user seconds for the 2000 games (`/usr/bin/time`); steps = playout steps a game, every seat.
