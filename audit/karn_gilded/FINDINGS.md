# Karn, Gilded Guardian (key `karn_gilded`): findings

The deck's living list of strengths and weaknesses and what was tested.

**List:** `decklists/JD/karn-gilded-guardian.md`, registered 2026-11-24 (Karn, Gilded Guardian, WUBRG; 64 spells and 35 lands, no basics;
15 Game Changers, Bracket 4). **Tiers:** t4 and t5 (`data/my_decks.toml`). First runs 2026-11-24 on the Felidar list (see Tested); the current list is not simulated. The deck replaces the Y'shtola and Yoshimaru/Tymna
decks, whose findings are in `audit/retired/yshtola/` and `audit/retired/yoshimaru/`.

## Plan (what the deck is meant to do)
- **Engine:** Karn draws a card for each colour among the other artifacts we control, up to five; coloured artifacts (14 in the list; the
  Transguild Courier and Infinite Guideline Station are five colours alone) and blink effects (Ephemerate, Conjurer's Closet, Restoration Angel,
  Flickerwisp, Teleportation Circle, the harnessed The Mind Stone) enter him again; Gandalf the White and Elesh Norn,
  Mother of Machines add triggers.
- **Wins:** Thassa's Oracle with Demonic Consultation or Doomsday; Exquisite Blood with Sanguine Bond or Vito (Haliya and Gold-Forged Thopteryx's lifelink
  as the starters; nothing doubles life gained since 2026-11-25); Exsanguinate off Cabal Coffers and Urborg; combat with Karn and Buster Sword or Sword of Feast and Famine.

## Strengths (by design, not measured)
- Many ways to draw and to find a line: seven tutors and the Karn draw.
- Counter and stop suite for the win turn: Force of Will, Counterspell, Silence, Teferi's Protection, Teferi, Time Raveler, Ranger-Captain of Eos.
- Four independent finishes.

## Weaknesses and limits (by design and from the engine work; none measured)
- **Karn is slow and fragile:** a real cost of 5 to 10 and {2} more per recast; an opponent's Flickerwisp or any removal sends him to the command zone.
- **Hand size at our own end step:** Teleportation Circle and the harnessed The Mind Stone return Karn in our end step, he draws before cleanup and
  the excess is discarded unless Thought Vessel or Reliquary Tower is out (they give no maximum hand size).
- **The draw is mandatory:** the library can empty; Thassa's Oracle is the outlet, `death_kind decked` is the stat to watch.
- **Blood Moon effects:** no basic lands.
- **Mana:** nine shocks, five fetches, three pain lands, City of Brass and Mana Confluence cost life next to Necropotence and Doomsday; red and green sources are thin (17 and 16).
- **Engine limits (JD-accepted or open):** The Mind Stone is Approximate (the stone never picks Infinite Guideline Station or The One
  Ring); Teferi's Protection is Approximate (JD waived the Full requirement 2026-11-24); an opponent's Flickerwisp on
  Karn goes to the command zone without asking; Cursed Mirror never copies a commander; the AI does not cast Karn at instant speed under Gandalf;
  Breya's 3-damage mode takes a player only; the AI numbers for Station, Breya, Herd Heirloom, Twitching Doll and Karn's own priority are
  provisional and untuned; the AI's use of Birthing Pod is unmeasured, the engine pays its activation with {G} and never with 2 life (the card allows both), cannot activate the Pod the turn it enters (the engine gives a noncreature artifact a creature's summoning sickness: `x.sick` is checked without `g.is_creature` in `pod()` in `impls/t2.rs`), never sacrifices a token (Breya's Thopters and the Station's Robots; the card allows it and a token fetches a 1-drop), and a person using it in practice is offered one computed sacrifice-and-fetch pair (a sacrifice and a creature one mana value higher, only when the AI's worth gain is at least 0.5), not a free choice of fodder or target (JD accepted all four limits on 2026-11-24; the two probe allow-list rows for the Pod, `FALSE_ALARMS` in `practice_ability_probe.rs` and `practice_trigger_probe.rs`, are his approved entries). More engine limits: in a rare spot the Oracle exception checks that the Oracle is castable before the blink itself is
  paid, so the AI can draw its library to zero and lose; a main-phase blink (Flickerwisp worst) costs Karn's attack and the AI does not weigh it;
  Flickerwisp keeps only the primary commander in exile; the Restoration Angel protector double-blinks Karn whenever it saves any creature, which
  flatters Karn's draw numbers in a baseline; The Reaper misses the case where a wipe kills it together with the creature, and a person is never
  offered its "may" (the card is always taken); for a person, Station, Breya, Herd Heirloom and Twitching Doll go through the card's own option hook;
  and the `st(...)` settings rows and `HAND_DELETED` in `ai/mod.rs`, `CTHRESH` in `impls/rules.rs` and the key list at `settings.rs:197` are keyed on the retired decks, so Karn gets the generic values.
- **Doomsday:** its pile builder and its play are card code that runs for every deck (only the `tutor_stat` trace stat is keyed on `thrasios`); whether it ever finds a winning pile with this list is unmeasured.
- **Deck-key gates that do not run for this key:** Solitude's pitch spares line pieces (`rules2.rs`), equipment holder scoring for Buster Sword and
  the Sword (`bulk_white.rs`, `cloud.rs`, `tymna_cards.rs`) and commander importance 7.0 (`cast.rs`) are keyed on the retired decks; Karn gets the generic paths.

## List change 2026-11-24 (JD's choice, not simulated): Felidar Guardian => Esper Sentinel
JD's decision: Esper Sentinel (his owned copy, moved from the Arzakon deck; `data/ownership.toml`) takes Felidar Guardian's slot (a proxy). Not slot-for-slot by the packages rule: a {3}{W} blink creature out, a {W} tax body in (mana value 4 for 1); it is a package change JD chose, with the loss below.
- **Job lost:** a four-mana 1/4 body with a "may" blink on entering, and its two infinite lines: Restoration Angel + Felidar Guardian (infinite blink; with Elesh Norn out and Karn as a target, an infinite draw) and Felidar Guardian + Cursed Mirror (the Mirror copies the Guardian). Both lines are gone from the guide's disclosure list. Blink effects are now six (Ephemerate, Cloudshift, Restoration Angel, Flickerwisp, Teleportation Circle, the harnessed The Mind Stone; Conjurer's Closet has since taken Cloudshift's place, see the entry below). The two lines were never modelled as a combo; they left the Spellbook data with the card and their two `KNOWN_GAPS` rows in `rust/crates/sim-core/tests/spellbook.rs` are removed (the new `karn_gilded` file has 4 included combos).
- **Job gained:** a one-mana white artifact creature (1/1): it is the 14th coloured artifact (white, so it counts for Karn's draw; white was already supplied by Shorikai, Breya, Thopteryx, The Mind Stone and The Wind Crystal, so it adds no new colour; Shorikai has since left the list), a tax body (draw a card unless the opponent pays {X}, X its power: 1, more with an Equipment on it) and a third Ranger-Captain of Eos target (creatures with mana value 1 or less: Yoshimaru, Giver of Runes, Esper Sentinel).
- **Counts after (before):** creatures 21 (21) without Karn; artifact creatures 7 (6), other creatures 14 (15), artifacts in all 22 (21); curve 1: 15 (14), 4: 9 (10); average mana value 2.81 (2.86), 180 over 64 (183); pips unchanged (W38: Felidar and Sentinel each have one {W}); blink effects 6 (7); Game Changers 15 (15); coloured artifacts 14 (13); land sources unchanged. Esper Sentinel is Full (`data/card_overrides.toml`; the note reads: opponents pay X for their first noncreature spell only when they can spare it, otherwise you draw).
- **What the numbers say:** with the Felidar list (commit 551d1d00, below), Felidar Guardian was cast in 12.0% of games at t5 and won 16.7% of the time when cast, 3.4 points under the deck's own 20.1% (opponents removed it 0.03 times a game); correlation only, and it is Karn's own number, so it needs no transfer from the retired Yoshimaru/Tymna list (t5: cast 9.7%, win when cast 45.5%, `audit/stage3-baseline-2026-11-23/yoshimaru-analyze.txt`).
- **To check in the first run:** Esper Sentinel's cast turn and cast rate, how often the table pays the {X} (the sim's opponents pay when they can spare it, per the card's note), what Ranger-Captain fetches now, whether Karn's colour count on entry moves (it should not: white is covered), and whether the missing blink shows in the Karn re-entry count per game.

## List change 2026-11-24 (JD's choice, not simulated): Conjurer's Closet in for Cloudshift
JD's decision: Conjurer's Closet ({5} artifact, JD's own copy, from the spare list; `data/ownership.toml`) takes the slot of Cloudshift ({W} instant, a proxy, out). Not simulated (JD's choice). A package change, not slot-for-slot: mana value 1 to 5, an instant becomes an artifact, one blink becomes a blink at every end step.
- **Job lost:** a one-mana white instant that blinked a creature we control at instant speed. It was one of Karn's few cheap protections: the AI holds it as a protector for Karn only (`ai/pool.rs`, the Karn-gated Cloudshift row, which stays: it is engine code for any deck), blinking him out of removal aimed at him, and he draws again. Karn is the card the table removes most (0.31 times a game at t4 in the 2026-11-24 baseline, Tested below). The baseline tables carry no Cloudshift line, so its cast rate and win rate are not recorded. The deck's other blink protectors, Ephemerate and Restoration Angel, stay. Cloudshift was also the cheapest way to enter Karn again at instant speed.
- **Job gained:** a colourless artifact that at the beginning of our end step may exile a creature we control and return it, so once it is out Karn (or Ranger-Captain of Eos, Skyclave Apparition, Solitude, Orcish Bowmasters, Restoration Angel) enters again every turn; each blink of Karn draws his full colour count again (Gandalf and Elesh Norn double it). The engine path is `t2.rs end_step_flicker`, shared with Teleportation Circle, with the Karn valuation the E4 work added (the AI blinks the creature with the best enters effect; the Circle was cast in 33.1% of the games it was drawn at t4 and 35.1% at t5). It is colourless, so it adds no colour to Karn's draw. It costs five, the same turn as Karn, and it protects nothing: the blink happens only at our end step.
- **Counts after (before):** curve: 1-drops 14 (15), 5-drops 8 (7); average mana value 2.88 (2.81), 184 over 64 (180); white pips W36 (37); artifacts 16 (15), artifacts in all 23 (22), instants 13 (14), creatures 21 (21) without Karn; coloured artifacts 14 (14), colourless artifacts one more; blink effects 6 (6); Game Changers 15 (15); land sources unchanged. Karn's colour count on entry does not change.
- **Limits:** Conjurer's Closet is Full in `data/card_overrides.toml` ("end step: blink the best ETB creature"); how well the AI picks the target and when it declines is unmeasured. The guide's text that instant-speed blinks resolve in one action now names Ephemerate and Restoration Angel; it stays true. The `data/spellbook/karn_gilded.json` file still holds the Cloudshift combos (the Spellbook fetch refreshes it).
- **To check in the first run:** how often Conjurer's Closet is cast and by which turn (against Karn's median turn 6), how often its blink targets Karn (the `flicker Conjurer's Closet -> Karn, Gilded Guardian` stat), the library and hand size after repeated end-step blinks (`death_kind decked`, cleanup discards), and whether Karn's removals per game (0.31 at t4) rise without Cloudshift.

## List change 2026-11-25 (JD's choice, not simulated): The Fire Crystal in for The Wind Crystal
JD's decision ("i am going with the fire crystal", "i own it"; printing fin 337): The Fire Crystal ({2}{R}{R} legendary artifact, JD's own copy; `data/ownership.toml` marks it `have` in this deck) takes the slot of The Wind Crystal ({2}{W}{W} legendary artifact, one of the cards JD was getting, now off the `missing` list). Not simulated; the 2026-11-24 runs below had The Wind Crystal in the list. A package change, not slot-for-slot by the packages rule: the type and the mana value match, the jobs do not.
- **Job lost:** a white coloured artifact (white keeps Esper Sentinel, The Mind Stone, Gold-Forged Thopteryx and Breya, so Karn's colour count does not move); the doubling of life gained (the drain package of Haliya, Vito, Sanguine Bond and Exquisite Blood now runs on single gains: the Exquisite Blood loop with Sanguine Bond or Vito still runs on any gain, Haliya's trigger is 1 life); {1} off white spells (it reduced only the generic part, so nothing for a clean five-colour Karn). In the 2026-11-24 baseline The Wind Crystal won 19.5% of games when cast at t4 (correlation only, the deck's own t4 rate was 23.6%).
- **Job gained:** a red coloured artifact (red, the thinnest colour, goes from three to four coloured artifacts); haste for every creature we control (Karn attacks the turn he enters or is blinked back in a precombat main phase, which the AI does not weigh); {4}{R}{R}, {T}: a token copy of a creature we control, sacrificed at the beginning of the next end step (a copy of Karn draws his full colour count again and the legend rule keeps one Karn; Flickerwisp, Skyclave Apparition, Solitude and the other enter triggers repeat; the copy has haste from the Crystal itself); {1} off red spells (generic part only; the deck's red spells are Cursed Mirror, Breya, Station, The Reaper and Karn himself, who is red, so it saves a mana only when a {2} is paid for a hybrid symbol or the tax).
- **Counts after (before):** coloured artifacts 14 (14), non-solo colours W4 U3 B4 R4 G4 (W5 U3 B4 R3 G4; the Courier and Station are all five alone); pips W34 (36), U16, B23, R5 (3), G4; curve unchanged (4-drops 9, average mana value 2.88, 184 over 64); card types and Game Changers (15) unchanged; land sources unchanged. Buys 17 (18).
- **Modelling:** The Fire Crystal is Full in `data/card_overrides.toml`; 29 tests in `rust/crates/sim-core/tests/cards_fire_crystal.rs`. The cost change and the haste are compiled (checked against the Oracle text: generic part only, red and multicolour red spells, its controller's own spells and creatures only); the copy ability is card code for a person (`impls/karn_gilded_cards.rs fire_crystal_go`, listed through `human/cards.rs`), with the token sacrificed by `turn::end_step` at the next end step to begin (`DataKey::CopySac`, taken as the step begins, so a copy made during an end step waits for the following one).
- **AI facts (named limits for JD to accept):** the AI never activates the copy ability (it has no rule and no card-code hook; only a person can). It values the Crystal by its compiled statics alone, as a cost change and a haste source (`ai::values::cast_prio`: cast priority 54 on early turns and 21 from turn 8 in the Karn seat, against 81 and 48 measured for The Wind Crystal); the copy ability adds nothing to that number. A creature token with no card record (a Robot, a Pilot, a Treasure-like token) can't be copied by the engine.
- **To check in the first run:** how often The Fire Crystal is cast and by which turn, whether the AI ever activates the copy ability and on what, Karn's attack count when blinked with the Crystal out, and the drain lines without a doubler (the Exquisite Blood win rate against the 2026-11-24 numbers).

## Tested
First runs on the Felidar list (commit 551d1d00, loose profile, 1,500 games per tier, seed 500000; outputs in `sims-2026-11-24/`). Every figure below is labelled t4 or t5; the Ur-Dragon and Sheoldred paired runs are recorded in their own findings files (`audit/urdragon/`, `audit/sheoldred/`, outputs in their `sims-2026-11-24/`).
- **Goldfish** (500 games against three inert 40-life seats): median kill turn 9.0, 44.6% won by turn 8, 88.6% of kills by combo; Karn cast in 79.8% of games at a median own turn of 5; cleanup discards 2.70 a game; mana short on 16.6% of turns (32.6% on turns 1 to 4); the deck itself lost in 0.2% of games (no cause given).
- **`--analyze` against the table: t4 23.6%, t5 20.1%** (a four-player pod at fair odds is 25%; binomial noise about 2.2 points per tier). Recorded bars: Y'shtola t5 35.1% (unpaired, main f1ffd896; a later rerun gave 31.3%), Yoshimaru t4 27.5% and t5 24.5%. The deck plays below both decks it replaced.
  - **Wins by combo:** t4 93.8% of wins (about 22.1 points), t5 91.7% (about 18.4 points); damage is 6.2% of wins at t4 (about 1.5 points) and 8.3% at t5 (about 1.7).
  - **Carried-over pieces win:** t4 Thassa's Oracle cast in 15.3% of games and won 90.4% of those, Demonic Consultation 65.5%, Exquisite Blood 55.4%, Sanguine Bond 44.9%, Ringsight 43.4%, Demonic Tutor 42.2%; at t5 Oracle 13.9% cast and 82.8% won, Consultation 59.1%, Exquisite Blood 51.7%.
  - **New cards, mixed:** t4 Buster Sword 17.5%, Shorikai 19.0%, The Wind Crystal 19.5% (it was in the list then; The Fire Crystal replaced it on 2026-11-25) and Gandalf 20.8% win when cast; at t5 Gandalf is 31.1% (+11.0, among the highest) and Buster Sword 21.0%. Correlation only: expensive cards are cast in games already going well.
  - **Karn resolves** in 55.2% of games at t4 and 54.9% at t5, median turn 6; opponents remove him most (0.31 times a game at t4).
  - **What eliminates Karn:** t4 Krenko by combat 13.0%, Edgar Markov 9.9%, Yuriko by triggers 9.0%, Chulane by combo 8.2%; t5 Kinnan by combo 14.7%, Urza by combo 14.4%, Miirym by combat 10.7%, Zur by combo 9.6%, Yawgmoth by combo 7.2%. At t4 the killers are mostly fast creature decks, at t5 mostly combo decks.
- **Defence package** (paired, baseline = the current list, variant = the swaps, so the delta is variant minus baseline): Toxic Deluge, Austere Command, Fatal Push and Propaganda in for Shorikai, The One Ring, Coalition Relic and Counterspell: t4 23.6% to 25.9% (+2.3, noise +-2.4, just inside it), t5 20.1% to 20.4% (+0.3, noise +-2.4): no clear effect, so the dropped defence suite is not shown to be the main cause (clearest at t5).
- **Where the gap to Y'shtola sits (arithmetic on two unpaired reports, a hypothesis, not a test), t5:** Karn wins about 18.4 points by combo and 1.7 by damage; Y'shtola won about 21.8 points by combo (62.2% of 35.1%) and 13.2 by damage (37.6%). The combo gap is 3.4 points and the damage gap 11.5. Oracle was cast in 13.9% of Karn's games against 15.5% of Y'shtola's, and Karn resolves in 55% of games against 80% for Y'shtola, whose trigger drained from about turn 4.
- **Not tested:** the current list (Esper Sentinel went in for Felidar Guardian, Birthing Pod for Shorikai and Conjurer's Closet for Cloudshift on 2026-11-24 and The Fire Crystal for The Wind Crystal on 2026-11-25, all untested, together with the Arzakon changes), Pithing Needle for Counterspell, a drain package (Sludge Strider, Thopter Foundry, Disciple of the Vault, Marionette Master, Blood Artist) and Darkness Crystal against The Fire Crystal.
