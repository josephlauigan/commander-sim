# Kinnan/Basalt outlet correction

The generic Kinnan + Basalt line still requires its existing recognized mana sink; it is unchanged. The Thrasios alternate now requires Kinnan, Basalt, Thrasios, a reachable Finale of Devastation, enough actual green mana to cast Finale, and an available first main-phase combat. It reserves the three mana needed to start a tapped Basalt alongside Finale's GG, then pays both together before the loop without treating unbounded colorless as colored mana (the shortcut spends GG early even if Thrasios is countered). Thrasios's activation and Finale's cast have separate interaction windows. UG being available no longer suppresses this outlet. Finale's combat remains a shortcut; to avoid declaring a win through ordinary blockers it requires enough untapped, non-sick attackers for all living opponents and no untapped opposing creatures. The combat check is repeated after the finisher cast; attack taxes, zero-attacker caps and Glacial Chasm also make the shortcut unavailable; other combat effects remain a limitation.

Mirage Mirror's former shortcut could award a win without checking a dual land or actual payoff. It is conservatively unavailable until those are modeled. This also leaves some real Kinnan wins unmodeled. This change does not expand the generic Kinnan mana sink or model a win with no payoff.

Focused regression coverage is in `engine_t6_combo3`: UG and no UG with Finale, missing outlet, missing payoff, insufficient green, tapped Basalt startup, countered Thrasios and Finale, and disabled Mirage. Existing `combo_outlets` covers the generic no-payoff guard.
