# Tier 6 list rebuild: implementation and verification

2026-11-03. Coordinator's Spellbook guard, full suite and simulation validation are pending. The list changes are hypotheses about speed, not measured improvements. TopDeck.gg API v2 data, courtesy of [TopDeck.gg](https://topdeck.gg), with results as listed on [EDHTop16](https://edhtop16.com), were saved in the original task scratchpad: top-eight finishes in events of at least 40 players, July–November 2026. See the pool changelog for frequencies and rationale.

Compared current origin/main `data/decks.json` with this branch's exported lists using jq. Every cut below is present in origin/main and every addition absent there. Scryfall cache reports every addition Commander-legal; the identities below all fit their commanders. All 14 unique additions were rated Full by current cardgen audit; caveats follow.

- **Kinnan (UG):** cut Colossal Skyturtle, Dispel, Dizzy Spell, Exotic Orchard, Imposter Mech, Sudden Substitution, Volatile Stormdrake; add Bloom Tender (G), Gaea's Cradle (G), Green Sun's Zenith (G), Incubation Druid (G), Seedborn Muse (G), Veil of Summer (G), Whir of Invention (U). The 156 real lists averaged ten mana creatures; these swaps increase mana and access to pieces. Whir finds artifacts and Green Sun's Zenith finds green creatures. The aura alternative was excluded: the engine's Kinnan + aura line needs a recognized mana sink, absent here, and the Bloom Tender + aura Spellbook combinations were unmodeled in the prior guard output.
- **Sisay (WUBRG):** cut Magda, Brazen Outlaw; Mockingbird; Redirect Lightning; Spider-Woman, Stunning Savior; add Cryptolith Rite (G), Force of Negation (U), Nature's Rhythm (G), The Queen of Dale (W). The 105 real lists supported card filtering, tokens, mana, protection and creature access. The existing plan names the Derevi/Emiel/Cradle-or-Bloom Tender/Mount Doom and Ioreth/Marvin/mana legend/Mount Doom lines; no new combo is claimed.
- **Rograkh/Thrasios (URG):** cut Chatterstorm, Urza's Saga, Wan Shi Tong, Librarian; add Elvish Mystic (G), Llanowar Elves (G), Mana Confluence (colorless identity). The 123 real lists averaged 5.3 mana creatures; two early dorks and a colored land support the existing creature/land lines. The plan removes the two cut nonlands from its engine and priority groups; no new combo is claimed.

The earlier diagnosis (`audit/t6-structure/diagnosis-2026-11-01.md`) identifies readiness and early mana as constraints: Kinnan ready 22–23% at median round 7, Sisay 19–31% at round 7, Rograkh/Thrasios 15–21% at round 8. The saved baseline ordering run, 300 games per deck, seed 500000, loose lookahead, recorded 22.7% (18.3–27.7), 20.7% (16.5–25.6), and 13.7% (10.2–18.0), respectively, into Tier 5 (`/tmp/claude-1000/-home-joseph-projects/61d4d38b-9b1e-4bc1-8845-ec4ae3a757af/scratchpad/t6-lists/check1-base.txt`). The saved swap output was incomplete; no post-change result is claimed. Saved trace files exist in that scratchpad's `tr/`, but their first Kinnan trace seats obsolete pool decks, so they are not reliable evidence for the current pool. No new simulations were run.

**Engine limitation escalated:** `combos.rs::kinnan_basalt` requires a recognized mana sink (Ballista or Urza), neither in this deck. `t6_combos_extra.rs` has Kinnan/Basalt/Thrasios and Kinnan/Basalt/Mirage Mirror handlers, but they return not ready when UG can be paid, expecting the generic handler to work. Thus those positions are missed when UG is available. Hullbreaker/Kinnan/eligible rocks has its own handler. Sisay's named lines and Rograkh/Thrasios's Oboro/Cradle/Gates, Witness/Snap/Cradle and Badgermole/clone variants have handlers in `t6_combos_t6.rs`; the latter's Finale/Mastermind finisher is specific to those lines. Do not infer that finisher applies to Kinnan. No engine or AI code was changed.
