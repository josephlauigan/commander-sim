# Najeela, the Blade-Blossom — 5c Tokens & Extra Combats

2026-09-20
**New commander.** This is the former Kenrith, the Returned King token shell re-helmed by Najeela, the Blade-Blossom (5-color identity from her {W}{U}{B}{R}{G} activation, so the 99 is legal unchanged). The commander round was three cards: out Kenrith, Faeburrow Elder, and Cyclonic Rift; in Najeela, Birds of Paradise, and Relic of Legends.
**Updated 2026-09-21.** Cloud's Limit Break → Sterling Grove (enchantment protection and a third enchantment tutor).
**Updated 2026-09-22.** Two swaps: **Smothering Tithe → Coalition Relic** (Tithe moved to Sephiroth) and **Duskana, the Rage Mother → Cryptolith Rite.**
**Updated 2026-09-22 (second pass).** **Cryptolith Rite → Talisman of Dominance**, a straight trade with the Sephiroth deck. Cryptolith Rite completed the Najeela infinite (attacking creatures untapped by her activation tap for WUBRG to activate her again), and the deck is meant to stay non-combo, so the Rite moved to Sephiroth and its Talisman of Dominance came here. Net for the day: Duskana and Smothering Tithe out, Coalition Relic and Talisman of Dominance in. **Zero Game Changers, no two-card infinite.** Deck stays at 100 and 37 lands.

## Strategy

Ramp into a wide board, make every token bigger than it should be, then use Najeela to take extra combats behind a team-wide anthem and swing for the table.

**Najeela, the Blade-Blossom** ({2}{R}, 3/2 Human Warrior) does two things. First, whenever a Warrior you control attacks, you may make a 1/1 white Warrior token tapped and attacking — so a Warrior-heavy attack widens itself mid-combat. Second, for {W}{U}{B}{R}{G} during combat she untaps all your attacking creatures, gives them trample, lifelink, and haste until end of turn, and grants an additional combat phase. That activation is the whole engine: one repeatable "swing again, bigger, with lifelink" button, provided you can produce one mana of every color.

**Early: ramp and see cards.** Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone, Talisman of Dominance, Chromatic Lantern, Coalition Relic, Relic of Legends, Birds of Paradise, Nature's Lore, Cultivate, Skyshroud Claim. Sylvan Library and Mystic Remora carry the early card flow; tapped tri-lands and Temples are fine on turns one to three because nothing wants to be cast yet. **Coalition Relic** is the new three-drop rock — any colour, and it can bank a charge counter to produce two mana on a later turn, which is exactly the burst Najeela's WUBRG activation wants. **Talisman of Dominance** is the new two-drop: it makes blue or black, the two colours this deck only splashes, so it fills the hardest pips in the activation.

**Midgame: build the board.** Adeline, Emmara, Rhys the Redeemed, Mycoloth, Jinnie Fay, Trostani's Summoner, and Hornet Queen make bodies. Legion's Landing, Rabble Rousing, and Felidar Retreat make them every turn. Secure the Wastes and March of the Multitudes do it at instant speed. **Rhys and Secure the Wastes are the two that make actual Warriors**, so they're the pieces that trigger Najeela's on-attack token-making; Professional Face-Breaker and Najeela herself are Warrior bodies too. Everything else is a non-Warrior token that doesn't trigger her first ability but still rides her extra combat and anthem.

**The payoff layer.** Cathars' Crusade puts a +1/+1 counter on every creature you control each time a creature enters — and Najeela's tokens *enter attacking*, so a wide Najeela combat stacks counters as it goes. Garruk's Uprising adds trample and draws on every big creature that lands, and Warleader's Call plus Wispdrinker Vampire drain on top, once per attacker per combat. Because these payoffs are enchantments and the deck folds if they're picked off, **Sterling Grove sits over them** — shroud on Cathars' Crusade, Warleader's Call, Sylvan Library, and Mystic Remora, so targeted removal bounces off the pieces you can't afford to lose.

**Closing.** The nut turn is a wide board, Najeela's activation for trample/lifelink/haste and a second combat, and Cathars' Crusade compounding counters across both swings — often lethal with lifelink papering over the crackback. Mirror Entity is the instant-speed finisher: pay X, the whole token board becomes X/X. Overwhelming Stampede or End-Raze Forerunners are the non-Najeela overrun. Against a stalled board, Warleader's Call and Wispdrinker Vampire win without attacking at all.

**Top end as a backup.** Atraxa Grand Unifier, Sun Titan, Nicol Bolas, Sauron, The Dawning Archaic, Wurmcoil Engine, and Atraxa, Praetors' Voice (which also proliferates your Crusade counters) for the games where the token plan gets swept.

## Consistency

The list is exactly 100 cards with 37 lands: 26 nonbasic plus 3 Forest, 2 Plains, 2 Mountain, 2 Island, 2 Swamp.

**Najeela is color-hungry to *activate*.** Her ability costs one mana of all five colours at once, so the fixing has to actually assemble WUBRG.

| Fixing type | Cards |
| --- | --- |
| Any colour, untapped | Command Tower, Exotic Orchard, Gemstone Mine, Spire of Industry, Aether Hub, Spectator Seating, The World Tree |
| Any colour, tapped or slow | Path of Ancestry, Vivid Creek, Vivid Grove, Vivid Marsh |
| Tri-lands (all tapped) | Arcane Sanctum, Crumbling Necropolis, Jungle Shrine, Savage Lands, Seaside Citadel |
| Rocks / dorks | Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Chromatic Lantern, Coalition Relic, Fellwar Stone, Relic of Legends, Talisman of Dominance, Birds of Paradise |
| Land ramp | Nature's Lore, Cultivate, Skyshroud Claim, Wargate |

Chromatic Lantern and The World Tree are the true enablers — either one turns every land into an any-color source, which is what reliably powers repeated Najeela activations. Firing her once is easy by turn five or six; firing her twice in a turn needs ten open mana with all five colours twice over, which is why Coalition Relic's stored charge and Relic of Legends earn their slots.

**Coalition Relic replaces Smothering Tithe's mana.** Tithe's Treasures were the deck's burst fuel for Najeela and Mirror Entity; Coalition Relic is steadier (it doesn't depend on opponents declining to pay) and gives a two-mana spike off a stored charge counter, but it doesn't scale over a long game the way an untaxed Tithe did. Talisman of Dominance adds a second cheap rock so the ramp count holds.

**Token redundancy is the deck's strength.** Fourteen-plus cards make bodies, across creatures, enchantments (Legion's Landing, Rabble Rousing, Felidar Retreat), and instant-speed spells (Secure the Wastes, March of the Multitudes). Wurmcoil Engine adds two more on death. No single removal spell sets you back more than a turn.

**Pump redundancy is thinner.** Cathars' Crusade, Overwhelming Stampede, End-Raze Forerunners, Najeela's anthem, and Mirror Entity as a mass-pump finisher. Duskana left in this round, so it's one piece lighter; Cathars' Crusade is still doing the heavy lifting, and Sterling Grove can shield it from spot removal.

**Enchantment protection.** The deck's protection suite — Unbreakable Formation, Clever Concealment, Duty Beyond Death, Ephemerate, Restoration Angel, Lightning Greaves — protects *creatures*. Sterling Grove's shroud covers the enchantments, and Cathars' Crusade is the removal magnet it most needs to protect. Two honest limits: shroud doesn't stop a board wipe, and it doesn't protect Sterling Grove itself — so lead with it when you can.

**Card draw.** Sylvan Library, Mystic Remora, Harmonize, Shamanic Revelation, Garruk's Uprising, Bonders' Enclave, Elrond's scry, Atraxa's ETB. Adequate, and Shamanic Revelation scales with the board.

**Sweepers you can cast.** Austere Command modes let you spare tokens, Vanquish the Horde scales down as the board grows, and Vandalblast is one-sided. There's no one-sided bounce reset since Cyclonic Rift left.

## Key lines

**The Najeela combat turn.** With a wide board, hold up WUBRG and swing. Warriors trigger her token-making as they attack; then activate: untap all attackers, give the team trample/lifelink/haste, take a second combat. If Cathars' Crusade is out, every token that entered attacking already pumped the whole board, so combat two hits harder and wider than combat one. Resolve tokens one at a time and let each Crusade trigger finish — the counters compound.

**Mirror Entity is the reach.** Even without Cathars' Crusade, a wide board plus Mirror Entity is lethal: pay X, everything becomes X/X, and with Najeela's trample and haste that's often game. It's an activated ability — hold it up and fire at instant speed to dodge sorcery-speed answers.

**Instant speed is the whole point.** Secure the Wastes, March of the Multitudes, Clever Concealment, and Unbreakable Formation all let you hold up a response and still develop. Pass with mana up, then either protect the board or make it at an opponent's end step and untap into a Najeela swing.

**Rhys and Secure the Wastes feed the Warrior trigger.** Rhys makes 1/1 Elf *Warrior* tokens; Secure the Wastes makes 1/1 *Warrior* tokens. Those are the token-makers that multiply through Najeela's first ability on attack — prioritize them before a Najeela alpha strike over, say, Hornet Queen's Insects.

**Lead with Sterling Grove when the pod holds enchantment removal.** Cast it, sac it later to top-deck Cathars' Crusade if you don't have it — or leave it out to blanket your board in shroud. Because it protects *other* enchantments, resolve it before the Crusade when you expect a Disenchant effect.

**Mycoloth wants a board already.** Devour on cast, then double the tokens each upkeep. Best target for Lightning Greaves — shroud plus haste means it eats and swings the same turn.

**Skyclave Apparition and Ephemerate** are the value line: blink the Apparition to exile a second permanent, and the token it leaves behind still counts for the go-wide plan.

**Sun Titan and The Dawning Archaic** rebuy Legion's Landing, Felidar Retreat, and the cheap enchantments after a wipe — the deck's main way back into a game it lost on board.

## Tutor targets by board state

Three ways to go get a card now: **Diabolic Tutor** (any card to hand, {2}{B}{B}), **Wargate** (X, search any *permanent* onto the battlefield tapped), and **Sterling Grove** (sac to put an *enchantment* on top of your library). Wargate can't fetch instants or sorceries, and Sterling Grove only finds enchantments and delays a turn.

- **Wide board, no payoff → Cathars' Crusade.** Wargate for {6}, Sterling Grove to top, or Diabolic Tutor to hand.
- **Board set, need to close now → Mirror Entity** (Wargate it in and activate the same turn) or an anthem you're missing.
- **Early, want the mana online → Chromatic Lantern or Coalition Relic** via Wargate.
- **Need the instant-speed pieces → Diabolic Tutor only.** Secure the Wastes, March, Clever Concealment, Anguished Unmaking, and the sweepers are spells Wargate and Sterling Grove can't touch.
- **Swept / behind → a rebuild permanent.** Wargate for Sun Titan or a token enchantment, or Diabolic Tutor for a sweeper if you need to reset first.
- **Missing your fifth color for Najeela → Wargate for The World Tree or Chromatic Lantern** to unlock the activation.

Rule of thumb: Sterling Grove is your cheapest enchantment finder *and* a protection piece; Wargate for the permanent you want in play *now*; Diabolic Tutor for anything the other two legally can't get.

## Bracket and Rule 0

**Zero Game Changers and no two-card infinite.** Smothering Tithe moved to Sephiroth and Cryptolith Rite moved there too, so nothing on the list is a Game Changer and the classic Najeela loop is gone. On paper that clears Bracket 2's hard lines, but three tutors (Diabolic Tutor, Wargate, Sterling Grove) and a commander that takes extra combats with a team anthem play above Bracket 2's power — **treat it as a low-end Bracket 3**, or talk it through Rule 0 if the pod is running Bracket 2.

**The design rule stands: nothing that refunds Najeela's WUBRG every time she untaps your attackers.** That's Faeburrow Elder, Seedborn Muse, Derevi, Cryptolith Rite, Sword of Feast and Famine, and similar — any of them turns Najeela into a two-card infinite. Keep them out if you want the deck non-combo.

**One edge case to know: Relic of Legends.** Relic can tap any untapped legendary creature you control for one mana of any colour, and Najeela's activation untaps your attacking creatures. So if Najeela and four other legendary creatures (Adeline, Emmara, Rhys, Jinnie Fay, Elrond, either Atraxa, Sauron, The Dawning Archaic) are all attacking — or three plus an attacking Birds of Paradise — they can pay for the next activation, and the loop repeats. It's not a two-card combo; it needs five specific creatures attacking at once and is rare in practice. But if it comes up, say so rather than surprising the table, and swap Relic of Legends for another rock if you'd rather rule it out entirely.

Sylvan Library and Mystic Remora both feel like Game Changers and are not on the list, so they cost you nothing on bracket.

Disclose before the game:

- **Najeela takes extra combats and hands the team trample, lifelink, and haste** — the table should know an empty-looking board can swing twice.
- **Austere Command and Vanquish the Horde** as sweepers.

With three open Game Changer slots, Rhystic Study or a replacement board reset (Cyclonic Rift) are the natural adds if you want to move firmly into Bracket 3; neither adds a combo. Re-check the official list at https://commanderbrackets.com/faq before an event.

## Flags and tuning levers

**The 09-22 changes — net 2 out, 2 in.**
- **Smothering Tithe → Coalition Relic.** Tithe moved to Sephiroth. You lose the deck's only Game Changer and its best long-game mana engine; you gain a reliable any-colour rock with a charge-counter burst.
- **Duskana, the Rage Mother → Talisman of Dominance** (via Cryptolith Rite, which was in the list briefly and then traded to Sephiroth for the Talisman to keep this deck non-combo). The Talisman is a cheap blue/black source for Najeela's two splash colours.

**The commander round still stands.** Kenrith → Najeela; Faeburrow Elder → Birds of Paradise; Cyclonic Rift → Relic of Legends.

**Warrior sub-theme is real but narrow.** Only Rhys, Secure the Wastes, Professional Face-Breaker, Najeela, and her own tokens are Warriors. The deck is a go-wide extra-combat deck first and a Warrior deck a distant second.

**Three open Game Changer slots.** None used. A replacement board reset (Cyclonic Rift, or Rout / Farewell) and Rhystic Study are the obvious candidates if you want to spend them; neither adds a combo.

**Two Atraxas, both legal.** Atraxa, Grand Unifier (also the Sephiroth commander) and Atraxa, Praetors' Voice are different cards. The Grand Unifier copy is the physical card shared with Sephiroth.

**Vorinclex, Monstrous Raider, when you own him,** doubles Cathars' Crusade and Felidar Retreat — an excellent fit. With Duskana gone, the softest remaining slot is Nicol Bolas, Dragon-God (demanding {U}{B}{R} in a deck whose blue and black are splashes).

**Card conflicts with your other decks.**
- **Smothering Tithe moved to Sephiroth** — no longer in this list.
- **Coalition Relic** is the copy the Sauron deck cut earlier; it lives only here now.
- **Diabolic Tutor is now in three decks** (this one, Sephiroth, and Sauron) — three physical copies.
- Atraxa, Grand Unifier is the Sephiroth commander — one card in two decks.
- Shared with **Sephiroth**: Anguished Unmaking, Birds of Paradise, Cultivate, Fabled Passage, Swords to Plowshares.
- Shared with **Sauron**: Crumbling Necropolis, Lightning Greaves.
- Shared with **Veyran**: Fellwar Stone, Temple of Epiphany, Terramorphic Expanse.
- Shared with **Sephiroth and Sauron**: Chromatic Lantern, Treno Dark City.
- Shared with **Sephiroth and Veyran**: Ash Barrens, Evolving Wilds.
- Shared with **Sauron and Veyran**: Chaos Warp, Path of Ancestry.
- Staples in all four decks: Arcane Signet, Command Tower, Exotic Orchard, Sol Ring.
- **Talisman of Dominance moved here from Sephiroth; Cryptolith Rite moved from here to Sephiroth.** Both are single copies.
- Singles to this deck: Coalition Relic, Relic of Legends, Sterling Grove, Talisman of Dominance, Temple of Silence.
- **To the binder**: Duskana, the Rage Mother.

**Marginal slots.** Nicol Bolas, Dragon-God is now the softest playable, and the first cut for Vorinclex, more pump, or a board reset.
