# Tergrid — Mono-Black Discard & Edicts

Opponent pool deck — **Tier 3: High Bracket 3**. Axis: Disruption / discard & edicts.

- **Commander:** Tergrid, God of Fright // Tergrid's Lantern
- **Color identity:** Black
- **Game Changers (3):** Tergrid, God of Fright // Tergrid's Lantern, Demonic Tutor, Orcish Bowmasters
- **Combos:** None.
- **Lands:** 36 (10 nonbasic + 26 basic)

## Role in the pool

Hand and board disruption. Punishes Veyran's hand-held combo and any deck that relies on a single creature. Dauthi Voidwalker and Bojuka Bog are graveyard hate.

## Strategy

Force opponents to discard and sacrifice, then use Tergrid to take every nontoken permanent they lose. Edict creatures (Fleshbag, Plaguecrafter, Archfiend of Depravity) and Grave Pact / Dictate of Erebos steal creatures; Liliana of the Veil, Hymn, Mind Twist, and Tinybones steal permanents out of hand. Waste Not and Megrim pay off every discard. No mass land denial: land-sacrifice effects like Pox are left out.

## Sim modeling notes

Model these first; if any is Partial or unmodeled, this deck plays below its real strength and its results should be flagged.

Tergrid's steal-on-discard/sacrifice (nontoken permanents only); edicts; symmetric vs. targeted discard; Grave Pact/Dictate; Cabal Coffers + Urborg mana.
