# Lathril — Golgari Elves

Opponent pool deck — **Tier 1: High Bracket 2 / Low Bracket 3**. Axis: Go-wide creatures.

- **Commander:** Lathril, Blade of the Elves
- **Color identity:** Black, Green
- **Game Changers (0):** none
- **Combos:** None.
- **Lands:** 34 (13 nonbasic + 21 basic)

## Role in the pool

A creature-heavy board that your sweepers need to answer at the right time, and a clean comparison point for Najeela's go-wide plan.

## Strategy

Dump a turn-one-to-three stream of mana elves, flood the board with Elf Warrior tokens from Lathril's combat damage and Elvish Promenade, then either tap ten Elves for Lathril's drain or overrun with Ezuri, Overwhelming Stampede, or Shaman of the Pack. Priest of Titania and Elvish Archdruid make the board a mana engine. No Craterhoof and no infinite loops at this tier.

## Sim modeling notes

Model these first; if any is Partial or unmodeled, this deck plays below its real strength and its results should be flagged.

Mana elves scaling with Elf count (Priest of Titania, Archdruid); Lathril's token-on-damage and tap-ten drain; lords and overruns; Skullclamp.
