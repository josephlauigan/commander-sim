# Alela, Artful Provocateur — Esper Faerie Flyers

Alela makes a 1/1 flying Faerie every time you cast an artifact or an enchantment, and gives every flier +1/+0. The deck
is built around that: more than twenty artifact and enchantment spells, a pile of fliers, cheap removal that happens to
be enchantments, and a handful of counterspells and card draw to keep the stream going. It wins by attacking in the
air with a wide team, helped by drain and team pumps. It is weak to board wipes, to mass removal for fliers, and to
decks that go over the top of a go-wide board before it gets going.

## Strategy

### The commander

Alela, Artful Provocateur ({1}{W}{U}{B}) is a 2/3 with flying, deathtouch and lifelink. *Other creatures you control with
flying get +1/+0. Whenever you cast an artifact or enchantment spell, create a 1/1 blue Faerie creature token with
flying.*

In practice Alela is a Faerie factory. Every artifact and enchantment you cast is a spell and a 2-power flier, so a
normal turn adds one or two attackers. Artifact creatures and the enchantment creature **Dockside Chef** count as well,
as long as you cast them. The Faeries are tokens, so they aren't cast and don't trigger Alela themselves. Alela's
lifelink and deathtouch also make her a good blocker, but she is the deck's engine, so keep her out of combat unless the
trade is worth it. If she dies, the cheap artifacts and enchantments are still fine cards, but the Faerie stream stops.

### Game plan

**Early turns (1–3).** Play cheap artifacts and enchantments and build toward Alela on turn 3 or 4. **Sol Ring**,
**Mind Stone**, **Page, Loose Leaf** and **Short Bow** are the early mana and bodies; **Feather of Flight**,
**Unquestioned Authority** and **Karmic Justice** are cheap enchantments. **Initiates of the Ebon Hand** and
**Dark Ritual** help cast Alela a turn early. **Rhystic Study** and **Night's Whisper** keep the hand full, and
**Hymn to Tourach** on turn 2 takes two cards from the opponent while they are still setting up.

**Middle turns (4–6).** Alela is out, so every artifact or enchantment is a Faerie. Cast the removal enchantments on the
biggest threats, add fliers, and start attacking in the air. Hold up a counterspell when you can; if you can't, keep
developing. Add the payoff creatures (see below) as they appear.

**Late game.** A wide air force plus a payoff finishes the game: **Shatterwing Pegasus** pumps the team, **Wispdrinker
Vampire** drains, **Tetsuko Umezawa, Fugitive** makes the Faeries unblockable, and **Opposition** locks the opponents'
board. The spare mana goes into **Cavalier of Dawn**, **Meteor Golem** and the blink effects.

### Removal that makes Faeries

**Arrest**, **Luminous Bonds**, **Static Prison**, **Ajani Fells the Godsire** and **Plan for All Outcomes** are all
enchantments, so each answers a threat and makes a Faerie.

- **Arrest** and **Luminous Bonds** stop a creature from attacking or blocking (Arrest also stops its activated
  abilities). They do not remove it, so use them on the creature that matters, not on one that will be gone anyway.
- **Static Prison** exiles a nonland permanent until it leaves. It gives two energy and costs one at the start of each of your
  first main phases, so it holds the permanent for about two turns and then goes away unless you find more energy
  (**Aether Hub** gives one when it enters). Use it as a tempo play, or on a permanent that only matters for a short while.
- **Ajani Fells the Godsire** exiles a creature with power 3 or more on chapter I, makes a Cat Warrior on chapter II and
  gives double strike on chapter III, which is a fast finish with a flying Faerie.
- **Plan for All Outcomes** puts a nonland permanent on the top or bottom of its owner's library (the owner chooses),
  and starts the Jace token (see below).
- **Karmic Justice** is a deterrent: if an opponent's spell or ability destroys one of your noncreature permanents, you
  destroy a permanent of theirs.

The instant-speed answers are **Prophesied End** (destroy any creature; if it wasn't attacking, its controller draws),
**Repel Calamity** (power or toughness 4 or more), **Last Gasp** (−3/−3), **Multiply by Zero** (base 0/0 until end of
turn, which kills most creatures that have no bonuses), **Vanquish the Weak** (power 3 or less), and **Spite // Malice**
(a counter for noncreature spells, or destroy a nonblack creature). **Lieutenant Kirtar** exiles an attacking creature,
and **Cavalier of Dawn** and **Meteor Golem** destroy a nonland permanent when they enter. Remember that the Cavalier
gives the permanent's controller a 3/3 Golem. Unsummon is a one-mana bounce that also saves your own creature.

### Payoffs for a wide flying board

- **Wispdrinker Vampire** drains each opponent for 1 whenever another creature with power 2 or less enters. A Faerie
  (power 2 with Alela out) triggers it, and so does every small creature you cast. Its {5}{W}{B}
  ability gives your small creatures deathtouch and lifelink.
- **Tetsuko Umezawa, Fugitive** makes creatures with power or toughness 1 or less unblockable. Faeries have toughness 1,
  so they all connect.
- **Jackdaw Savior** returns a creature card with lesser mana value from your graveyard to the battlefield whenever it or
  another flier you control dies.
- **Shatterwing Pegasus** pumps the team for {4}{W}.
- **Stirring Hopesinger** (flying, lifelink) puts a +1/+1 counter on each of your creatures whenever you cast an
  instant or sorcery that targets a creature, so removal and tricks grow the team.
- **Plumecreed Mentor** puts a +1/+1 counter on a creature you control without flying whenever it or another flier
  enters. **Pileated Provisioner** does the same once. **Desperate Futurescribe** gives another creature +1/+1 at the
  start of combat (a counter instead, if you have scried or surveilled this turn).
- **Opposition** taps an opponent's artifact, creature or land for each untapped creature you tap. Faeries are ideal
  fuel, because they are new tokens that haven't attacked yet.
- **Windborn Muse** (flying) makes each attacker cost its controller {2}.
- **Felidar Retreat** makes a 2/2 Cat Beast, or puts a +1/+1 counter on every creature, whenever a land enters.
- **Malcator, Purity Overseer** makes a 3/3 Golem when it enters, and another at your end step if three or more
  artifacts entered this turn.

### Control and tempo

**Essence Scatter**, **Negate**, **Statute of Denial** (with a blue creature, you loot), **Muddle the Mixture** (an
instant or sorcery) and **Spite // Malice** cover almost everything. **Judge's Familiar** is a one-mana flier that can
counter an instant or sorcery unless its controller pays {1}. **Notion Thief** stops opponents from drawing extra
cards, and draws them for you instead, which punishes tables full of draw spells. **Ethersworn Canonist** stops each
player from casting a second nonartifact spell in a turn, which hurts storm decks and still lets you cast artifacts
(which are the Faerie triggers). **Ray of Command** steals a blocker or an attacker for a turn. **Clever Concealment**
phases out your team to dodge a wipe or removal. **Sphinx's Approach** draws two at instant speed.

### Jace token mini-package

**Keeper of the Quiet Hour**, **Plan for All Outcomes** and **Fatehold Charm** each "empower Jace", making a Jace
planeswalker token (surveil 1 or draw a card) or adding loyalty to it. With a planeswalker out, **Fatehold Annex**,
**Theorix Annex** and **Meticulous Commons** enter untapped. **Venser, the Sojourner** is also a planeswalker.
Fatehold Charm also draws a card, bounces a spell or creature, or gives your team +1/+2.

### Blink and recursion

**Daydream** (with flashback) exiles a creature you control and returns it with a +1/+1 counter, so it also works as
protection against removal. **Venser, the Sojourner**'s +2 exiles a permanent you own and returns it at the next end
step. Both reuse the enters effects of **Cavalier of Dawn** and **Meteor Golem** (a second removal), **Inspiring
Overseer** (another card), **Aven Fogbringer** and **Airlift Chaplain**. **Emry, Lurker of the Loch** costs less with
artifacts, mills four when it enters, and then lets you cast artifacts from your graveyard (**Short Bow**, **Mind Stone**,
**Strixhaven Skycoach**, **Reconstructed Thopter**), each a new Faerie. **Reconstructed Thopter** can also be unearthed
for {2}. **Helping Hand** returns a creature with mana value 3 or less (**Page, Loose Leaf**, **Imperious Inkmage**,
**Dockside Chef**, a Hopesinger or Jackdaw Savior) and **Jackdaw Savior** returns a lesser one when a flier dies.

### The rest of the deck

- **Airlift Chaplain** (flying) mills three and keeps a Plains or a small creature, or grows.
- **Imperious Inkmage** surveils 2 on entering, which fixes the draws.
- **Dockside Chef** is an enchantment creature that sacrifices an artifact or creature to draw (use it on a Thopter after
  it has been unearthed, or on a Faerie that is about to die).
- **Strixhaven Skycoach** is a flying vehicle that fetches a basic land when it enters.
- **Inspiring Overseer** is a flying body that draws a card.

### How it wins

By combat in the air. With Alela out, each Faerie hits for 2, so four Faeries are 8 damage, with Pegasus, Hopesinger
counters or Felidar Retreat on top. Wispdrinker Vampire drains the table for 1 on every small creature that enters.
Tetsuko makes sure the Faeries can't be blocked, and Ajani's chapter III gives a creature double strike. Opposition and
Windborn Muse hold back the opponents' counter-attack so that the race is one-sided.

### Weak spots and how to play around them

- **Board wipes and mass bounce.** The deck is full of small creatures. Hold **Clever Concealment**, keep a counter up,
  and don't overextend: three or four Faeries are enough to win in a few turns.
- **Alela dying.** Cast her when you have a counter, a **Daydream** or an **Unquestioned Authority** (protection from
  creatures) ready, and put the Faerie makers first when you must choose.
- **Fast or very large threats.** Use the instant removal and **Spite // Malice** early, and cast **Windborn Muse** or
  **Opposition** before the attack comes. Don't spend the removal enchantments on small creatures.
- **Racing decks.** Alela, Stirring Hopesinger and Tymna have lifelink, and Windborn Muse and Opposition slow attackers down. Use them to stay ahead in a race, and keep Alela and the best payoffs back.

## Key lines

- **Turn 3 Alela** off Initiates of the Ebon Hand or Dark Ritual, then a cheap artifact or enchantment every turn.
- **Removal that makes a Faerie:** Arrest, Luminous Bonds, Static Prison on the biggest threat.
- **Wispdrinker Vampire + a Faerie stream:** each Faerie that enters drains the table for 1.
- **Tetsuko + Faeries:** every Faerie connects; Shatterwing Pegasus turns the swarm into a kill.
- **Opposition + new Faeries:** tap down the opponent's blockers and lands on their turn with tokens that haven't
  attacked.
- **Tymna draws after a hit:** **Tymna the Weaver** lets you pay X life and draw X cards in a postcombat main phase,
  where X is the number of opponents dealt combat damage this turn. Faeries are the ones that connect, so a wide air
  force draws cards as well as dealing damage. Her lifelink offsets the life you pay.

**Mulligan:** keep 3+ lands and at least one cheap artifact or enchantment; land Alela on turn 3 or 4.

## Mana and consistency

36 lands: 11 nonbasic and 25 basic (9 Plains, 9 Island, 7 Swamp). Mana sources besides lands: **Sol Ring**, **Mind
Stone**, **Page, Loose Leaf**, **Initiates of the Ebon Hand** and **Dark Ritual**; **Strixhaven Skycoach** fetches a
basic.

- **White-black duals:** Isolated Chapel (enters tapped unless you control a Plains or a Swamp), Shattered Sanctum
  (enters tapped unless you control two or more other lands), Meticulous Commons (enters tapped unless you control a
  planeswalker) and Tainted Field ({W} or {B} if you control a Swamp).
- **White-blue and blue-black:** Fatehold Annex and Theorix Annex (both enter tapped unless you control a planeswalker).
- **Three-colour and fixing:** Exotic Orchard (any colour an opponent's land could produce, which helps a three-colour
  deck), Path of Ancestry (enters tapped; mana in your commander's colours), Aether Hub (one energy, then any colour
  once) and Hidden Grotto ({1}, {T} for any colour, with surveil 1).
- **Ghost Quarter** is the utility land: it destroys a land, and is useful against nonbasic-heavy decks or
  utility lands.

**Card draw:** Rhystic Study, Night's Whisper, Inspiring Overseer, Tymna the Weaver, Notion Thief, Sphinx's Approach,
Mind Stone, and the Auras Feather of Flight and Unquestioned Authority (each draws a card when it enters).

**Interaction:** about a dozen removal spells and creatures, and six counters (Essence Scatter, Negate, Statute of
Denial, Muddle the Mixture, Spite // Malice, Judge's Familiar). The deck has 27 creatures.

## Bracket and Rule 0

**Bracket 3,** with two Game Changers: **Rhystic Study** and **Notion Thief**. No two-card infinite combos, no mass land
destruction, no extra turns. Opposition is a soft lock piece worth mentioning before the game.
