# Alela, Artful Provocateur — Esper Faerie Flyers

2026-10-05
**Added 2026-10-05.** In the simulator as deck key `alela` (`python3 -m commander_sim --deck alela --pool t3`) and in practice mode.

## Strategy

Alela, Artful Provocateur ({1}{W}{U}{B}) is a 2/3 with flying, deathtouch and lifelink. *Other creatures you control with flying get +1/+0. Whenever you cast an artifact or enchantment spell, create a 1/1 blue Faerie creature token with flying.* The deck runs about 21 artifact and enchantment spells, so a normal turn adds one or two flying attackers, and each Faerie hits for 2 with Alela out.

**Early turns (1–3).** Play cheap artifacts and enchantments to start the Faerie stream: Sol Ring, Mind Stone, Short Bow, Page, Loose Leaf, Feather of Flight, Unquestioned Authority and Karmic Justice. Initiates of the Ebon Hand and Dark Ritual help cast Alela on turn 3. Rhystic Study and Night's Whisper keep the hand full.

**Removal doubles as Faerie fuel.** Arrest, Luminous Bonds, Static Prison, Ajani Fells the Godsire and Plan for All Outcomes are all enchantments, so each one also makes a Faerie. The instant-speed answers are Prophesied End, Repel Calamity, Last Gasp, Multiply by Zero, Vanquish the Weak and Spite // Malice.

**Payoffs for a wide flying board:**
- **Wispdrinker Vampire** drains each opponent for 1 whenever a Faerie (power 2 or less) enters.
- **Tetsuko Umezawa, Fugitive** makes every Faerie unblockable (toughness 1).
- **Jackdaw Savior** returns a creature card from your graveyard to the battlefield whenever one of your fliers dies.
- **Shatterwing Pegasus** pumps the team for {4}{W}.
- **Stirring Hopesinger** puts a +1/+1 counter on each of your creatures whenever you target a creature with an instant or sorcery.
- **Opposition** turns each untapped Faerie into a tap-down for an opponent's creature, land or artifact.
- **Windborn Muse** taxes attackers.

**Control layer.** Notion Thief, Ethersworn Canonist, Essence Scatter, Negate, Statute of Denial and Spite // Malice slow opponents who race.

**Jace token mini-package.** Keeper of the Quiet Hour, Plan for All Outcomes and Fatehold Charm each "empower Jace", making a Jace planeswalker token that surveils or draws. With it out, Fatehold Annex, Theorix Annex and Meticulous Commons enter untapped.

**Blink value.** Daydream (with flashback) or Venser, the Sojourner blink Cavalier of Dawn or Meteor Golem for a second removal, or Inspiring Overseer for another card.

## Key lines

- **Turn 3 Alela** off Initiates of the Ebon Hand or Dark Ritual, then a cheap artifact or enchantment every turn.
- **Removal that makes a Faerie:** Arrest, Luminous Bonds, Static Prison on the biggest threat.
- **Wispdrinker Vampire + a Faerie stream:** each artifact or enchantment drains the table for 1.
- **Tetsuko + Faeries:** every Faerie connects; Shatterwing Pegasus turns the swarm into a kill.

**Mulligan:** keep 3+ lands and at least one cheap artifact or enchantment; land Alela on turn 3 or 4.

## Bracket and Rule 0

**Bracket 3,** with two Game Changers: **Rhystic Study** and **Notion Thief**. No two-card infinite combos, no mass land destruction, no extra turns. Opposition is a soft lock piece worth mentioning before the game.

**Bench (in bulk if you want to tune):** Unbreakable Formation, Cho-Manno's Blessing, Medic's Kitesail, Twisted Fates, Silence the Echo, Aven Fisher, Sanguine Glorifier, Way of the Mentor, Way of the Cryomancer, Rewrite Regrets, Mnemonic Wall, Bonders' Enclave.
