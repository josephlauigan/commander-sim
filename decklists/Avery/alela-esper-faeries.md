# Alela, Artful Provocateur — Esper Faerie Flyers

Alela makes a 1/1 flying Faerie every time you cast an artifact or an enchantment, and gives every other flier +1/+0.
The deck is built around that: more than twenty artifact and enchantment spells, a pile of fliers, cheap removal, a few
ways to recast the same artifacts and enchantments, and **Skullclamp** to turn spare Faeries into cards. It wins by
attacking in the air with a wide team, helped by drain and team pumps. It is weak to board wipes, to mass removal for
fliers, and to decks that go over the top of a go-wide board before it gets going.

## Strategy

### The commander

Alela, Artful Provocateur ({1}{W}{U}{B}) is a 2/3 with flying, deathtouch and lifelink. *Other creatures you control with
flying get +1/+0. Whenever you cast an artifact or enchantment spell, create a 1/1 blue Faerie creature token with
flying.*

In practice Alela is a Faerie factory. Every artifact and enchantment you cast is a spell and a 2-power flier, so a
normal turn adds one or two attackers. Artifact creatures (**Burnished Hart**, **Wall of Junk**, **Reconstructed
Thopter**, **Igneous Golem**, **Meteor Golem**) and the enchantment creature **Dockside Chef** count as well, as long as
you cast them. The Faeries are tokens, so they aren't cast and don't trigger Alela themselves. Alela's lifelink and
deathtouch also make her a good blocker, but she is the deck's engine, so keep her out of combat unless the trade is
worth it. If she dies, the cheap artifacts and enchantments are still fine cards, but the Faerie stream stops.

### Game plan

**Early turns (1–3).** Play cheap artifacts and enchantments and build toward Alela on turn 3 or 4. **Sol Ring**,
**Mind Stone** and **Dark Ritual** are the fast mana, and **Burnished Hart** fetches two basics later. **Short Bow**,
**Skullclamp**, **Credit Voucher** and **Lightning Greaves** are cheap artifacts; **Feather of Flight**, **Unquestioned
Authority**, **Angelic Shield** and **Karmic Justice** are cheap enchantments. Hold some of them until Alela is out, so
each one makes a Faerie. **Night's Whisper** keeps the hand full, and **Fabricate** finds the artifact you need most
(usually Skullclamp, or Sol Ring in an opening hand without fast mana).

**Middle turns (4–6).** Alela is out, so every artifact or enchantment is a Faerie. Put **Lightning Greaves** on her if
the table has spot removal. Spend removal on the biggest threats, add fliers, and start attacking in the air. Hold up a
counterspell when you can; if you can't, keep developing. Add the payoff creatures (see below) as they appear.

**Late game.** A wide air force plus a payoff finishes the game: **Shatterwing Pegasus** and **Serra the Benevolent**
pump the team, and **Wispdrinker Vampire** drains. **Skullclamp** turns every Faerie you don't need into two cards. The
spare mana goes into **Cavalier of Dawn**, **Meteor Golem**, **Cabal Patriarch** and the recursion pieces.

### Skullclamp and the Faeries

**Skullclamp** ({1}, equip {1}) gives +1/−1 and draws two cards when the equipped creature dies. A Faerie is a 2/1 with
Alela out, so with Skullclamp it becomes 3/0 and dies at once: {1} for two cards, every time. It does the same to the
other 1-toughness creatures (**Angelic Page**, **Judge's Familiar**, **Tragic Poet**, **Avenger en-Dal**) once they
have done their job. Skullclamp is the deck's main card-draw engine, so it is the first card to find with
**Fabricate**.

**Angelic Shield** gives your creatures +0/+1, which makes a Faerie a 2/2 and turns the Skullclamp trick off (it
becomes 3/1 and lives). Choose one or the other: keep the Shield when the table has pingers or −1/−1 effects, and
sacrifice it (it bounces a creature) when you want to draw with Skullclamp.

### Removal

**Arrest**, **Luminous Bonds** and **Plan for All Outcomes** are enchantments, so each answers a threat and makes a
Faerie.

- **Arrest** and **Luminous Bonds** stop a creature from attacking or blocking (Arrest also stops its activated
  abilities). They do not remove it, so use them on the creature that matters, not on one that will be gone anyway.
- **Plan for All Outcomes** puts a nonland permanent on the top or bottom of its owner's library (the owner chooses),
  and makes a Jace planeswalker token (surveil 1 or draw a card), which also lets **Meticulous Commons** and **Theorix
  Annex** enter untapped.
- **Karmic Justice** is a deterrent: if an opponent's spell or ability destroys one of your noncreature permanents, you
  destroy a permanent of theirs.

The instant-speed answers are **Swords to Plowshares** and **Path to Exile** (one mana, exile any creature; the first
gives its controller life, the second a basic land), **Prophesied End** (destroy any creature; if it wasn't attacking,
its controller draws), **Repel Calamity** (power or toughness 4 or more), **Last Gasp** (−3/−3) and **Multiply by
Zero** (base 0/0 until end of turn, which kills most creatures that have no bonuses). **Unsummon** and **Angelic Shield**
(sacrifice it) bounce a creature, or save one of yours.

The creatures that remove things:

- **Lieutenant Kirtar** exiles an attacking creature, and **Avenger en-Dal** ({2}{W}, tap, discard a card) does the
  same; its controller gains life equal to its toughness.
- **Cavalier of Dawn** and **Meteor Golem** destroy a nonland permanent when they enter. Remember that the Cavalier gives
  the permanent's controller a 3/3 Golem.
- **Cabal Patriarch** ({3}{B}{B}{B}, 5/5) gives a creature −2/−2 for {2}{B} plus a sacrificed creature (a Faerie is
  ideal) or a creature card exiled from your graveyard. It is triple black in a deck with eight Swamps, so it is a
  late-game card.

### Payoffs for a wide flying board

- **Wispdrinker Vampire** drains each opponent for 1 whenever another creature with power 2 or less enters. A Faerie
  (power 2 with Alela out) triggers it, and so does every small creature you cast. Its {5}{W}{B} ability gives your
  small creatures deathtouch and lifelink.
- **Serra the Benevolent** (planeswalker, 4 loyalty) gives your fliers +1/+1 for the turn with her +2, or makes a 4/4
  flying, vigilant Angel with her −3. Use the +2 on the turn you swing with the Faeries. Her −6 emblem keeps you at 1
  life as long as you control a creature. She also lets Meticulous Commons and Theorix Annex enter untapped.
- **Shatterwing Pegasus** pumps the team for {4}{W}.
- **Stirring Hopesinger** (flying, lifelink) puts a +1/+1 counter on each of your creatures whenever you cast an
  instant or sorcery that targets a creature, so removal and tricks grow the team.
- **Jackdaw Savior** returns a creature card with lesser mana value from your graveyard to the battlefield whenever it or
  another flier you control dies. With Skullclamp, a Faerie dying also triggers it.
- **Plumecreed Mentor** puts a +1/+1 counter on a creature you control without flying whenever it or another flier
  enters. **Pileated Provisioner** does the same once. **Desperate Futurescribe** gives another creature +1/+1 at the
  start of combat (a counter instead, if you have scried or surveilled this turn).
- **Windborn Muse** (flying) makes each attacker cost its controller {2}.
- **Felidar Retreat** makes a 2/2 Cat Beast, or puts a +1/+1 counter on every creature, whenever a land enters.
- **Malcator, Purity Overseer** makes a 3/3 Golem when it enters, and another at your end step if three or more
  artifacts entered this turn.
- **Angelic Page** is a two-mana flier that taps to give an attacking or blocking creature +1/+1.

### Control and tempo

**Essence Scatter**, **Negate**, **Statute of Denial** (with a blue creature, you loot) and **Muddle the Mixture** (an
instant or sorcery) cover most spells. **Judge's Familiar** is a one-mana flier that can counter an instant or sorcery
unless its controller pays {1}. **Notion Thief** stops opponents from drawing extra cards, and draws them for you
instead, which punishes tables full of draw spells. **Ethersworn Canonist** stops each player from casting a second
nonartifact spell in a turn, which hurts storm decks and still lets you cast artifacts (which are the Faerie triggers).
**Clever Concealment** phases out your team to dodge a wipe or removal.

**Lightning Greaves** (equip {0}) gives haste and shroud. Keep it on Alela against spot removal. Shroud also stops your
own spells, so move it to another creature first (equip {0}, at sorcery speed) before you target Alela with an Aura or
**Daydream**.

### Recursion and recasting

Because every cast artifact or enchantment is a Faerie, getting the same cards back is worth more here than in most
decks.

- **Wall of Junk** ({2}, 0/7 artifact defender) returns to your hand at end of combat when it blocks. Recast it for {2}
  and you get another Faerie, so it is a blocker that makes a token every time it is used.
- **Crystal Chimes** ({3}, then {3}, tap, sacrifice) returns every enchantment card in your graveyard to your hand:
  Arrests on creatures that have died, a sacrificed Angelic Shield, Feather of Flight, Dockside Chef. Each recast is a
  Faerie. **Tragic Poet** ({W}, sacrifice) returns one.
- **Emry, Lurker of the Loch** costs less with artifacts, mills four when it enters, and then lets you cast artifacts
  from your graveyard (**Short Bow**, **Mind Stone**, **Credit Voucher**, **Reconstructed Thopter**), each a new Faerie.
  **Reconstructed Thopter** can also be unearthed for {2}.
- **Gravedigger** and **Helping Hand** return creatures (Gravedigger to your hand, Helping Hand to the battlefield, mana
  value 3 or less), and **Jackdaw Savior** returns a lesser one when a flier dies.
- **Daydream** (with flashback) exiles a creature you control and returns it with a +1/+1 counter, so it also works as
  protection against removal. **Venser, the Sojourner**'s +2 exiles a permanent you own and returns it at the next end
  step. Both reuse the enters effects of **Cavalier of Dawn** and **Meteor Golem** (a second removal), **Inspiring
  Overseer** (another card), **Gravedigger** and **Airlift Chaplain**.

### The rest of the deck

- **Airlift Chaplain** (flying) mills three and keeps a Plains or a small creature, or grows.
- **Imperious Inkmage** surveils 2 on entering, which fixes the draws.
- **Dockside Chef** is an enchantment creature that sacrifices an artifact or creature to draw (use it on a Thopter after
  it has been unearthed, or on a Faerie that is about to die).
- **Inspiring Overseer** is a flying body that draws a card.
- **Credit Voucher** is a Faerie when cast; later, {2} and sacrifice it to shuffle the cards you don't want back and
  draw that many.
- **Mirage Mirror** is a Faerie when cast, and for {2} becomes a copy of any artifact, creature, enchantment or land
  until end of turn: copy the biggest creature on the table to attack or block.
- **Scrapheap** gains you 1 life whenever an artifact or enchantment goes to your graveyard from the battlefield
  (sacrificed Credit Voucher, Crystal Chimes and Angelic Shield; an Arrest when its creature dies).
- **Igneous Golem** is a 3/4 artifact creature that can gain trample, and a Faerie when cast.

### How it wins

By combat in the air. With Alela out, each Faerie hits for 2, so four Faeries are 8 damage, with Pegasus, Serra's +2,
Hopesinger counters or Felidar Retreat on top. Wispdrinker Vampire drains the table for 1 on every small creature that
enters. Skullclamp keeps the hand full while that happens, and Windborn Muse and Wall of Junk hold back the opponents'
counter-attack so that the race is one-sided.

### Weak spots and how to play around them

- **Board wipes and mass bounce.** The deck is full of small creatures. Hold **Clever Concealment**, keep a counter up,
  and don't overextend: three or four Faeries are enough to win in a few turns. Faeries you would lose to a wipe anyway
  are better spent on Skullclamp first.
- **Alela dying.** Cast her when you have **Lightning Greaves**, a counter, a **Daydream** or an **Unquestioned
  Authority** (protection from creatures) ready, and put the Faerie makers first when you must choose.
- **Fast or very large threats.** Use Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile and the other instant removal early, and cast
  **Windborn Muse** before the attack comes. Don't spend the removal enchantments on small creatures.
- **Racing decks.** Alela and Stirring Hopesinger have lifelink, Wall of Junk blocks every turn, and Windborn Muse slows
  attackers down. Use them to stay ahead in a race, and keep Alela and the best payoffs back.

## Key lines

- **Turn 3 Alela** off Sol Ring, Mind Stone or Dark Ritual, then a cheap artifact or enchantment every turn.
- **Skullclamp + Faeries:** every Faerie is {1} for two cards; Fabricate finds the Clamp.
- **Removal that makes a Faerie:** Arrest, Luminous Bonds, Plan for All Outcomes on the biggest threat.
- **Wispdrinker Vampire + a Faerie stream:** each Faerie that enters drains the table for 1.
- **Serra's +2 on the big attack:** the Faeries become 3/2 fliers for the turn; Shatterwing Pegasus adds more.
- **Wall of Junk loop:** block, return to hand, recast for {2}, get a Faerie.
- **Crystal Chimes refill:** once several enchantments are in the graveyard, get them all back and recast them for a
  Faerie each.

**Mulligan:** keep 3+ lands and at least one cheap artifact or enchantment; land Alela on turn 3 or 4.

## Mana and consistency

34 lands: 6 nonbasic and 28 basic (11 Plains, 9 Island, 8 Swamp). Mana sources besides lands: **Sol Ring**, **Mind
Stone** and **Dark Ritual**; **Burnished Hart** fetches two basics.

- **White-black duals:** Isolated Chapel (enters tapped unless you control a Plains or a Swamp) and Meticulous Commons
  (enters tapped unless you control a planeswalker).
- **Blue-black:** Theorix Annex (enters tapped unless you control a planeswalker). Venser, Serra and the Jace token from
  Plan for All Outcomes are the planeswalkers.
- **Three-colour fixing:** Path of Ancestry (enters tapped; mana in your commander's colours) and Hidden Grotto ({1},
  {T} for any colour, with surveil 1).
- **Ghost Quarter** is the utility land: it destroys a land, and is useful against nonbasic-heavy decks or
  utility lands.

**Card draw:** Skullclamp, Night's Whisper, Inspiring Overseer, Notion Thief, Dockside Chef, Mind Stone, Credit Voucher
(a hand refresh), and the Auras Feather of Flight and Unquestioned Authority (each draws a card when it enters).
**Fabricate** tutors for an artifact.

**Interaction:** fourteen removal spells and creatures, two bounce effects (Unsummon, Angelic Shield), and five counters
(Essence Scatter, Negate, Statute of Denial, Muddle the Mixture, Judge's Familiar). The deck has 29 creatures.

## Bracket and Rule 0

**Bracket 3,** with one Game Changer: **Notion Thief**. No two-card infinite combos, no mass land destruction, no extra
turns. Skullclamp is a strong draw engine with the Faeries, which is worth mentioning before the game.
