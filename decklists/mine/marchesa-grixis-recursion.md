# Marchesa, the Black Rose — Grixis Sacrifice and Recursion

2026-09-27
**Built 2026-09-27** as a stand-alone deck from the binder and bulk lists only (plus basics). Marchesa is the only purchased card. No cards are shared with Sephiroth, Sauron, or Veyran.

## Strategy

Put +1/+1 counters on your creatures, sacrifice them for value, and let Marchesa bring them back every end step. The table runs out of creatures, cards, and life before you run out of bodies.

Marchesa, the Black Rose ({1}{U}{B}{R}, 3/3) reads: dethrone; other creatures you control have dethrone; whenever a creature you control with a +1/+1 counter on it dies, return that card to the battlefield under your control at the beginning of the next end step.

**Getting counters.** Dethrone adds a counter whenever a creature attacks the player with the most life (or tied for most). Triskelion enters with three. Deepglow Skate doubles every counter on your board, and Thrummingbird and Tezzeret's Gambit proliferate.

**Sacrifice outlets.** Carrion Feeder (free, and grows itself), Skullport Merchant, Deadly Dispute, and Lethal Throwdown. Al Bhed Salvagers drains an opponent for 1 on every death.

**Recursion targets.**
- **Triskelion:** remove two counters for 2 damage, sacrifice it with one left, and it returns with three.
- **Accursed Marauder:** each player sacrifices a nontoken creature every time it enters.
- **Sephiroth, Planet's Heir:** −2/−2 to opponents' creatures on every return, and it gains counters as they die.
- **Burglar Rat, Phyrexian Rager, Forge Devil, Crackling Drake:** discard, cards, pings, and draws on repeat.
- **Phyrexian Delver:** reanimates another creature each time it returns.

**Card advantage.** Rhystic Study, Mystic Remora, and Necropotence keep the hand full; Notion Thief takes opponents' extra draws.

## Key lines

**Triskelion loop.** With Carrion Feeder or Skullport Merchant out: remove two counters (2 damage), sacrifice with one counter left, return at end step with three. About 2 damage plus an Al Bhed drain per turn cycle, and it doubles as repeatable creature removal.

**Marauder loop.** Accursed Marauder makes each player sacrifice a nontoken creature when it enters. Give it a counter (dethrone attack, Thrummingbird, Tezzeret's Gambit), sacrifice it, and it returns at end step for another edict. Returned creatures come back without counters, so each loop needs a fresh counter — Triskelion is the exception, since it enters with three.

**One-sided wipe.** Crux of Fate (non-Dragon) when your key creatures have counters: everything dies, your countered creatures return at end step.

**Theft.** Act of Treason plus any sac outlet permanently removes an opponent's best creature. Enslave steals one outright.

**Sequencing.** Land a sac outlet before Marchesa. Attack the life leader early to spread dethrone counters. Hold Lightning Greaves or Champion's Helm for Marchesa once the recursion engine is running.

## Consistency

The list is exactly 100 cards. 36 lands (16 nonbasic, 9 Swamp, 6 Island, 5 Mountain) plus five rocks: Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Chromatic Lantern, Coalition Relic, Relic of Legends. Seize the Spoils, Deadly Dispute, and Skullport Merchant add Treasure.

**Interaction.** Fifteen removal spells (Go for the Throat, Terror, Last Gasp, Lightning Bolt, Chaos Warp, Sephiroth's Intervention, Disintegrate, Scorching Dragonfire, Lightning Strike, Orcish Cannonade, Void Snare, Disembowel, Soul Enervation, Premature Burial, Crux of Fate), plus Accursed Marauder and Sephiroth as repeatable removal. Two counters: Essence Scatter, Disdainful Stroke.

**Reanimation.** Zombify, Grave Researcher // Reanimate, Evil Reawakened, Unearth, Phyrexian Delver, Nim Deathmantle.

**No tutors.** Consistency comes from card draw volume rather than search.

## Bracket and Rule 0

**Bracket 3.** Three Game Changers: **Rhystic Study**, **Necropotence**, and **Notion Thief**. No infinite combos and no two-card win.

Disclose before the game:

- **Rhystic Study**, **Necropotence**, and **Notion Thief**.
- **Marchesa returns creatures that die with +1/+1 counters**, so board wipes are much weaker against this deck.
- **Accursed Marauder** can repeat its edict every turn.
- **Hellkite Tyrant** steals artifacts on combat damage and has an alternate win with twenty artifacts.

Re-check the official list at https://commanderbrackets.com/faq before an event.

## Flags and tuning levers

**Necropotence exiles discards.** Cards pitched to hand size never reach the graveyard, so they are lost to Unearth, Zombify, and Marchesa's recursion.

**Graveyard hate is the main threat.** Rest in Peace, Bojuka Bog, or Scavenger Grounds shuts off the recursion. Keep Chaos Warp and Void Snare for Rest in Peace-style permanents.

**Weakest slots.** Balustrade Spy, Festering Goblin, and Orcish Cannonade.

**Unused pool options.** Mind Sculpt, Brain Freeze, Grapeshot, Rummaging Goblin, Colossus Hammer, and Mask of Memory are in the binder/bulk lists if you want to test swaps.

**Card pool.** Every card comes from binder.txt or bulk.txt. Where a card is in both files (Fabled Passage, Evil Reawakened, Last Gasp), the binder copy is used.
