# Zur the Enchanter — Esper Auras

2026-10-02
**Added 2026-10-02.** Not in the simulator yet (the simulator's decks are Sephiroth, Veyran, Sauron and Marchesa). This is a different list from the pool's Tier 5 Zur deck (`decklists/pool/t5-high-b4/zur-esper-enchantment-control.md`).

## Strategy

Get Zur down, keep it alive, and attack with it every turn. Each attack puts the right enchantment straight onto the battlefield: a card engine early, an Aura that locks down the biggest threat in the middle of the game, and Auras that turn Zur into a 21-damage commander once the table is under control.

Zur the Enchanter ({1}{W}{U}{B}, 1/4, flying) reads: *whenever Zur attacks, you may search your library for an enchantment card with mana value 3 or less, put it onto the battlefield, then shuffle.*

**Zur is the deck's only tutor, so attack with it.** Sixteen of the seventeen enchantments cost 3 or less (Prayer of Binding, at 4, is the exception). A 1/4 flier rarely dies attacking, and the trigger resolves before blockers, so a fetched Aura is already on Zur when damage is dealt.

**A fetched Aura isn't cast and doesn't target.** You choose what it enchants as it enters, so hexproof, shroud and ward don't stop it, and neither do counterspells. Protection still does. That makes Arrest, Prison Sentence, Luminous Bonds and Bound in Silence answers to creatures your removal spells can't touch. It also means Lightning Greaves' shroud on Zur doesn't block its own fetches.

**Card engines first.** Rhystic Study, Necropotence and Mystic Remora are all fetchable, and Zur puts them in without paying for them. That matters most for **Necropotence**: {B}{B}{B} is a hard cast with five Swamps, and free off Zur. Esper Sentinel and Notion Thief tax the table as well, and Night's Whisper, Read the Bones, Divination, Deep Analysis and Tezzeret's Gambit refill the hand.

**Removal on a stick.**
- **Arrest and Prison Sentence:** the creature can't attack, block or use activated abilities. Prison Sentence also scries 2.
- **Luminous Bonds and Bound in Silence:** the creature can't attack or block.
- **Encrust:** the artifact or creature doesn't untap and loses its activated abilities, so it answers mana rocks and equipment-style artifacts too.
- **Kasmina's Transmutation:** the creature loses all abilities and becomes a 1/1. It's the answer to a commander whose static or triggered ability is the problem.
- **Phyrexian Boon:** -1/-2 on a non-black creature, or +2/+1 on Zur.

**Then make Zur lethal.** Zur is white, blue *and* black, so the black-only Auras work on it.
- **Ethereal Armor:** +1/+1 for each enchantment you control, and first strike.
- **All That Glitters:** +1/+1 for each artifact and enchantment you control.
- **Demonic Embrace:** +3/+1. You can recast it from the graveyard for 3 life and a discard.
- **Phyrexian Boon:** +2/+1, since Zur is black.
- **Duelist's Heritage:** gives an attacker double strike.
- **Bastion Protector:** your commanders get +2/+2 and indestructible.
- **Spirit Link:** you gain life equal to Zur's damage, which pays back Necropotence.

Twenty-one commander damage kills, so a double-striking Zur needs only 11 power.

**Protect the investment.** Every Aura on Zur is lost if Zur goes, so the deck carries:
- **Lightning Greaves:** shroud and haste.
- **Gift of Immortality:** Zur comes back when it dies, and the Aura comes back with it at the next end step. Let Zur go to the graveyard rather than the command zone for this to work.
- **Clever Concealment:** phase out Zur and everything attached to it in response to a wipe.
- **Rootborn Defenses:** indestructible for your creatures.
- **Bastion Protector:** indestructible for Zur all the time.

## Key lines

**The opening.** Turn 2 Sol Ring, Arcane Signet or Talisman of Progress, then Zur on turn 3. With Lightning Greaves out, Zur attacks the turn it lands. The first fetch is **Rhystic Study** at a full table, or **Necropotence** when your life total is healthy and nobody is pressuring you.

**The one-hit kill.**
- **Setup:** Duelist's Heritage on the battlefield *before* the attack, since its trigger is "whenever one or more creatures attack" and a copy fetched mid-attack has missed it. Zur wears Ethereal Armor and All That Glitters, with one other enchantment and two artifacts out.
- **The result:** Zur is 11/14 with double strike, 22 commander damage in a single combat. Zur's own trigger can fetch the last Aura during that attack.

**Wipe on your terms.**
- **Austere Command:** choose "creatures with mana value 3 or less" and "artifacts". Zur (mana value 4) and your Auras survive.
- **Crux of Fate and Vanquish the Horde:** these kill Zur. Pair them with **Bastion Protector** or **Rootborn Defenses** (indestructible), or with **Clever Concealment**.
- **The Eternal Wanderer's −4:** every player keeps one creature, and yours is Zur.

**Blink for value, not on Zur.** Momentary Blink (with flashback) and Restoration Angel replay Accursed Marauder for another edict, or Skyclave Apparition for another exile. Blinking Zur sends every Aura on it to the graveyard, so only do it to save Zur from an exile effect when it has nothing attached.

**Notion Thief + Deep Analysis.** Deep Analysis says "target player draws two cards". Target an opponent while Notion Thief is out, and you draw them instead, twice with flashback.

**Sequencing.**
- **Rocks before Zur:** a turn-3 Zur needs a rock on turn 2.
- **Greaves before Zur:** or with it, so Zur attacks at once.
- **Engines before Voltron:** take the card engines first, removal Auras when a threat appears, and the power Auras last, when you can finish within a turn or two. An early Voltron Zur only invites removal.

## Zur's fetch by board state

Zur can fetch any enchantment with mana value 3 or less: everything but Prayer of Binding.

- **Early, a full table → Rhystic Study.** With a healthy life total and no pressure → **Necropotence** (free here, despite its {B}{B}{B}). **Mystic Remora** only in the first few turns, then let it go to its upkeep.
- **A creature that must stop attacking or tapping → Arrest or Prison Sentence.** If it only needs to stop attacking and blocking → **Luminous Bonds** or **Bound in Silence**.
- **A creature whose abilities are the problem (a commander's static or trigger) → Kasmina's Transmutation.**
- **An artifact or a creature with a tap ability → Encrust.**
- **Hexproof or shroud on the threat → any of the above.** Fetched Auras don't target.
- **Zur is the removal magnet → Gift of Immortality on Zur.**
- **Setting up next turn's kill → Duelist's Heritage**, then the power Auras on the following attacks.
- **Closing this turn → Ethereal Armor or All That Glitters** (whichever counts more), else **Demonic Embrace** or **Phyrexian Boon**.
- **Racing an aggressive deck, or paying back Necropotence → Spirit Link on Zur.**

## Bracket and Rule 0

**Bracket 3,** at the cap with three Game Changers: **Rhystic Study**, **Necropotence** and **Notion Thief**. A fourth moves the deck to Bracket 4. Mystic Remora and Esper Sentinel are off-list. There are no infinite combos, no mass land destruction and no extra turns.

Disclose before the game:

- **Rhystic Study**, **Necropotence** and **Notion Thief** (the three Game Changers).
- **Zur tutors an enchantment onto the battlefield every time it attacks**, and those Auras ignore hexproof.
- **Commander damage is the main win:** a suited-up, double-striking Zur kills in one hit.
- **Aven Mindcensor** limits opponents' library searches to the top four cards.

Re-check the official list at https://commanderbrackets.com/faq before an event.

## Flags and tuning levers

**Tapped lands are the biggest cost.** Eleven always enter tapped, and four more often do. Untapped duals in place of the Guildgate, the Vivid lands or Arcane Sanctum would make a turn-3 Zur far more reliable. Options: Drowned Catacomb (the blue-black check land, next to Glacial Fortress and Isolated Chapel), or Hallowed Fountain, Watery Grave and Godless Shrine.

**Every Aura on Zur is a two- or three-for-one waiting to happen.** Keep Clever Concealment or Rootborn Defenses up on the turn you suit Zur up, and hold the power Auras until you can win soon after.

**Lightning Greaves blocks your own Auras cast from hand.** Shroud stops you targeting Zur. Move Greaves to another creature first (equip {0}), or fetch the Aura with Zur's trigger instead, which doesn't target.

**Life is a resource here.** Necropotence plus Anguished Unmaking, Night's Whisper, Read the Bones, Deep Analysis's flashback, Tezzeret's Gambit's Phyrexian mana, Spire of Industry and Talisman of Progress all cost life. Spirit Link on Zur and Scoured Barrens are the only gains, apart from Prayer of Binding's 2.

**Weakest slots.**
- **Divine Verdict:** only hits attacking or blocking creatures.
- **Cancel:** three mana, against Dovin's Veto at two.
- **Divination:** a plain draw-two.
- **Aven Fisher:** a 2/2 for four.
- **Azorius Guildmage:** both of its abilities are expensive.

Good replacements would be more enchantments with mana value 3 or less, so that Zur's searches have more to choose from.

**Mystic Remora's upkeep grows.** Pay it for a turn or two at most, then let it go.
