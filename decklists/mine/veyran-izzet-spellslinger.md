## Strategy

Chain cheap instants and sorceries, let Veyran make every cast-trigger fire twice, and convert those doubled triggers into damage, fliers, or cards.

Veyran ({1}{U}{R}, 2/2) reads: magecraft gives it +1/+1 until end of turn, and *if you casting or copying an instant or sorcery causes a triggered ability of a permanent you control to trigger, that ability triggers an additional time*.

The scope matters. Veyran doubles magecraft and "whenever you cast an instant or sorcery" abilities. It does **not** double attack triggers, combat-damage triggers, or ETB triggers — so the Emeritus attack triggers, and Lightning's damage trigger all fire once.

| Payoff | Per spell | With Veyran | Wizard/Shaman (Harmonic Prodigy too) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Guttersnipe | 2 damage to each opponent | 4 | 6 |
| Thunderdrum Soloist | 1 damage each opponent (3 if 5+ mana spent) | 2 (6) | no |
| Ral, Storm Conduit | 1 damage to a target on cast | 2 | no (planeswalker) |
| Aetherflux Reservoir | gain life = spells cast this turn | that lifegain fires twice | no (artifact) |
| Archmage Emeritus | draw 1 | draw 2 | draw 3 |
| Murmuring Mystic | one 1/1 flying Bird | 2 Birds | 3 Birds |
| Rite of the Dragoncaller | one 5/5 flying Dragon | 2 Dragons | no |
| Eris, Roar of the Storm | one 4/4 flying prowess Dragon on your 2nd spell | 2 Dragons | no |
| Niv-Mizzet, the Firemind | 1 damage to any target whenever you draw (it also taps to draw) | pings on every extra draw | no |
| Thousand-Year Storm | copy the spell once per instant/sorcery cast before it this turn | copies twice as many times | no (enchantment) |

Thor, Asgard's Avenger adds 1 to every instance of damage from another source you control. With Veyran it turns each Guttersnipe trigger into 3 and each Thunderdrum trigger into 2, so a three-spell turn kills a 40-life table faster than the raw numbers suggest.

**Ral, Storm Conduit** is the third pinger. He deals 1 to any target on every instant or sorcery you cast (2 under Veyran), and he gains loyalty whenever you copy or double a spell, so he protects and grows himself.

**Thousand-Year Storm** is the new multiplier. Its trigger is a "whenever you cast an instant or sorcery" ability on a permanent you control, so Veyran doubles it: the third spell of a turn gets four copies instead of two. Each copy triggers magecraft again (Archmage Emeritus, Harmonic Prodigy, Veyran's own pump), though copies aren't *cast*, so Guttersnipe and Aetherflux still count only real casts.

**Aetherflux Reservoir** is the burst finisher. Its lifegain is a "whenever you cast a spell" trigger on a permanent you control, so Veyran doubles it on every instant or sorcery: each cast gains life equal to the current spell count, twice. A dense turn crosses 50 life quickly, at which point pay 50, deal 50 to a player.

**Ways to close.** Burn the table with Guttersnipe, Thunderdrum Soloist, and Ral over two or three spell-dense turns. Build an air force with Rite of the Dragoncaller and Eris, then swing. Point Crackle with Power at a player after a Seething Song burst. Or build a long spell turn under Thousand-Year Storm and cross 50 life with Aetherflux for a 50-point snipe.

Eris is the reward for a full graveyard: it costs {2} less per *different* mana value among instants and sorceries there, so a yard with four mana values makes it a 2-mana 4/4 flier.

## Consistency

The list is exactly 100 cards.

34 lands (19 nonbasic, 9 Island, 6 Mountain) plus four rocks: Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone, Thought Vessel. (Aetherflux Reservoir is an artifact but not a mana source.)

| Measure | Count | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blue lands | 24 | 9 Island plus the duals (incl. Steam Vents, Spirebluff Canal, Stormcarved Coast), Command Tower, Exotic Orchard, Path of Ancestry |
| Red lands | 20 | 6 Mountain plus the duals (incl. Steam Vents, Spirebluff Canal, Stormcarved Coast) and Sokenzan |
| Colorless-only lands | 0 | — |
| No mana the turn they land | 3 | Ash Barrens, Evolving Wilds, Terramorphic Expanse |
| Usually enter tapped | 5 | Coastal Peak, Temple of Epiphany, Path of Ancestry, Scorched Geyser, Mistrise Village (Stormcarved Coast only on turns one and two) |

Red is still the tighter color, but 20 red lands is healthier than the old 18 for Thor ({2}{R}{R}) and for a Crackle with Power big enough to matter. Two-mana rocks are thin — only Arcane Signet and Fellwar Stone fix on turn two — so the untapped duals carry early fixing, and Spirebluff Canal joins Steam Vents as a source that reliably enters untapped in the opening turns.

**Spell density.** 36 instants and sorceries, plus four prepare cards whose backs are castable spells: Emeritus of Ideation // Ancestral Recall, Emeritus of Conflict // Lightning Bolt, Blazing Firesinger // Seething Song, Sanar // Wild Idea. Each copy is a separate cast, so each triggers magecraft again.

**Finding cards.** Repeatable draw bodies (Archmage Emeritus, Hydro-Channeler, and Niv-Mizzet, Parun, which draws whenever anyone casts an instant or sorcery) and a deep cantrip suite: Visions of Beyond, Think Twice, Deduce, Quick Study, Dreams of Laguna, Light Up the Stage, Expressive Iteration, Flow State, Stock Up. Plunder the Trollshaws is the best of them here, since flashback turns one card into two casts. Visions of Beyond is a one-mana cantrip early and draws three once any graveyard reaches 20 cards, which in a four-player game with a reanimator at the table happens sooner than you'd think. Old Fat Spider Can't See Me gives Veyran hexproof on chapter I, then two cards on III and IV.

**Interaction.** Eight ways to answer a spell (Counterspell, Force of Will, Spell Pierce, An Offer You Can't Refuse, Disdainful Stroke, Mystic Confluence, Return the Favor, Venser), nine pieces of spot removal, and three resets in Blasphemous Act, River's Rebuke, and Cyclonic Rift. Every one is also a magecraft trigger, which is why holding up interaction costs this deck no tempo. Force of Will is the free one — it protects your commander or your combo turn even fully tapped out, and it's live whether or not Veyran is on the board.

**Redundancy gap, narrowed.** Three recurring damage-on-cast sources (Guttersnipe, Thunderdrum Soloist, Ral, Storm Conduit) and one graveyard rebuy (Mizzix's Mastery). Losing one pinger no longer turns the burn plan off.

**Fragility, partly fixed.** Veyran is still a 2/2 with no evasion, but **Swiftfoot Boots** now gives it hexproof and haste, so targeted removal no longer kills it on sight and it can attack for magecraft the turn it lands. Boots don't stop sweepers or edicts. Hold Force of Will, Return the Favor, or a Mistrise Village activation for the rest; Old Fat Spider's chapter I is a second hexproof window.

## Key lines

**There is no infinite anymore.** Displacer Kitten left for Sephiroth, so Blazing Firesinger is now just a ritual on a body: it enters prepared and you may cast one copy of Seething Song for a net {R}{R} — a good way to fund a spell-dense turn, not a loop.

**Thousand-Year Storm turn.** Land Storm, then chain cheap spells on the next turn with Veyran out: spell one gets no copies, spell two gets two, spell three gets four, and so on. Point the copies at burn (Lightning Bolt, Burst Lightning, Abrade) for direct damage or at cantrips to refill. With Guttersnipe or Thunderdrum Soloist out, the casts still trigger them, and the copies still trigger Archmage Emeritus. With Aetherflux Reservoir out, a six- or seven-spell Storm turn gets you most of the way to 50 life on its own.

**Aetherflux as a snipe.** Every cast under Veyran gains life twice — equal to the running spell count. A long turn crosses 50; pay 50 and deal 50 to the most dangerous player. It's no longer an infinite, so pick the target who's ahead.

**Niv-Mizzet is the other big finisher.** Every instant or sorcery you cast draws a card (twice with Veyran), and every card you draw deals 1 damage to any target, so a spell turn becomes a stream of pings. It can't be countered.

**Mizzix's Mastery** on River's Rebuke or a large Crackle with Power is the single-card blowout. 

**Sequencing.** Deploy pingers and token engines before Veyran, not after. Veyran costs three, dies to anything, and only doubles what is already on the board. Ral, Storm Conduit and Aetherflux Reservoir both want to be down before the spell-dense turn. Emeritus of Ideation wants to exile eight cards from your graveyard on attack, which competes with Eris and Mizzix's Mastery for the same yard, so feed it lands and spent cantrips.

## Tutor targets by board state

Four tutors, each pointing somewhere different, plus one graveyard rebuy. **Mystical Tutor and Solve the Equation only find instants and sorceries**, **Fabricate** finds an artifact (or makes a 1/1 Servo), and **Imperial Recruiter** finds a creature with power 2 or less.

- **Need the burn engine → Imperial Recruiter for Guttersnipe, Kessig Flamebreather, or Thunderdrum Soloist** (all power 2 or less), or for Blazing Firesinger when you want the mana burst.
- **Want a finisher on board before the big turn → Fabricate for Aetherflux Reservoir.**
- **Veyran keeps dying → Fabricate for Swiftfoot Boots.**
- **Need to protect the turn → Solve the Equation for a counter** (to hand — Counterspell, Return the Favor, Disdainful Stroke), or **Mystical Tutor at an opponent's end step**. Force of Will you'd rather already hold, since it's free.
- **Need reach to close → Mystical Tutor or Solve the Equation for Crackle with Power** after a mana burst, or **Mizzix's Mastery** to recast River's Rebuke or a big Crackle out of the graveyard.
- **Need to dig / rebuild the hand → a draw spell** (Stock Up, Dreams of Laguna) via either instant/sorcery tutor.
- **Mana-screwed → Fabricate for Sol Ring or Thought Vessel.**

Thousand-Year Storm is an enchantment, so none of the tutors can reach it; it's a card you draw into.

Timing note: **Mystical Tutor puts the card on top of your library** — fire it at the end of the turn before you go off, or convert it with a Mystic Sanctuary shuffle or an Archmage Emeritus draw. **Solve the Equation goes to hand**, so it's the better pick when you intend to cast the card this turn.

## Bracket and Rule 0

Bracket 3, at the cap with **three** Game Changers: **Mystical Tutor**, **Cyclonic Rift**, and **Force of Will**. A fourth moves the deck to Bracket 4. Ral, Storm Conduit, Aetherflux Reservoir, Thousand-Year Storm, and Swiftfoot Boots are all off-list.

The deck no longer contains a two-card combo: the Displacer Kitten + Blazing Firesinger infinite left with the Kitten. That makes Veyran the one fully fair deck of the three.

Disclose before the game:

- **Aetherflux Reservoir** as a wincon — a long spell turn under Veyran crosses 50 life and snipes a player for 50.
- **Thousand-Year Storm**, since doubled copies make single turns much bigger than the board suggests.
- **Cyclonic Rift**, **Force of Will** (a free counterspell), and **Jin-Gitaxias, Progress Tyrant**, which taxes or answers the first spell each opponent casts.
- **Niv-Mizzet** turns, where every spell draws and every draw pings.

Re-check the official list at https://commanderbrackets.com/faq before an event.

## Flags and tuning levers

**The 09-21 swap — 1 out, 1 in (both lands).** Terminal Moraine → Spirebluff Canal. This is the "Terminal Moraine for a third untapped dual" upgrade the old notes flagged, now taken: a colorless land that entered slow and sacked for a basic becomes an Izzet fast land that enters untapped through the early turns and taps for both colours. Red moves to 19, colorless-only lands drop to one, land count holds at 34. Not a Game Changer.

**The 09-25 swaps — 3 out, 3 in.**

| Out | In | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Displacer Kitten | Swiftfoot Boots | Ends the Firesinger infinite (Kitten moved to Sephiroth); the commander gets hexproof and haste instead. |
| Disruptor Flute | Thousand-Year Storm | The flagged softest slot out; a spell-copy engine that Veyran doubles in. |
| Sleight of Hand | Visions of Beyond | Cantrip for cantrip; Visions can draw three late. |

**Sim status.** Not re-run on this list. The earlier Veyran numbers included the Kitten combo, so they don't describe this version.

**Weakest remaining slot**, if you want to keep tuning: the mana base has no obvious weak land left. On the spell side, Blazing Firesinger is now the softest — without the Kitten it's a one-shot ritual on a 2-power body. Kessig Flamebreather and Imperial Recruiter are the next to look at.

**Game Changers — at the cap.** Three: Mystical Tutor, Cyclonic Rift, Force of Will. A fourth moves the deck to Bracket 4. Force of Will earns the slot over Fierce Guardianship (free only while you control your commander — shaky on a 2/2 that dies constantly) and Rhystic Study (generic flow that doesn't touch the magecraft engine); Force of Will is free unconditionally and doesn't need Veyran alive.

**Rules notes carried forward.** Veyran has no first strike, and magecraft gives +1/+1 until end of turn, not permanent counters. The doubling applies only when your casting or copying an instant or sorcery is what caused the trigger.

**Steam Vents, Coastal Peak, and Scorched Geyser** are all Island Mountain duals with red-blue identity, so all three are clean Izzet fixing and all three turn on Sulfur Falls. Steam Vents is the best — untapped whenever you'll pay 2 life. Coastal Peak always enters tapped and cycles for {2}; Scorched Geyser enters untapped once you control two basics.

**Graveyard tension resolved.** Scavenger Grounds was the only card that exiled your own graveyard; with it gone, Eris, Mizzix's Mastery, and Plunder the Trollshaws flashback no longer have an in-deck enemy.

**Card conflicts with your other decks** (Sephiroth and Sauron; Najeela is no longer tracked).
- Staples in all three decks: Arcane Signet, Command Tower, Exotic Orchard, Sol Ring.
- Shared with **Sauron**: Blasphemous Act, Chaos Warp, Counterspell, Cyclonic Rift, Path of Ancestry, Shivan Reef, Steam Vents, Sulfur Falls.
- Shared with **Sephiroth**: Ash Barrens, Evolving Wilds.
- **Moved to Sephiroth**: Displacer Kitten. **Moved in from Sephiroth**: Swiftfoot Boots.
- **To the binder this round**: Disruptor Flute, Sleight of Hand.

**If you want red to be more reliable,** Spirebluff Canal and Stormcarved Coast have bumped it to 20. The next step is bringing back Izzet Signet as a two-mana rock that also fixes red.
