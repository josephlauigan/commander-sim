## Strategy

Chain cheap instants and sorceries, let Veyran make every cast-trigger fire twice, and convert those doubled triggers into damage, cards, Treasure, and creature tokens.

Veyran ({1}{U}{R}, 2/2) reads: magecraft gives it +1/+1 until end of turn, and *if you casting or copying an instant or sorcery causes a triggered ability of a permanent you control to trigger, that ability triggers an additional time*.

The scope matters. Veyran doubles magecraft and "whenever you cast an instant or sorcery" abilities. It does **not** double attack triggers, combat-damage triggers, ETB triggers, or draw triggers — so the Emeritus attack triggers and the Niv-Mizzets' "whenever you draw" pings fire once per draw.

**Pingers.** Guttersnipe and **Kessig Flamebreather** each hit every opponent when you cast, and Veyran doubles both. Ral, Storm Conduit adds another. Both pingers are Shamans, so Harmonic Prodigy adds a third trigger.

**Spells make creatures.** Four token makers trigger when you cast an instant or sorcery, and Veyran doubles each one:

- **Young Pyromancer** ({1}{R}): a 1/1 red Elemental. It's a Shaman, so Harmonic Prodigy adds a third token.
- **Third Path Iconoclast** ({U}{R}): a 1/1 Soldier artifact creature. It triggers on any noncreature spell, but only instants and sorceries are doubled.
- **Talrand, Sky Summoner** ({2}{U}{U}): a 2/2 flying Drake. It's a Wizard, so Harmonic Prodigy adds a third.
- **Docent of Perfection** ({3}{U}{U}, 5/4 flier): a 1/1 Wizard. Once you control three Wizards it transforms into **Final Iteration**, which gives your Wizards +2/+1 and flying, so the Wizard tokens become 3/2 fliers.

With one of these out, a four-spell turn leaves eight tokens. Unlike the commander, the token makers keep working when Veyran is gone, just at half strength, so losing Veyran no longer stops the deck. On turns you can't go off, cheap spells build a board you can attack with.

**Storm-Kiln Artist pays for the chain.** Its magecraft makes a Treasure whenever you cast or copy an instant or sorcery. Veyran doubles it, and it's a Shaman, so Harmonic Prodigy adds a third. It gets +1/+0 for each artifact you control; Treasures and Iconoclast's Soldiers both count. Because it's magecraft, it also triggers on copies from Thousand-Year Storm and Alania.

**Fiery Emancipation triples everything.** If a source you control would deal damage, it deals triple instead. With Veyran out, a Guttersnipe trigger pair becomes 12 damage to each opponent per spell. Every 1/1 token attacks for 3, a Drake for 6, and Crackle with Power and Aetherflux Reservoir hit three times as hard. It costs six ({3}{R}{R}{R}), so it usually lands the turn before the big turn. It is not a Game Changer.

**The Niv-Mizzets turn cards into damage.** Niv-Mizzet, Parun draws a card whenever *any* player casts an instant or sorcery (your casts doubled by Veyran), and pings for every card you draw. Niv-Mizzet, the Firemind pings on every draw too and taps to draw. With both out, each card you draw is two pings, and **Harmonic Prodigy doubles both of them again** — both Niv-Mizzets are Wizards. Archmage Emeritus draws on every magecraft trigger, so one spell with Parun, Emeritus, and Veyran out can draw four and ping eight.

**Thousand-Year Storm** copies each instant or sorcery once for every other one you've cast before it this turn. Copies trigger magecraft (and Veyran doubles those) but aren't casts, so they don't trigger Guttersnipe, Kessig, Parun, or the token makers. Storm-Kiln Artist's magecraft does see them. The fourth spell of a turn becomes four spells' worth of magecraft.

**Alania, Divergent Storm copies the first instant and the first sorcery you cast each turn** (yours and opponents' turns alike), if you have a target opponent draw a card for each copy. Each copy is optional, so take only the ones worth a card. Casting the spell is what triggers Alania, so **Veyran makes it trigger twice**, and Alania is a Wizard, so **Harmonic Prodigy adds a third**: with both out, your first instant and first sorcery each resolve up to four times. Like Thousand-Year Storm's, the copies trigger magecraft (Archmage Emeritus draws, Veyran grows) but aren't casts, so Guttersnipe, Kessig, Parun and the token makers see only the original, while Storm-Kiln Artist makes a Treasure for each copy. The best first spells: **Jeska's Will** (hand the cards to the opponent you'll count for mana, and each copy makes more), Ancestral Recall, Expressive Iteration, Seething Song (each copy is another five red), Crackle with Power (copies keep X), an overloaded Cyclonic Rift, and Lightning Bolt. A copied counterspell is rarely worth a card. Spells cast before Alania landed still count: if your first instant this turn came before Alania, the next one isn't copied.

**Protecting Veyran.** Three pieces, chosen from simulator testing:

- **Lightning Greaves** gives shroud and haste, and its equip is {0}, so you can move it to a new threat for free.
- **Slip Out the Back** ({U}) puts a +1/+1 counter on Veyran and phases it out, answering a removal spell or a board wipe for one mana.
- **Deflecting Swat** is free while you control a commander. It redirects a removal spell, or a counterspell aimed at your big spell, without tapping mana.

Veyran is still removed about once a game in the simulator, which is the other reason the token makers matter.

Eris is the reward for a full graveyard: it costs {2} less per *different* mana value among instants and sorceries there.

**Jeska's Will fuels the big turn.** With Veyran on the battlefield you get both modes: add {R} for each card in an opponent's hand (target the fullest hand), then exile the top three cards of your library and play them this turn. That's usually four to seven mana and three more spells for one card, and it's itself a sorcery, so it triggers every pinger. It replaces Force of Will's protection with fuel: the deck now wins more big turns by going longer and protects them less.

**Twinflame doubles your engines for a turn.** For {1}{R} it makes a hasty token copy of a creature you control, exiled at the next end step; each extra target costs {2}{R} more. Copy Guttersnipe or Kessig Flamebreather before a chain and every spell after it pings twice as hard (and Veyran doubles the copy's triggers too). Copy Archmage Emeritus for twice the cards, Young Pyromancer or Storm-Kiln Artist for twice the tokens or Treasure, or Venser for a second bounce. It can't usefully copy Veyran, Talrand or the Niv-Mizzets: they're legendary, so a copy dies to the legend rule right away.

## Key lines

**The big turn.** Pingers or token makers down, Veyran protected, Fiery Emancipation out if you have it, then chain. Thousand-Year Storm plus a mid-turn Seething Song (Blazing Firesinger's prepared spell), Jeska's Will, or Mizzix's Mastery on a full yard is the most explosive line the deck has left. Cast Twinflame on your best pinger early in the chain, not late — every spell after it counts double, and the copy is exiled at end of turn anyway.

**Aetherflux Reservoir** gains 1 life for the first spell, 2 for the second, and so on, and pays 50 life to deal 50 to one player. It's no longer tied to an infinite, but a long turn — especially one extended by Thousand-Year Storm copies of cantrips — still gets you there.

**Rite of the Dragoncaller** makes a 5/5 flier per instant or sorcery, doubled by Veyran.

**Going wide.** Without a big turn available, cast cheap spells to build a token board and attack. Under Fiery Emancipation every 1/1 hits for 3. Board wipes are the risk: keep Slip Out the Back for Veyran, not the tokens, and rebuild with the next few spells.

**Mizzix's Mastery** on River's Rebuke or a large Crackle with Power is the single-card blowout.

**Deck-out warning.** Niv-Mizzet, Parun's draw is not optional, and it triggers off opponents' spells too. With Parun, Archmage Emeritus, Veyran, and Harmonic Prodigy all out, a long turn can draw a lot of cards. Watch your library.

**Sequencing.** Deploy pingers, token makers and Storm-Kiln Artist before Veyran and before the chain, not in the middle of it. Put Lightning Greaves down before or with Veyran, not after it has already eaten removal. Ral and Aetherflux both want to be down before the spell-dense turn. Emeritus of Ideation wants to exile eight cards from your graveyard on attack, which competes with Eris and Mizzix's Mastery for the same yard, so feed it lands and spent cantrips.

The list is exactly 100 cards.

34 lands (19 nonbasic, 9 Island, 6 Mountain) plus three rocks: Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone. Storm-Kiln Artist's Treasures add mana once spells start. (Aetherflux Reservoir and Lightning Greaves are artifacts but not mana sources.)

**Curve pressure.** Both Niv-Mizzets and Thousand-Year Storm cost six (Parun is {U}{U}{U}{R}{R}{R}). That's three six-drops where Displacer Kitten, Kylox, and Dualcaster Mage used to be, so the deck is top-heavier than before, and Alania (five mana) replaced a two-drop. Parun's triple-pip cost in both colours is the hardest cast in the list. The 10-04 rebuild adds Talrand and Storm-Kiln Artist (four), Docent of Perfection (five) and Fiery Emancipation (six). The cheap additions are Young Pyromancer and Third Path Iconoclast (two each), and Thought Vessel left, so the deck is down to three rocks.

**Spell density.** 33 instants and sorceries, plus four prepare cards whose backs are castable spells: Emeritus of Ideation // Ancestral Recall, Emeritus of Conflict // Lightning Bolt, Blazing Firesinger // Seething Song, Sanar // Wild Idea.

**Finding cards.** Archmage Emeritus and both Niv-Mizzets are repeatable draw (and Alania's copies of a cantrip draw again); Muse Seeker is gone. Cantrips: Think Twice, Deduce, Quick Study, Dreams of Laguna, Light Up the Stage, Expressive Iteration, Flow State, Stock Up, Plunder the Trollshaws, and **Visions of Beyond** (draws three instead of one once any graveyard has twenty cards).

**Interaction.** The 10-04 rebuild traded interaction for engines: Spell Pierce, Pongify, Reality Shift, Mystic Confluence and Return the Favor are gone. The deck has:

- **Three ways to stop a spell:** Counterspell, An Offer You Can't Refuse and Venser. Deflecting Swat redirects a spell instead (free with Veyran out).
- **Seven pieces of spot removal**, plus **Unsummon**, a one-mana bounce that also saves one of your own creatures.
- **Three resets:** Blasphemous Act, River's Rebuke and Cyclonic Rift.

Every one is also a magecraft trigger. Expect to hold up mana less often and win more by building a board.

**Redundancy.** Three recurring damage-on-cast sources (Guttersnipe, Kessig Flamebreather, Ral), two draw-to-damage Niv-Mizzets, and four token makers. Losing Veyran or any one engine doesn't turn the deck off.

## Tutor targets by board state

Four tutors now, each pointing somewhere different, plus one graveyard rebuy. **Mystical Tutor and Solve the Equation only find instants and sorceries**, **Fabricate finds artifacts**, and **Imperial Recruiter finds a creature with power 2 or less**.

- **Need a damage engine → Imperial Recruiter for Guttersnipe or Kessig Flamebreather.** Or Harmonic Prodigy when a Niv-Mizzet, Talrand or a Shaman engine is already out.
- **Need a board → Imperial Recruiter for Talrand or Young Pyromancer.**
- **Need mana for the chain → Imperial Recruiter for Storm-Kiln Artist.**
- **Need more cards → Imperial Recruiter for Archmage Emeritus.**
- **Veyran keeps dying → Fabricate for Lightning Greaves**, or Mystical Tutor for Slip Out the Back or Deflecting Swat.
- **Setting up a finish → Fabricate for Aetherflux Reservoir** before the long turn.
- **Need to protect a big turn → Solve the Equation for a counter**, or **Mystical Tutor at an opponent's end step**.
- **Need fuel for the big turn → Mystical Tutor or Solve the Equation for Jeska's Will** (with Veyran out, both modes).
- **Need reach to close → Mystical Tutor or Solve the Equation for Crackle with Power**, or **Mizzix's Mastery** to recast River's Rebuke or a big Crackle from the yard.
- **Mana-screwed → Fabricate for Sol Ring or Fellwar Stone.**

Timing note: **Mystical Tutor puts the card on top of your library**, so fire it at the end of the turn before you go off. **Solve the Equation goes to hand**, so it's the better pick when you intend to cast the card this turn. Venser can bounce Imperial Recruiter to your hand for a second search.

## Bracket and Rule 0

Bracket 3, at the cap with **three** Game Changers: **Mystical Tutor**, **Cyclonic Rift**, and **Jeska's Will**. A fourth moves the deck to Bracket 4. Imperial Recruiter, Thousand-Year Storm, both Niv-Mizzets, Ral, Storm Conduit, Aetherflux Reservoir, Twinflame, Fiery Emancipation and Deflecting Swat are all off-list.

**No two-card combo.** With Displacer Kitten gone, the Kitten + Blazing Firesinger infinite is out of the deck. The deck still produces large, non-infinite turns. **Twinflame is half of a known infinite** (with Dualcaster Mage, which left the deck on 09-26), so if Dualcaster Mage ever comes back, the deck has a two-card combo again.

Disclose before the game:

- **Cyclonic Rift**, **Jeska's Will**, and **Mystical Tutor** (the three Game Changers).
- **Aetherflux Reservoir** as a 50-damage finisher after a long turn.
- **Thousand-Year Storm** and the pinger suite, which can turn one turn into a lot of damage to each opponent.
- **Fiery Emancipation**, which triples all of your damage (a Guttersnipe with Veyran out deals 12 to each opponent per spell).
- **Jin-Gitaxias, Progress Tyrant**, which taxes or answers the first spell each opponent casts.

Re-check the official list at https://commanderbrackets.com/faq before an event.
