# Edgar Markov — Mardu Vampires

Opponent pool deck — **Tier 4: Low Bracket 4**. Axis: Vampire tribal aggro.

- **Commander:** Edgar Markov
- **Color identity:** White, Black, Red
- **Game Changers (2):** Bolas's Citadel, Teferi's Protection
- **Combos:** A life-gain drain and a life-loss gain loop for any life change: Sanguine Bond, Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose or Marauding Blight-Priest with Exquisite Blood or Bloodthirsty Conqueror. Bloodletter of Aclazotz doubles every point of it on its own turn.
- **Lands:** 35 (22 nonbasic + 13 basic)

## Role in the pool

Whether your decks survive a wide Vampire board that grows every turn from turn two, and whether they can answer a two-card drain combo that wins from any life change.

## At the table

- **Plan:** Edgar Markov's eminence works from the command zone: every Vampire spell cast makes a 1/1 Vampire, so the board goes wide before Edgar is cast. Cheap Vampires (Knight of the Ebon Legion, Indulgent Aristocrat, Viscera Seer, Vampire Socialite, Cordial Vampire) come down first; Edgar, Charmed Groom and the counter engines make the swarm lethal; cheap burn and removal (Lightning Bolt, Terminate, Heartless Act, Fatal Push, Abrade, Mardu Charm) keep the way clear. Edgar's attack trigger, Drana, Cordial Vampire and Blade of the Bloodchief stack +1/+1 counters.
- **Wins by:** Combat with a pumped, wide board (first strike from Edgar and Drana); the drain loops (Sanguine Bond, Vito or Blight-Priest with Exquisite Blood or Bloodthirsty Conqueror); Blood Artist, Cruel Celebrant and Elenda draining as creatures die; Bloodletter of Aclazotz doubling all of it on its own turn; Malakir Bloodwitch draining in chunks.
- **Watch out for:** Edgar in the command zone: removing him on the battlefield does not stop the tokens. New Blood steals your best creature, and Damn overloaded wipes the board. Bolas's Citadel refills the hand.
- **Interaction:** Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile, Anguished Unmaking, Bloodchief's Thirst, Lightning Bolt, Terminate, Heartless Act, Fatal Push, Abrade, Mardu Charm, Orcish Cannonade, Damn, Ruinous Ultimatum, Bojuka Bog; Teferi's Protection and Boros Charm protect the board. No counterspells; Cavern of Souls makes its Vampires uncounterable.

## Strategy

Cast cheap Vampires while Edgar sits in the command zone, so each spell is two bodies, then cast Edgar (haste, first strike) and attack: every attack puts a counter on every Vampire. The counter engines turn the token swarm into lethal damage by turns six to eight; sacrifice outlets (Viscera Seer, Phyrexian Altar, Indulgent Aristocrat, Village Rites) and the death drains (Blood Artist, Cruel Celebrant) turn a wipe into damage. With no tutors left, the drain loop comes together only when its pieces are drawn.
