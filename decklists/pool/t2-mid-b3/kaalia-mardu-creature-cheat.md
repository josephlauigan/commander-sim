# Kaalia of the Vast — Mardu Angels, Demons & Dragons

Opponent pool deck — **Tier 2: Mid Bracket 3**. Axis: Fast threats / combat.

- **Commander:** Kaalia of the Vast
- **Color identity:** White, Black, Red
- **Game Changers (2):** Smothering Tithe, Demonic Tutor
- **Combos:** None.
- **Lands:** 37 (18 nonbasic + 19 basic)

## Role in the pool

A turn-four seven-drop. A direct race against Sephiroth's reanimation speed, and a check on how much instant-speed removal your decks keep up.

## Strategy

Land Kaalia on turn three, protect her, and start putting a six- or seven-drop Angel, Demon, or Dragon onto the battlefield attacking every turn from turn four. Master of Cruelties, Terror of the Peaks, Balefire Dragon, Archfiend of Despair, and Rune-Scarred Demon are the payoffs that end games or pull tutors. Boots, Greaves, Whispersilk, Mother and Giver of Runes, and Deflecting Swat protect the commander.

## Sim modeling notes

Model these first; if any is Partial or unmodeled, this deck plays below its real strength and its results should be flagged.

Kaalia's put-onto-battlefield-attacking trigger (ETBs still fire; cast triggers don't); commander protection; big flier combat.
