# Kaalia of the Vast — Mardu Angels, Demons & Dragons

Opponent pool deck — **Tier 2: Mid Bracket 3**. Axis: Fast threats / combat.

- **Commander:** Kaalia of the Vast
- **Color identity:** White, Black, Red
- **Game Changers (2):** Demonic Tutor, Teferi's Protection
- **Combos:** None (no two-card infinite combo; Avacyn, Angel of Horror and Liesa, Forgotten Archangel with Razaketh, the Foulblooded are value loops, not infinite).
- **Lands:** 36 (22 nonbasic + 14 basic)

## Role in the pool

A turn-four seven-drop that keeps coming back. A direct race against fast reanimation, and a check on how much instant-speed removal and graveyard hate your decks keep up.

## At the table

- **Plan:** Ramps with Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone, three Signets, three Talismans and Dark Ritual into Kaalia of the Vast, then attacks to put an Angel, Demon or Dragon from hand onto the battlefield attacking. Lightning Greaves, Swiftfoot Boots, Mother of Runes, Grand Abolisher and Teferi's Protection protect her; Kaalia, Zenith Seeker and Demonic Tutor refill the hand with fatties.
- **Wins by:** Big fliers cheated in every turn: Master of Cruelties (an unblocked hit leaves a player at 1), Terror of the Peaks, Balefire Dragon, Twinflame Tyrant and Gisela (doubled damage), Lord of the Void (steals a creature off the top seven), Archfiend of Despair, Rune-Scarred Demon, Hellkite Tyrant and Lyra Dawnbringer. Dragon Tempest and Windcrag Siege add haste and burn; Aurelia and Isshin add combats and attack triggers.
- **Watch out for:** Kaalia can bring a huge threat down on turn four, so kill her on sight or keep a flying blocker and removal ready. Board wipes don't stick: Avacyn, Angel of Horror returns its creatures at the end step and Liesa returns them to hand, while Liesa exiles yours. Angelic Arbiter makes you choose between casting and attacking; Drana and Linvala shuts off your creatures' activated and mana abilities; Serra's Emissary (creature) makes its creatures unblockable by yours and its player immune to combat damage. Valgavoth exiles everything that would hit your graveyard and lets them cast it for life (with Vilis, Broker of Blood drawing for every life paid).
- **Interaction:** Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile, Terminate, Anguished Unmaking, Boros Charm, Blasphemous Act, Damn, Ruinous Ultimatum, Angel of Despair, Archfiend of Depravity, Angel of Serenity and Rakdos, Patron of Chaos (sacrifice two or they draw two). Deflecting Swat, Flawless Maneuver, Akroma's Will and Teferi's Protection protect the board.

## Strategy

Land Kaalia on turn three, protect her, and from turn four put a six- to nine-drop Angel, Demon or Dragon onto the battlefield attacking every turn. Rebuy the threats through wipes with Avacyn, Angel of Horror, Liesa and Reanimate; dig for them with Kaalia, Zenith Seeker, Demonic Tutor, Demonic Counsel and Vilis's life-for-cards. Master of Cruelties, Terror of the Peaks, Twinflame Tyrant with Gisela and Lord of the Void end games.
