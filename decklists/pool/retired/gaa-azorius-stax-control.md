# Grand Arbiter Augustin IV — Azorius Stax

Opponent pool deck — **Tier 4: Low Bracket 4**. Axis: Stax / tax control.

- **Commander:** Grand Arbiter Augustin IV
- **Color identity:** White, Blue
- **Game Changers (7):** Grand Arbiter Augustin IV, Smothering Tithe, Drannith Magistrate, Force of Will, Rhystic Study, Enlightened Tutor, Teferi's Protection
- **Combos:** Isochron Scepter + Dramatic Reversal (unbounded mana with 3+ mana from nonland permanents, reduced by GAA's discount) → Walking Ballista.
- **Lands:** 35 (16 nonbasic + 19 basic)

## Role in the pool

Stax. Commanders taxed or uncastable, one spell per turn, and every spell costing more. Hits Veyran's spellslinging and every commander-centric plan especially hard.

## Strategy

GAA IV makes the pilot's white and blue spells cheaper and everyone else's spells cost one more; Sphere of Resistance, Thorn of Amethyst, Vryn Wingmare, and Glowrider stack the tax, and Rule of Law, Deafening Silence, Archon of Emeria, and Ethersworn Canonist cap opponents at one spell a turn. Drannith Magistrate shuts off casting commanders and casting from graveyards. The kill is Isochron Scepter + Dramatic Reversal for unbounded mana into Walking Ballista.

## Sim modeling notes

Model these first; if any is Partial or unmodeled, this deck plays below its real strength and its results should be flagged.

Static cost increases and one-spell-per-turn limits; Drannith commander/graveyard casting lock; Thalia/Glowrider taxes; Isochron + Reversal infinite mana; counterspells.
