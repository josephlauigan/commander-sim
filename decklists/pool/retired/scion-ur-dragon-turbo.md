# Scion of the Ur-Dragon — Five-Colour cEDH Turbo (v1 baseline)

The v1 consensus list: every card in more than about 68% of EDHTop16 Scion entries (120 entries at 50+ player events,
last year), plus Matthew Robins' 2nd-of-137 list (NJcEDH Sinkhole 2, 2026-07-19) for the mana base and flex slots. It
is the baseline every later version is measured against; the plan and the design decisions are in JD's design
document.

- **Commander:** Scion of the Ur-Dragon
- **Color identity:** White, Blue, Black, Red, Green
- **Game Changers (20):** Ancient Tomb, Chrome Mox, Crop Rotation, Demonic Tutor, Enlightened Tutor, Force of Will, Gamble, Imperial Seal, Intuition, Lion's Eye Diamond, Mana Vault, Mox Diamond, Mystical Tutor, Necropotence, Orcish Bowmasters, Rhystic Study, Smothering Tithe, Thassa's Oracle, Underworld Breach, Vampiric Tutor

## Strategy

Fast mana and rituals into the cEDH finishes: Thassa's Oracle with Demonic Consultation or Tainted Pact, and
Underworld Breach with Lion's Eye Diamond and Brain Freeze. Scion of the Ur-Dragon ({2}: a Dragon from the library
into the graveyard, and Scion becomes a copy of it until end of turn) turns into Prismari, the Inspiration (your
instants and sorceries have storm), Ancient Copper Dragon (Treasures on combat damage) or Korvold, Fae-Cursed King
(sacrifice for cards). Free counters and Silence / Orim's Chant protect the win turn.
