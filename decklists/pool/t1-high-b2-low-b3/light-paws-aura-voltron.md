# Light-Paws — Mono-White Aura Voltron

Opponent pool deck — **Tier 1: High Bracket 2 / Low Bracket 3**. Axis: Voltron (commander damage).

- **Commander:** Light-Paws, Emperor's Voice
- **Color identity:** White
- **Game Changers (0):** none
- **Combos:** None (no two-card infinite; Flickering Ward's {W} return-and-recast is a repeatable Aura tutor with Light-Paws, not a loop).
- **Lands:** 33 (4 nonbasic + 29 basic)

## Role in the pool

Whether your decks carry enough spot removal, instant-speed answers, and chump blockers for a single huge threat. Its protection colours (Mask of Law and Grace, Shield of Duty and Reason, Benevolent Blessing) also test decks whose removal is all one or two colours.

## At the table

- **Plan:** Plays cheap Aura-lovers (Sram, Mesa Enchantress, Kor Spiritdancer, Danitha, Starnheim Courser, Pearl-Ear), then casts an Aura on Light-Paws, Emperor's Voice. Each cast Aura tutors a cheaper Aura onto her, and the cost reducers make the chain cheap; a crowd of plain one- and two-drops (Kor Skyfisher, Whitemane Lion, Glory Seeker and the like) fills the early turns.
- **Wins by:** Stacks Auras on Light-Paws and kills by commander damage: Ethereal Armor, Sage's Reverie and Thran Power Suit scale with what is on her, and Face of Divinity, Angelic Destiny and Feather of Flight add size and keywords. Her only evasion is Feather of Flight, so she needs a clear lane, or Winds of Rath and Single Combat to make one.
- **Watch out for:** Everything rides on one creature, so exile or wipe her in response to the Aura chain. Shardmage's Rescue, Blacksmith's Skill, Karametra's Blessing, Benevolent Blessing and Stoneskin answer spot removal at instant speed, Flickerform dodges removal and wipes (she and her Auras come back at the end step), and Hyena Umbra and Umbra Mystic give umbra armor. Cho-Manno's Blessing gives protection at instant speed. Chains of Custody and Thran Power Suit add ward {2}. Retether rebuilds the Auras after a wipe.
- **Interaction:** Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile, Generous Gift, Darksteel Mutation, Reprobation, Ossification, Sheltered by Ghosts, Chains of Custody, Winds of Rath and Single Combat (each player keeps one creature or planeswalker, then nobody casts creatures until her next turn). Rebuff the Wicked counters a spell aimed at her; Pariah moves damage aimed at her controller onto a creature.

## Strategy

Cast an Aura, and Light-Paws tutors another Aura onto the battlefield; stack four or five on the commander and swing for 21 commander damage in two or three hits. Ethereal Armor and Sage's Reverie scale with what she has, Face of Divinity adds first strike and lifelink once another Aura is on her, and Light of Promise turns Spirit Link and On Serra's Wings lifegain into counters. The protection layer is a hand of one-mana instants and flash Auras plus Flickerform and the umbras. Winds of Rath and Single Combat clear the board around her.
