# Kinnan — Mana-Creature Combo (cEDH)

Opponent pool deck — **Tier 6: cEDH (Bracket 5)**. Axis: Mana combo with creatures.

- **Commander:** Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy
- **Color identity:** Blue, Green
- **Game Changers (10):** Ancient Tomb, Chrome Mox, Consecrated Sphinx, Fierce Guardianship, Force of Will, Grim Monolith, Mana Vault, Mox Diamond, Rhystic Study, The One Ring
- **Combos:** written in Stage 2 from the deck's primer.
- **Lands:** 24 (22 nonbasic + 2 basic)
- **Source:** SIEGE cEDH 10K, #1 of 308, 2026-06-13, record 5-0-3 ([decklist](https://edhtop16.com/tournament/level-7s-siege-at-the-castle-10k/decklist/10XZGpOw5vVlD7VeYRO1XvBv3Ft2))

## Role in the pool

Second by meta share (6.9%, 19.2% conversion). It wins through Kinnan's extra mana (Basalt Monolith, Dramatic Reversal) on a creature base. It tests Godo against a deck that races it with mana dorks rather than fighting the stack.
