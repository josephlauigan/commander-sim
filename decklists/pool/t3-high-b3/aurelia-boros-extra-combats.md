# Aurelia — Boros Extra Combats

Opponent pool deck — **Tier 3: High Bracket 3**. Axis: Aggro / extra combats.

- **Commander:** Aurelia, the Warleader
- **Color identity:** White, Red
- **Game Changers (3):** Smothering Tithe, Jeska's Will, Enlightened Tutor
- **Combos:** Helm of the Host + Combat Celebrant: a hasty Celebrant copy each combat exerts for another combat, unbounded. Two cards, 6+ mana to assemble — late-game only.
- **Lands:** 35 (15 nonbasic + 20 basic)

## Role in the pool

Multiple combats per turn blow past normal blocker math. Tests instant-speed removal on the commander and fog-style effects.

## Strategy

Aurelia gives a second combat every turn she attacks; every other extra-combat card stacks on top. Attack-trigger creatures (Hero of Bladehold, Adeline, Brimaz, Rabblemaster, Hellrider) pay off twice or three times per turn, and equipment like Sword of Fire and Ice and Embercleave hits on every combat. The late-game combo is Helm of the Host copying Combat Celebrant for unbounded combats.

## Sim modeling notes

Model these first; if any is Partial or unmodeled, this deck plays below its real strength and its results should be flagged.

Additional combat phases (Aurelia, Relentless Assault, Seize the Day, World at War, Combat Celebrant, Hellkite Charger, Port Razer, Karlach, Scourge); attack triggers per combat; equipment; Helm of the Host + Combat Celebrant loop.
