# Marwyn — Mono-Green Elves

Opponent pool deck — **Tier 3: High Bracket 3**. Axis: Go-wide / Craterhoof finish.

- **Commander:** Marwyn, the Nurturer
- **Color identity:** Green
- **Game Changers (3):** Gaea's Cradle, Natural Order, Worldly Tutor
- **Combos:** None infinite. Craterhoof Behemoth is the one-turn kill (Natural Order, Finale, Chord, GSZ find it).
- **Lands:** 31 (8 nonbasic + 23 basic)

## Role in the pool

A one-turn kill from a wide board. Your decks need a sweeper or instant-speed interaction ready by about turn five or six.

## At the table

- **Plan:** Turns one-mana elves like Elvish Mystic, Llanowar Elves and Fyndhorn Elves into a huge board, with Marwyn, the Nurturer, Priest of Titania, Elvish Archdruid and Gaea's Cradle making a lot of mana by turn three or four.
- **Wins by:** Craterhoof Behemoth, found by Natural Order, Green Sun's Zenith, Chord of Calling or Finale of Devastation. Ezuri, Renegade Leader and Avenger of Zendikar are backups.
- **Watch out for:** A wide board of elves by about turn four: have a sweeper or instant-speed answer ready. Natural Order puts Craterhoof straight onto the battlefield, and Survival of the Fittest keeps finding it. Gaea's Cradle makes the turn explosive.
- **Interaction:** Beast Within, Reclamation Sage, Collector Ouphe, Boseiju, Who Endures and Heroic Intervention. Kill the mana elves early or sweep before the board goes wide.

## Strategy

Curve out on mana elves, then turn the board into huge mana through Priest of Titania, Archdruid, Circle of Dreams Druid, Gaea's Cradle, and Marwyn herself. The finish is Craterhoof Behemoth, found by Natural Order, Finale of Devastation, Chord of Calling, or Green Sun's Zenith; Ezuri and Avenger of Zendikar are the backups. Beast Whisperer and Harmonize keep the hand full. There is no two-card infinite mana.

## Sim modeling notes

Model these first; if any is Partial or unmodeled, this deck plays below its real strength and its results should be flagged.

Mana elves scaling with Elf count; Gaea's Cradle; Craterhoof overrun math (+X/+X, trample, X = creature count); creature tutors (Natural Order puts Hoof straight onto the battlefield).
