# Atraxa — Four-Color Infect & Proliferate

Opponent pool deck — **Tier 3: High Bracket 3**. Axis: Proliferate with infect and planeswalkers.

- **Commander:** Atraxa, Praetors' Voice
- **Color identity:** White, Blue, Black, Green
- **Game Changers (3):** Farewell, Narset, Parter of Veils, Rhystic Study
- **Combos:** None.
- **Lands:** 35 (23 nonbasic + 12 basic)

## Role in the pool

A proliferate deck with two plans. One is poison: a few cheap infect and toxic creatures (Blighted Agent, Plague Stinger, Skithiryx, Ichor Rats, Skrelv, Ixhel) and a proliferate every turn count an opponent down to ten poison counters, whatever their life total. The other is counters and planeswalkers: Hardened Scales, Doubling Season and Vorinclex make every proliferate bigger, and Elspeth, Teferi, The Wandering Emperor, Liliana, Oko and Vraska ride the same proliferates up to their ultimates. Your decks need to answer small evasive creatures early, kill the proliferate engines and the planeswalkers, and not let the table's value engine run for ten rounds.

## At the table

- **Plan:** Ramps with Sol Ring, Birds of Paradise, Bloom Tender, Cultivate, Farseek and Nature's Lore, then lands whichever plan the hand allows: cheap infect and toxic creatures, or Hardened Scales, Courser of Kruphix, Eternal Witness, Shalai and Spark Double with the planeswalkers. Proliferate every turn: Atraxa at each end step, Evolution Sage and Flux Channeler, Tainted Observer, Ezuri, Contagion Engine, Karn's Bastion and Ichormoon Gauntlet.
- **Wins by:** Poison counters (Blighted Agent unblockable, Plague Stinger and Skithiryx flying, Ichor Rats, then proliferate; Tekuthal doubles each proliferate and Vorinclex doubles the counters), or a board of growing creatures, Walking Ballista and planeswalkers at high loyalty (Elspeth's soldiers, Liliana, Teferi and The Wandering Emperor).
- **Watch out for:** Any poison at all: each proliferate adds to every poisoned opponent. Rhystic Study taxes your spells, Narset stops your extra draws, Oko, Thief of Crowns turns your best permanent into an Elk, and a planeswalker left alone ticks up to its ultimate.
- **Interaction:** Counterspell, Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile, Assassin's Trophy, Vraska's Fall, Infectious Bite and Phyresis Outbreak, with Contagion Engine, Walking Ballista, The Wandering Emperor, Liliana and Farewell. Heroic Intervention and Lightning Greaves protect its board.

## Strategy

Develop mana and a proliferate engine first. Hit each opponent once with an infect or toxic creature and proliferate every turn until the poison reaches ten, or grow the creatures and planeswalkers with the same proliferates (Hardened Scales and Doubling Season add to every counter placed; Vorinclex doubles them). Planeswalkers (Ajani, Tamiyo, Vraska, Oko, Narset, Teferi, The Wandering Emperor, Elspeth, Liliana) add value and gain loyalty from every proliferate. No combo. Build: the infect core of EDHREC's Atraxa, Praetors' Voice average deck (Infect / Upgraded) with the counters and planeswalkers value of its +1/+1 Counters and Planeswalkers themes (2026-11-06).
