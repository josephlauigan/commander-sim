# Tymna the Weaver & Jeska, Thrice Reborn — Mardu Partners (W/B/R)

**Added 2026-10-13.** A third Tymna build, after the Thrasios (`tymna-thrasios-partners.md`) and Kraum
(`tymna-kraum-partners.md`) builds: three colours instead of four, for a steadier mana base and an early second
commander. **23 of its 100 cards are Jodah leftovers** (Tymna among them; Thrasios 47, Kraum 40): the retired deck's
blue and green cards don't fit. The rest are 59 bulk cards, 17 basics and **Jeska, the one buy** (about $5.75).

**Not in the simulator yet:** no deck key, not modelled, not measured. Built to be measured against the Kraum build
(15.3% over all tiers on 2026-10-13, look-ahead, loose, 1,000 games per tier).

## Strategy

**Tymna the Weaver** ({1}{W}{B}, 2/2 lifelink): *at the beginning of each of your postcombat main phases, you may pay
X life, where X is the number of opponents that were dealt combat damage this turn. If you do, draw X cards.*
**Jeska, Thrice Reborn** ({2}{R}, planeswalker): *enters with a loyalty counter for each time you've cast a commander
from the command zone this game. 0: choose target creature; until your next turn, if it would deal combat damage to
one of your opponents, it deals triple that damage instead. −X: X damage to each of up to three targets.*

- **Hit everyone, draw cards.** Cheap fliers and menace creatures (Falcon and Redwing, Nightwind Glider, Abigale,
  Moseo, Lord Jyscal Guado, Exultant Skymarcher, Thriving Skyclaw, Necrosquito, Shatterwing Pegasus, Firebird, Photon;
  Lightning, Black Widow, Vincent, Scolding Administrator, Callous Inspector; Spectral Lynx).
- **Triple it.** Jeska's 0 on the creature that will connect: Lyra (15 lifelinking damage), a double striker
  (Quill-Blade Laureate, Char-Rumbler, Captain Marvel, Practiced Offense) for six times its power, or Tymna herself
  for 6 commander damage. Her −X clears blockers and mana creatures.
- **Finish.** Lyra, Elenda, Terror of the Peaks, Razia, Captain Marvel, Hellkite Tyrant, Sneering Shadewriter.
- **Interact.** Sixteen removal spells (Toxic Deluge the only sweeper), no counterspells; Bastion Protector,
  Ultimate Magic: Holy and Shardmage's Rescue protect the commanders.

## Key lines

- **Turns 1-3:** a cheap evasive creature, a Talisman, Tymna on turn 3.
- **Turn 4:** Jeska (2 loyalty after Tymna); 0 on the best creature that connects, then attack every opponent you can.
- **Late:** Jeska's −X to clear blockers or finish creatures; the finishers with a tripled hit.

## Consistency

35 lands: 9 Jodah lands (painlands, Nomad Outpost, Shattered Sanctum, Command Tower, Exotic Orchard, Path of Ancestry,
Unclaimed Territory), 9 from bulk (Clifftop Retreat, Furycalm Snarl, Spectator Seating, Rugged Prairie, Slayers'
Stronghold, Radiant Summit, Scoured Barrens and two Temples) and 17 basics (6 Plains, 6 Swamp, 5 Mountain). Ramp:
two Talismans, Chromatic Lantern, Star Compass, Potioner's Trove, Kalain, Strixhaven Skycoach.

## Bracket and Rule 0

**Bracket 3 (low end) or 2:** no Game Changers, no two-card combos, no mass land destruction, no extra turns. Disclose
Aven Mindcensor.
