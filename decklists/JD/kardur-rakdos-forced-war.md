# Kardur, Doomscourge — Rakdos Forced War

**Added 2026-10-14.** JD's Rakdos goad deck: opponents are forced to fight each other, and every creature that dies
drains them. Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER and JD's new Phyrexian Tower and Phyrexian Altar are the death engine.
**26 buys, about $59** (about $31 without Sephiroth); everything else is from the bulk and binder lists.

**Not measured yet.**

## Strategy

**Kardur, Doomscourge** ({2}{B}{R}, 4/3): *When Kardur enters, until your next turn, creatures your opponents control
attack each combat if able and attack a player other than you if able. Whenever an attacking creature dies, each
opponent loses 1 life and you gain 1 life.*

- **Make the table fight itself.** Kardur's entry and the goad cards (Shiny, Parasitic and Ghoulish Impetus on their
  best creatures, Bloodthirsty Blade, Agitator Ant, Grenzo, Vengeful Ancestor, Geode Rager, and Disrupt Decorum and
  Maximum Carnage for whole boards) force opponents' creatures to attack someone other than you. Death Kiss doubles the
  power of creatures attacking your opponents.
- **Every death drains.** Sephiroth (and his emblem once flipped), Blood Artist, Zulaport Cutthroat, Mayhem Devil,
  Bastion of Remembrance, Al Bhed Salvagers and Kardur's own trigger turn the brawls into drains, on other players'
  turns too.
- **Your own deaths are fuel.** Tokens and cheap bodies (Lagomos's daily 2/1, Mogg War Marshal, Dread Drone,
  Spawn-Gang Commander, Eternal Student) go to Phyrexian Altar, Phyrexian Tower and Viscera Seer for mana and
  triggers; four deaths in a turn flips Sephiroth for a permanent drain emblem. Pitiless Plunderer turns deaths into
  Treasure.
- **Steal and sacrifice:** Act of Treason or Systems Override, attack with it, then sacrifice it to the Altar.
- **Finish:** drains, Terror of the Peaks, Hellkite Tyrant, and Blasphemous Act or Toxic Deluge with the emblem out.

## Key lines

- **Turns 1-3:** ramp (Sol Ring, Signets, Talisman), a sacrifice outlet, token makers.
- **Turn 4: Kardur** while opponents have creatures: their next round is a brawl among themselves.
- **Keep the war going:** an Impetus on the biggest threat, Agitator Ant every end step, Disrupt Decorum or Maximum
  Carnage before a big round.
- **Flip Sephiroth:** four deaths in one turn (sacrifices to Altar, Tower, Seer), then wipe the board for a big drain.

## Bracket and Rule 0

**Bracket 2-3:** no Game Changers, no two-card combos, no mass land destruction, no extra turns. Explain goad at the
start: goaded creatures must attack each combat and must attack someone other than you.
