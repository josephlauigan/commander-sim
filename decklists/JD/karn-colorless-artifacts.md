# Karn, Legacy Reforged — Colourless Urza and Mishra Artifacts

Fill the board with cheap artifacts, cast Karn, and turn his upkeep mana into big artifacts every turn. Karn adds
{C} for each artifact you control at the start of your upkeep, so every Bauble, rock and artifact land is one more
mana the next turn. The deck wins with large artifact threats (Wurmcoil Engine, Blightsteel Colossus, Portal to
Phyrexia, Myr Battlesphere), with a Basalt Monolith and Rings of Brighthearth infinite-mana combo, or by grinding
with planeswalkers. It runs as many Urza and Mishra cards as fit the plan, built around the Tron lands. It is weak
to artifact wipes and artifact hate (Vandalblast, Collector Ouphe, Null Rod), and it has no counterspells.

## Strategy

### The commander

Karn, Legacy Reforged ({5}, Legendary Artifact Creature — Golem) reads: *Karn's power and toughness are each equal to the greatest mana value among artifacts you control. At the beginning of your upkeep, add {C} for each artifact you control. This mana can't be spent to cast nonartifact spells. Until end of turn, you don't lose this mana as steps and phases end.*

Three things make Karn work here:

- **The mana scales with the artifact count.** Five artifacts on the battlefield is five extra mana every turn. Zero-cost artifacts (the Baubles, Mox Opal, Everflowing Chalice for zero) are each worth {C} a turn while they stay out.
- **The mana lasts the whole turn and pays for activated abilities too.** It can't cast a planeswalker or All Is Dust, but it pays for artifact spells and for abilities: Urza's Factory, Mishra's Factory, Urza's Blueprints' echo, Mirrorworks and Mishra's Self-Replicator copies, Staff of Domination, Walking Ballista counters and equip costs.
- **Karn is as big as your biggest artifact.** He is at least 5/5 (he counts himself), 9/9 with Portal to Phyrexia or Darksteel Forge out and 12/12 with Blightsteel Colossus. That also turns on Bonders' Enclave.

Karn costs five. With a Sol Ring, Mishra's Workshop, Ancient Tomb or the Tron lands he lands on turn 3, sometimes turn 2.

### Game plan

**Opening hand.** Keep three or more lands, or two lands and a rock. Cheap artifacts and a Tron piece or Urza's Saga make a hand better. Ship hands with only big artifacts and no way to cast them.

**Turns 1–3.** Play cheap artifacts and rocks: Sol Ring, Mana Vault, Grim Monolith, the Baubles, Mind Stone, Everflowing Chalice, Mox Opal once you have three artifacts. Urza's Saga makes Constructs and fetches a {0}–{1} artifact on chapter III. Cast Karn as early as you can, with Lightning Greaves or Swiftfoot Boots if you have them.

**Turns 4–6.** Karn's upkeep mana plus your lands cast a large artifact each turn. Use card draw (Mystic Forge, Urza's Blueprints, Karn, Scion of Urza, The One Ring) to keep the hand full. Remove threats with Duplicant, Meteor Golem, Spine of Ish Sah or a planeswalker.

**Late game.** Win with a large threat, a wipe that only hits your opponents (see below), or the infinite combo.

### Big threats

- **Wurmcoil Engine** (6): deathtouch, lifelink, and two 3/3 Wurms when it dies.
- **Blightsteel Colossus** (12): an indestructible 11/11 trample infect that shuffles back if it would go to the graveyard. Kuldotha Forgemaster puts it onto the battlefield.
- **Portal to Phyrexia** (9): each opponent sacrifices three creatures, and you reanimate a creature from any graveyard every upkeep.
- **Myr Battlesphere** (7): five bodies, and direct damage when it attacks.
- **Triplicate Titan** (9): a 9/9 flier that leaves three 3/3 Golems.
- **Steel Hellkite** (6): a flier that destroys every nonland permanent with mana value X of a player it hits.
- **Platinum Angel** (7): you can't lose while it's out.
- **Cityscape Leveler** (8): destroys a nonland permanent when cast and on every attack; unearth brings it back.
- **Batterskull** (5): a 4/4 vigilance lifelink body that can return to your hand to dodge removal.

### Removal and one-sided wipes

Colourless is the deck's main defence: most wipes that hit coloured permanents only hit your opponents.

- **All Is Dust** (7, sorcery): each player sacrifices every coloured permanent. You lose nothing.
- **Ugin, the Spirit Dragon** (8): −X exiles each coloured permanent with mana value X or less. Yours are colourless, so it is one-sided.
- **Ugin, the Ineffable** (6): your colourless spells cost {2} less (nearly the whole deck); −3 destroys a coloured permanent.
- **Karn Liberated** (7): −3 exiles any permanent; +4 strips cards from hands.
- **Spot removal:** Duplicant, Meteor Golem, Spine of Ish Sah (returns to your hand when it dies), Unstable Obelisk, The Mightstone and Weakstone (−5/−5), Steel Hellkite, Cityscape Leveler.
- **Symmetric wipes:** Oblivion Stone (fate counters protect your best permanents) and Nevinyrral's Disk hit you too; use them when you are behind. Ratchet Bomb hits tokens at zero or one mana value.
- **Graveyard and ability hate:** Soul-Guide Lantern, Pithing Needle.

### Card draw and tutors

- **Draw engines:** Mystic Forge (cast artifacts and colourless spells from the top of your library), The One Ring, Urza's Blueprints, Karn, Scion of Urza, Staff of Domination ({5}: draw), Endless Atlas (needs three Wastes), Mazemind Tome, Urza's Tome, Skullclamp (on Construct, Myr and Thopter tokens), Hedron Archive, Mind Stone.
- **Draw lands:** War Room ({3}, {T}: draw a card; your commander has no colours, so it costs no life), Bonders' Enclave ({3}, {T}: draw while you control a creature with power 4 or more; Karn usually is).
- **Tutors:** Urza's Saga (a {0}–{1} artifact), Expedition Map and Urza's Cave (any land), Inventors' Fair (any artifact, with three or more artifacts), Kuldotha Forgemaster (sacrifice three artifacts: any artifact onto the battlefield), Karn, the Great Creator (−2: an artifact from exile, or from outside the game if your table allows a sideboard).

### Protection

Lightning Greaves and Swiftfoot Boots protect Karn the turn he lands. Darksteel Forge makes every artifact you control indestructible, so it blanks Vandalblast and most wipes. Darksteel Citadel is an indestructible artifact land. Buried Ruin returns an artifact from the graveyard. Platinum Angel and The One Ring's protection buy a turn.

### How it wins

1. **Large threats** off Karn's mana: Wurmcoil, Blightsteel, Battlesphere, Triplicate Titan, Steel Hellkite, Cityscape Leveler.
2. **Portal to Phyrexia** strips the table's creatures and takes over their graveyards.
3. **Basalt Monolith + Rings of Brighthearth** makes infinite colourless mana (see Key lines), which Walking Ballista turns into infinite damage and Staff of Domination into infinite cards.
4. **One-sided wipes** (All Is Dust, Ugin) followed by any attacker.

### Weak spots and how to play around them

- **Artifact wipes** (Vandalblast, Bane of Progress, Austere Command) hit almost everything. Keep a threat or two in hand, hold Darksteel Forge for when the board is big, and use Oblivion Stone's fate counters before wiping.
- **Null Rod and Collector Ouphe** stop the rocks' abilities (not Karn's trigger or the lands). Remove them with Meteor Golem, Spine of Ish Sah or Karn Liberated; Duplicant and Ugin, the Ineffable also answer Ouphe.
- **No counterspells.** You can't stop a combo turn on the stack. Pithing Needle on a key card and Platinum Angel help; otherwise race.
- **Blood Moon and Back to Basics** hit the nonbasic lands. The Wastes, the rocks and Karn's mana still work.
- **Karn is the engine.** Protect him with Greaves or Boots before you rely on his mana. Recasting him costs {2} more each time.

## Key lines

**Basalt Monolith + Rings of Brighthearth, infinite mana.** Tap Basalt for {C}{C}{C}. Activate its untap ability ({3}); Rings triggers, pay {2} to copy it. The copy resolves and untaps Basalt; tap it again for {C}{C}{C}. The original untap ability then resolves and untaps it again; tap it for {C}{C}{C}. Each loop nets one {C}. You need {2} beyond Basalt's mana to start. Spend the mana on Walking Ballista ({4}: a counter; remove a counter: 1 damage) or Staff of Domination ({1}: untap; {5}, {T}: draw).

**Kuldotha Forgemaster into Blightsteel or Portal.** With Forgemaster out since the start of your turn and untapped, sacrifice three spent artifacts (Baubles, a used Mana Vault, Expedition Map) to put Blightsteel Colossus, Portal to Phyrexia or Darksteel Forge onto the battlefield.

**Turn 2 Karn.** Mishra's Workshop plus a one-drop artifact on turn 1, or Sol Ring, or Ancient Tomb plus a rock.

**Mishra's Self-Replicator chain.** With Karn's mana, each artifact you cast copies every Self-Replicator for {1} each, so the copies multiply. Cast several cheap artifacts in one turn.

**Rings of Brighthearth copies planeswalker abilities.** Loyalty abilities are activated abilities: copy Karn Liberated −3 (exile two permanents), Ugin, the Spirit Dragon +2 (6 damage) or Karn, Scion of Urza +1 (two cards) for {2}.

**Sequencing.**
- **Keep zero-cost artifacts on the battlefield** while they make Karn mana; sacrifice the Baubles at the end of an opponent's turn to draw on your upkeep.
- **Cast All Is Dust or Ugin before your threats** if the table is full of coloured creatures: they cost you nothing.
- **Oblivion Stone:** put fate counters on Karn and your best permanents over several turns before you sacrifice it.
- **Mishra's Workshop mana only casts artifact spells.** It can't cast the planeswalkers or All Is Dust.

## Mana and consistency

The list is exactly 100 cards: Karn plus 99, singleton except 14 Wastes, all colourless.

34 lands (14 Wastes and 20 nonbasic), 16 mana rocks, three cards that fetch Wastes (Solemn Simulacrum, Burnished Hart, Wayfarer's Bauble), and Karn's upkeep mana.

**Curve.** Average mana value 3.8 over the 65 nonland cards: 13 at zero or one, 21 at two or three, 14 at four or five, 11 at six or seven, and six at eight or more (Ugin, the Spirit Dragon, Cityscape Leveler, Portal to Phyrexia, Darksteel Forge, Triplicate Titan, Blightsteel Colossus).

**Card types.** 17 creatures, 42 noncreature artifacts, 5 planeswalkers, 1 sorcery, 34 lands, plus Karn. 61 of the 99 are artifacts (counting the two artifact lands), so Karn usually makes four or more mana by turn 5.

**Redundancy.** Karn's mana comes from any artifact. Large threats come from many sources (nine finishers, two artifact tutors, Mystic Forge). Removal comes from nine spot answers and two one-sided wipes.

## Bracket and Rule 0

**Bracket 4.** Five Game Changers: **Ancient Tomb**, **Grim Monolith**, **Mana Vault**, **Mishra's Workshop** and **The One Ring**; Bracket 3 allows three. Basalt Monolith + Rings of Brighthearth is a two-card infinite combo; check the current bracket rules on two-card combos before a Bracket 3 game. For a Bracket 3 table, cut two Game Changers and Rings of Brighthearth.

Disclose before the game:

- **Basalt Monolith + Rings of Brighthearth** is an infinite-mana combo (outlets: Walking Ballista, Staff of Domination).
- **Proxies:** eight cards (listed above).
- **One-sided wipes:** All Is Dust, Ugin, the Spirit Dragon.
- **Karn, the Great Creator** can fetch from outside the game; agree on a sideboard or limit it to exile.

Re-check the official list at https://commanderbrackets.com/faq before an event.
