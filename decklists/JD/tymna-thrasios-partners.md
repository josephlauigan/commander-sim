# Tymna the Weaver & Thrasios, Triton Hero — Four-Colour Partners (W/U/B/G)

**Added 2026-10-13.** JD's new deck around Tymna the Weaver, built to reuse as much of the retired Jodah deck as
possible: **47 of its 100 cards are Jodah leftovers** (Tymna among them), and Desertion and Dissipate (Jodah cards
cut on 2026-10-09) come back from the binder. The rest are 40 bulk cards, 10 basics and **Thrasios, the one
buy**. Thrasios was chosen over the other green partners because white/blue/black/green keeps the most Jodah cards
(51 fit, against 44 for Tana and 34 for an Abzan partner).

**Not in the simulator yet:** no deck key, not modelled, not measured.

## Strategy

**Tymna the Weaver** ({1}{W}{B}, 2/2 lifelink): *at the beginning of each of your postcombat main phases, you may pay
X life, where X is the number of opponents that were dealt combat damage this turn. If you do, draw X cards.*
**Thrasios, Triton Hero** ({G}{U}, 1/3): *{4}: Scry 1, then reveal the top card of your library. If it's a land card,
put it onto the battlefield tapped. Otherwise, draw a card.*

- **Hit everyone, draw cards.** Tymna draws one card for every opponent hit in combat, so the deck plays cheap
  creatures that are hard to block: fliers (Cuboid Colony, The Wasp, Falcon and Redwing, Nightwind Glider, Abigale,
  Moseo, Aven Mindcensor, Nightveil Predator, Sphinx of New Prahv), Silhana Ledgewalker (only fliers can block her),
  Exit Specialist (big creatures can't block it) and Spectral Lynx (protection from green). Mirri lets each opponent
  block with only one creature. Reconnaissance Mission and The Aetherspark draw off the same hits.
- **Mana into cards.** Thrasios turns every spare four mana into a land or a card, so the deck runs heavy ramp: Birds
  of Paradise, Paradise Druid, Fyndhorn Elder, Faeburrow Elder (one mana of each colour you have on the battlefield),
  Coiling Oracle, Cultivate and five mana rocks.
- **Finish.** Lyra, Dragonlord Dromoka, Elenda, Tolsimir and Dakkon Blackblade from Jodah; King Darien and Tolsimir
  make the small attackers bigger.
- **Interact.** Fifteen removal spells (Toxic Deluge the only sweeper), five counterspells (Force of Will, Arcane
  Denial, Mana Leak, Dissipate, Desertion) and four protection cards: Privileged Position, Bastion Protector (your
  commanders get +2/+2 and indestructible), Ultimate Magic: Holy and Shardmage's Rescue.

## Key lines

- **Turns 1-3: mana and a cheap attacker.** A mana creature or rock, then a two-drop flier and Tymna on turn 3.
- **Attack every opponent you can, then draw.** Spread attackers so as many opponents as possible take damage; pay the
  life for Tymna only as far as your life total allows.
- **Thrasios when the mana is spare.** Cast him early, then put any mana you didn't use at the end of an opponent's
  turn into his ability.
- **Protect the engine.** Hold Ultimate Magic: Holy or Shardmage's Rescue for removal on Tymna; Bastion Protector
  makes both commanders indestructible.

## Consistency

35 lands plus ten ramp cards. 17 lands are Jodah's (painlands, tri-lands, Command Tower, Exotic Orchard, Path of
Ancestry, Unclaimed Territory, Vivid Creek); 8 come from bulk (Glacial Fortress, Port Town, Fortified Village, Canopy
Vista, Deathcap Glade and three Temples); 10 basics (3 Forest, 3 Island, 2 Plains, 2 Swamp). Unclaimed Territory:
name **Human** (Tymna and ten other creatures are Humans). **Weak spot:** nine lands always enter tapped and seven more
do unless their condition is met; the Temples are the first to swap if dual lands are bought.

## Bracket and Rule 0

**Bracket 3,** with two Game Changers: **Force of Will** and **Worldly Tutor**. No two-card infinite combos, no mass
land destruction, no extra turns. Disclose Aven Mindcensor (opponents' searches see only the top four cards) and
Dragonlord Dromoka (no spells from opponents during your turn).

Re-check the official list at https://commanderbrackets.com/faq before an event.

## Jodah cards left out

Of the 51 Jodah cards in these colours, four stay in the binder: **Szadek** (his combat damage becomes counters and
milling, so it never counts for Tymna), **Korlash** (too few Swamps), **Carth** (only three planeswalkers to find) and
**Mirari** (too few instants and sorceries to copy). Nineteen other Jodah cards are already in other decks (Cloud,
Kefka, Titania), and Jodah himself is five-colour.
