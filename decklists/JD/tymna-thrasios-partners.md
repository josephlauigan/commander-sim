# Tymna the Weaver & Thrasios, Triton Hero — Four-Colour Partners (W/U/B/G)

> Built from the Tymna/Kraum deck (2026-10-19).

Two commanders that turn spare mana into cards: Tymna draws one card for each opponent your creatures hit in combat, and
Thrasios turns any four spare mana into a scry, a land or a card. Behind a wall of attack taxes (Ghostly Prison,
Propaganda, Crawlspace and their friends) and a green ramp and draw package, the deck wins with flying finishers: Elspeth,
the Titans, Baneslayer, Lyra, Dragonlord Dromoka and more. A small artifact combo, **Basalt Monolith + Rings of
Brighthearth + Walking Ballista**, can end the game on the spot. It is weak to decks that can pay the attack taxes every
turn, to fast combo, and to falling behind on mana.

## Strategy

### The commanders

**Tymna the Weaver** ({1}{W}{B}, 2/2 lifelink). *At the beginning of each of your postcombat main phases, you may pay X
life, where X is the number of opponents that were dealt combat damage this turn. If you do, draw X cards.*

**Thrasios, Triton Hero** ({G}{U}, 1/1). *{4}: Scry 1, then reveal the top card of your library. If it's a land card, put
it onto the battlefield tapped. Otherwise, draw a card.*

Both have plain Partner, so they share the command zone. Thrasios costs two mana, so he comes down on turn 2 and every
spare four mana afterwards becomes a land or a card: activate him at the end of an opponent's turn (the mana would untap
anyway) and in your second main phase once nothing better is castable. Tymna comes down on turn 3 and draws off every
opponent your creatures reach; what she counts is *opponents* hit, not creatures, so a 1/1 token into each player is
worth more than one big hit. Keep **Lightning Greaves** for Tymna: she is the deck's most-removed card, and Greaves
(equip {0}) gives her shroud and lets her attack at once.

### Game plan

**Early turns (1–3).** A mana creature or rock (Avacyn's Pilgrim, Faeburrow Elder, Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone, Orzhov
Signet, a Talisman) into **Thrasios on turn 2** or **Tymna on turn 3**. Coiling Oracle and Paradox Surveyor are
two-for-ones that find lands.

**Middle turns (4–6).** Get the defense down: **Ghostly Prison**, **Propaganda**, **Crawlspace**, **Windborn Muse**,
**Archon of Absolution** and **Baird** make attacking you expensive, so opponents attack each other instead. Attack
each opponent with something so Tymna draws, and spend every spare four mana on Thrasios.

**Late game.** The finishers take over: **Elspeth, Sun's Champion**, **Sun Titan**, **Grave Titan**, **Baneslayer Angel**,
**Lyra Dawnbringer**, **Dragonlord Dromoka** ({4}{G}{W}, 5/7 flying, lifelink, can't be countered; opponents can't cast
spells on your turn), **Resplendent Angel**.

### Defense: why it matters

Without defense this shell lost most of its games to creature decks in combat: its own creatures are needed to attack for
Tymna, so it can't also block. The attack taxes fix that:

- **Ghostly Prison** and **Propaganda** ({2} per attacker), **Windborn Muse** (the same, on a flier), **Archon of
  Absolution** ({1} per attacker, protection from white) and **Baird, Steward of Argive** ({1} per attacker) stack: a
  creature attacking you can cost {6} or more.
- **Crawlspace**: no more than two creatures can attack you each combat, which shuts down token swarms and extra-combat
  decks.
- **Elspeth**'s −3 destroys every creature with power 4 or more (yours too: count your Titans and Angels first);
  **Sunblast Angel** destroys every tapped creature when it enters (yours too, so cast it before you attack, after an
  opponent's big attack); **Austere Command** is the full reset (choose creatures with mana value 3 or less and 4 or
  more: your attack taxes and rocks survive).

### The Basalt Monolith + Rings of Brighthearth combo

**Basalt Monolith** ({3}, taps for {C}{C}{C}, {3}: untap) and **Rings of Brighthearth** ({3}, pay {2} to copy an ability
you activate that isn't a mana ability) make unlimited colourless mana: tap the Monolith for {C}{C}{C}, activate its
untap, pay {2} for Rings' copy, and the original and the copy untap it twice, so it taps for {C}{C}{C} twice more: {6}
for {5} each time round. The first loop needs {5} in all (the Monolith's own three and two more, or five from other
sources when it is tapped).

- **The finisher is Walking Ballista** ({X}{X}): cast it for a huge X and remove the counters, one damage each, at
  every opponent. It works from the battlefield or from the hand.
- **Without a Ballista in hand, Thrasios digs for it**: each {4} puts the top card into your hand (a land goes onto the
  battlefield), so with the loop running Thrasios finds the Ballista wherever it sits in the library.
- **Worldly Tutor** ({G}) fetches the Ballista onto the top of the library when the Monolith and Rings are out; with
  Thrasios the dig draws it at once, without him it is the next draw.

The loop is two cards that do nothing alone, so hold them until both can be played in a turn when the table has open
mana for removal: an opponent who destroys Rings or the Monolith in response ends it.

### Finishers

- **Elspeth, Sun's Champion** (+1: three 1/1 Soldiers a turn) is the best card in the deck: a wall of blockers and
  attackers in one.
- **Sun Titan** returns a permanent with mana value 3 or less from your graveyard whenever it enters or attacks: a dead
  Tymna, an attack tax, a rock. **Grave Titan** brings two 2/2 Zombies on entering and on each attack.
- **Baneslayer Angel** and **Lyra Dawnbringer** are lifelinking fliers with first strike that race and block.
  **Resplendent Angel** ({1}{W}{W}, 3/3 flier) makes a 4/4 flying, vigilant Angel at each end step in which you
  gained 5 or more life, which the lifelinkers (Baneslayer, Lyra, Vampire Nighthawk, Tymna, Dromoka) make easy.
- **Dragonlord Dromoka** cannot be countered and shuts the table out of your turn, so an end-of-turn trick or a flash
  blocker never comes.
- **Diabolic Tutor** ({2}{B}{B}) fetches any card. Fetch Elspeth when behind, a Titan or Dromoka to close, or a piece of
  the combo.
- **Shield of Kaldra** (equip {4}) makes a creature indestructible, **Helm of Kaldra** (equip {2}) gives first strike,
  trample and haste, and **Conqueror's Flail** gives +4/+4 with all four colours and stops opponents from casting spells
  on your turn.

### Tymna's attackers

Tymna wants every opponent hit each turn. **Adeline** makes a 1/1 attacking *each* opponent whenever you attack, and
**Hero of Bladehold** makes two attacking tokens with battle cry; **Dauthi Voidwalker** (shadow), **Vampire
Nighthawk**, **The Wasp** (taps a blocker when it attacks), **Nightwind Glider**, **Falcon and Redwing** and **Nightveil
Predator** get through on their own. **Cecil, Dark Knight** ({B}, 2/3 deathtouch) attacks from turn two: nothing wants to
block him, so he starts Tymna's draws early; each hit costs you that much life, and at 20 life or less he untaps and
becomes **Cecil, Redeemed Paladin** (4/4 lifelink, and the other attackers are indestructible when he attacks). Attack the
opponents you can reach, keep enough back to block what the taxes let through, and draw.

### Removal and interaction

**Swords to Plowshares** and **Path to Exile** (one mana), **Darksteel Mutation** (also answers a commander),
**Skyclave Apparition**, **Witherbloom Charm** (destroy a nonland permanent with mana value 2 or less, or sacrifice a
permanent to draw two, or gain 5), **Bite Down** (a creature of yours deals damage equal to its power to one of theirs),
**Memory Trap** and **Disenchant**; **Elspeth** adds more. **Arcane Denial** is the one counterspell, and **Aven
Mindcensor** (flash) blunts tutors and land searches. **Restoration Angel** (flash) blinks a creature out of removal and
reuses an enters effect (a Titan, Skyclave Apparition). **Dark Endurance** ({1}{B}: +2/+0 and indestructible), **Blossoming
Defense** ({G}: +2/+2 and hexproof) and **Lightning Greaves** protect Tymna.

### Ramp and card advantage beyond the commanders

**Avacyn's Pilgrim** and **Faeburrow Elder** (taps for one mana of each colour among your permanents) ramp and fix;
**Coiling Oracle** and **Paradox Surveyor** find lands or cards when they enter; **Fact or Fiction** digs. **Worldly
Tutor** is the deck's one Game Changer: a creature on top of the library, such as the Ballista when the combo is out,
Dromoka or a Titan.

### How it wins

Fliers and lifelink behind the attack taxes, with Tymna's draws and Thrasios's dig finding a Titan, Elspeth or Dromoka;
or the Basalt Monolith + Rings of Brighthearth loop into Walking Ballista, from the hand or found with Thrasios.

### Weak spots and how to play around them

- **Fast decks.** The first four turns are slow; the taxes matter only from turn 4. Spend the first turns on mana and a
  blocker rather than holding up Tymna's draws.
- **Combo and storm.** Aven Mindcensor, Arcane Denial and Memory Trap are the answers; a Dromoka on the battlefield
  stops anything cast on your turn.
- **Wipes.** The defense is mostly enchantments and artifacts, which survive creature wipes; rebuild the attackers with
  the Titans and Elspeth.
- **Commander removal.** Tymna dies to everything; Greaves, Dark Endurance and Blossoming Defense protect her, and the
  command zone makes her a three-mana recast plus tax.

## Key lines

- **Turn 2 Thrasios or a rock, turn 3 Tymna, turn 4 an attack tax:** the table attacks someone else while Tymna draws.
- **Every spare {4} into Thrasios** at the end of an opponent's turn: a land or a card, with a scry first.
- **Basalt Monolith + Rings of Brighthearth + Walking Ballista:** five mana, then a Ballista for the table's life
  total; **Worldly Tutor** or Diabolic Tutor finds the missing piece.
- **Crawlspace + Ghostly Prison:** at most two attackers, each costing {2}.
- **Sun Titan on a dead Tymna or a destroyed attack tax:** it comes back every attack.
- **Sunblast Angel after an opponent's alpha strike:** every tapped attacker dies.

**Mulligan:** keep 3+ lands that make at least three of the four colours, with a rock, a mana creature or a turn-2 or
turn-3 commander. A hand that can't cast a commander by turn 3 or 4 is a mulligan.

## Mana and consistency

38 lands: 16 nonbasic and 22 basic (7 Plains, 6 Island, 6 Swamp, 3 Forest); no land enters tapped every time. Mana
rocks and creatures: **Arcane Signet**, **Fellwar Stone**, **Orzhov Signet**, **Talisman of Curiosity**, **Talisman of
Unity**, **Avacyn's Pilgrim**, **Faeburrow Elder**.

- **Any colour:** Command Tower, Exotic Orchard, Unclaimed Territory (name **Human**).
- **Checklands** (untapped with a basic of the right type): Isolated Chapel (W/B), Drowned Catacomb (U/B), Glacial
  Fortress (W/U), Sunpetal Grove (G/W).
- **Other two-colour lands:** Brushland, Caves of Koilos, Llanowar Wastes and Underground River (pain lands), Deathcap
  Glade (G/B, untapped with two or more other lands), Port Town (reveal a basic), Yavimaya Coast (pain land), Breeding
  Pool (shock land: pay 2 life or it enters tapped), Undergrowth Stadium (B/G, untapped with two or more opponents).

**Card draw:** Tymna, Thrasios, Coiling Oracle, Paradox Surveyor, Fact or Fiction, Witherbloom Charm.
**Tutors:** Worldly Tutor, Diabolic Tutor.

## Bracket and Rule 0

**Bracket 3,** with one Game Changer, **Worldly Tutor**. No mass land destruction and no extra turns. **Basalt Monolith +
Rings of Brighthearth** makes unlimited colourless mana; with Walking Ballista it wins the game, which is a two-card
infinite mana loop that Bracket 3 normally leaves out: tell the table about it, and about the attack taxes (Ghostly
Prison, Propaganda, Crawlspace, Windborn Muse, Archon of Absolution, Baird), Aven Mindcensor, Dragonlord Dromoka and
Conqueror's Flail (no spells on your turn) before the game.
