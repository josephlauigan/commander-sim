# Sephiroth, the Savior — Phyrexian Reanimator

Fill the graveyard, cheat a Phyrexian bomb onto the battlefield three or four turns before anyone can cast one, and drain the table through sacrifice loops while the Praetors make the board unplayable. When the pieces line up, an infinite loop ends the game on the spot. The deck is weak to graveyard hate and to a fast combo deck that goes first.

Sephiroth, the Savior is the Final Fantasy printing of Atraxa, Grand Unifier, and appears under that name in the import list. (This is the Seph/Atraxa deck, key `seph`. Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER, a different card, is in the 99.)

## Strategy

### The commander

Atraxa, Grand Unifier: *Flying, vigilance, deathtouch, lifelink. When Atraxa enters, reveal the top ten cards of your library. For each card type, you may put a card of that type from among the revealed cards into your hand. Put the rest on the bottom of your library in a random order.* Sephiroth, the Savior plays the same way.

She is a 7/7 that can be cast for seven, and one entry is a whole hand: up to one creature, one land, one artifact, one enchantment, one instant, one sorcery and one planeswalker from the top ten (the deck has no planeswalkers or battles, so expect up to six cards). She is the best reanimation target in the deck, because the enter trigger comes with a 7/7 flying lifelink body. Blink her (Ephemerate, Teleportation Circle, Conjurer's Closet, Displacer Kitten) and she digs ten cards deeper. She is a four-colour card, so don't hard-cast her unless the mana is easy; reanimation is the main route. The cards she puts on the bottom are gone for the game, so don't bring her in with a library close to empty.

### Game plan

**Turns 1 to 3: mana and yard.** Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Talisman of Hierarchy, Chromatic Lantern, Birds of Paradise and Sakura-Tribe Elder for mana. **Dark Ritual** ({B}: add {B}{B}{B}) pays for Entomb and Reanimate in the same turn, as early as turn one. Stitcher's Supplier, Buried Alive, Entomb and **Glimpse the Unthinkable** ({U}{B}: target player mills ten; aim it at yourself) load the graveyard. Tortured Existence keeps it filling every turn. **Gifts Ungiven** is a yard-filler and tutor in one: name four cards you're happy to have in either zone (for example four reanimation targets, or Mikaeus and Triskelion plus two reanimation spells), and whatever the table puts in your graveyard is exactly what you wanted there.

**Turns 3 to 5: cheat something out.** Reanimate, Grave Researcher // Reanimate, Animate Dead, Necromancy, Victimize and Dread Return point at Archon of Cruelty, Grave Titan, Rune-Scarred Demon, Sheoldred, or Sephiroth herself. **Rune-Scarred Demon** ({5}{B}{B}, 6/6 flier) puts any card from your library into your hand when it enters, so every reanimation or blink of it is another tutor. Sheoldred, Whispering One then returns a creature every upkeep for free. **Victimize** ({2}{B} sorcery) sacrifices a creature to return two creature cards from your graveyard tapped, so it is two bombs for one spell, and the sacrifice triggers Blood Artist and Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER.

**Late game.** Hold up Arcane Denial, Swan Song and Ephemerate, keep Austere Command as the reset, assemble a loop, and use the grind engines (Muldrotha, Sheoldred, Whispering One, the end-step flickers) to keep the pressure on if the first plan is answered. Sheoldred's, Archon's and Elesh Norn's drains and edicts keep the table small while you do it.

### Fill the yard

Stitcher's Supplier mills three when it enters and again when it dies, so it is a good first-turn play and a good sacrifice. Entomb puts any card in the graveyard for {B}, Buried Alive puts up to three creature cards there, **Glimpse the Unthinkable** mills ten (target yourself; it sets up Living Death), and Tortured Existence ({B}, discard a creature card: return a creature card from the graveyard to your hand) turns an uncastable fatty in hand into the creature you need. **Skullclamp** on Stitcher's Supplier or a Zombie token kills it at once (the Clamp gives +1/-1) and draws two; with Supplier it also mills three more. Demonic Tutor and Vampiric Tutor are there to find the missing piece; see the tutor section below.

### Reanimation

Six reanimation spells and a mass one: **Reanimate** ({B}, lose life equal to the card's mana value, and it can take a creature from any graveyard), **Grave Researcher // Reanimate** (a {2}{B} 3/3 that surveils 1 each upkeep; when three or more creature cards are in your graveyard it becomes prepared, and you may cast a copy of Reanimate from it, so it is a Reanimate every turn while the yard stays full), **Animate Dead** and **Necromancy** (enchantments that bring a creature back and go with it; Necromancy can be cast at instant speed, but then it is sacrificed at the next cleanup step), **Victimize** ({2}{B}: sacrifice a creature, return two creature cards from your graveyard tapped) and **Dread Return** ({2}{B}{B}, return a creature card from your graveyard; flashback by sacrificing three creatures, which Grave Titan's Zombies or a loop pay). **Living Death** ({3}{B}{B}) has each player exile the creature cards in their graveyard, sacrifice their creatures, then put the exiled cards onto the battlefield. After Glimpse the Unthinkable or Buried Alive it returns a whole board, and it is also a reset when the table's creatures are better than yours; check their graveyards first, since theirs come back too. Every one of them can take a legendary creature, so any spell reaches any bomb. **Yawgmoth's Will** replays the graveyard for a turn: use it for a big turn once the yard is deep. Everything that would go to the graveyard that turn is exiled instead, so don't try the Nim Deathmantle loop under it. Sheoldred, Whispering One, Muldrotha and Grave Pact (below) are the other half of the reanimation plan.

### The card-advantage layer

**Phyrexian Arena** adds a card every upkeep for 1 life, and **Rhystic Study** ({2}{U}) draws a card whenever an opponent casts a spell unless that player pays {1}. **Sheoldred, the Apocalypse** gains you 2 life for every card you draw and makes each opponent lose 2 whenever they draw, so with her out the Arena's and the Study's draws gain more life than they cost.

**Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER** ({2}{B}, 3/3) lets you sacrifice another creature when he enters or attacks, and draws a card if you do. Whenever another creature dies, target opponent loses 1 life and you gain 1; the fourth time that resolves in a turn he transforms into a 5/5 flier, and you get an emblem that drains 1 whenever any creature dies. He is the second drain creature beside Blood Artist, so one removal spell on a payoff doesn't stop a loop from killing, and in a loop he flips at once and the emblem stays after he is gone. Flipped, he can sacrifice any number of other creatures when he attacks and draw that many.

**Displacer Kitten** blinks a nonland permanent every time you cast a noncreature spell. Blink Sephiroth for another top-ten dig, Archon of Cruelty for another drain-and-edict, Rune-Scarred Demon for another tutor, Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER for another sacrifice-and-draw, Kitchen Finks for 2 more life, or Grave Titan for two more Zombies.

### Muldrotha turns the graveyard into a second hand

During each of your turns you may play a land and cast one permanent spell of each permanent type from your graveyard: a creature, an artifact, and an enchantment. In this deck that means:
- **A reanimation spell every turn.** Animate Dead and Necromancy are enchantments, so once one is in the graveyard Muldrotha recasts it each turn for another reanimation.
- **Loop pieces back after removal.** Triskelion (as the artifact or the creature), Kitchen Finks, Nim Deathmantle, Ashnod's Altar, Phyrexian Altar, or Viscera Seer, so one answer no longer breaks a combo.
- **Engines back after a wipe.** Aura Shards, Phyrexian Arena, Rhystic Study, or Skullclamp.
- **A land from the graveyard each turn.** Replay a fetch land every turn (1 life each time) for a dual or a basic, which also puts a land in the graveyard for Muldrotha to replay next turn, or Strip Mine for land destruction every turn (say so before the game). Crucible of Worlds does the same without her.

She is a 6/6 for six and a fine reanimation target herself.

### Protection and blink

**Ephemerate is protection and a second Kitten in one card.** For {W} at instant speed, exile your creature and return it. In response to a removal spell, theft, or any other targeted effect, the spell fizzles because the creature is a new object, so a reanimated bomb survives the first answer the table throws at it. With nothing to protect, blink Archon of Cruelty, Grave Titan, or Sephiroth for their enter triggers. Either way, rebound casts it again for free at your next upkeep: a second drain-and-edict from Archon or two more Zombies from Titan. It can only target creatures, so it can't save Aura Shards.

**The flicker package re-buys enter triggers every turn.** **Teleportation Circle** and **Conjurer's Closet** each exile a creature you control at the beginning of your end step and return it: Sephiroth for another top-ten dig, Archon of Cruelty for another drain-and-edict, Grave Titan for two more Zombies. Teleportation Circle can take an artifact instead, so with no creature worth it, flicker a tapped Sol Ring or Signet and it comes back untapped for the opponents' turns. **Restoration Angel** is a 3/4 flash flier that does the same once at instant speed: at the end of an opponent's turn for value, or in response to removal or theft aimed at a bomb (the spell fizzles, as with Ephemerate). It can't flicker an Angel, so not Sephiroth herself.

**Arcane Denial** ({1}{U}) counters any spell. Its controller may draw up to two cards at the next upkeep and you draw one, so save it for the spell that matters (a wipe, Rest in Peace, a counter aimed at your combo). **Swan Song** ({U}) counters an enchantment, instant or sorcery (it gives that player a 2/2 flying Bird, so it is best used on a wipe or a tutor). **Grand Abolisher** ({W}{W}) stops opponents casting spells or using creature, artifact and enchantment abilities during your turn.

### The stax layer is what makes it unfair

Elesh Norn, Grand Cenobite shrinks every opposing creature by 2/2 and grows yours. Elesh Norn, Mother of Machines doubles your own enter triggers and blanks theirs. Sheoldred, the Apocalypse taxes their draw steps; Sheoldred, Whispering One makes each opponent sacrifice a creature every upkeep. **Aura Shards** destroys an artifact or enchantment every time a creature enters under your control, so every reanimation, Grave Titan Zombie, and Kitten blink is removal. **Grave Pact** ({1}{B}{B}{B}): whenever a creature you control dies, each other player sacrifices a creature. Every free sacrifice, from a Zombie token to Stitcher's Supplier to a Kitchen Finks that persists back, is an edict on the whole table. It wants the Swamps and black duals.

### Removal

Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile and Anguished Unmaking ({1}{W}{B}, exile any nonland permanent, you lose 3) handle one problem each. **Summon: Bahamut** is a 9/9 flying Saga creature for {9}, and a reanimation target as much as a spell. Its chapters I and II each destroy a nonland permanent, III draws two, and IV, **Mega Flare**, deals damage equal to the total mana value of your other permanents to each opponent before it's sacrificed. Flickering it starts it over at chapter I with two more destroys. **Karn's Bastion** ({4}, {T}: proliferate) moves Bahamut's lore toward Mega Flare and grows +1/+1 counters, such as Triskelion's. **Toxic Deluge** ({2}{B}, pay X life) and **Austere Command** ({4}{W}{W}) are the resets. Austere Command is "choose two": destroy all artifacts, all enchantments, all creatures with mana value 3 or less, or all creatures with mana value 4 or greater. Pick the two that hit the table and spare your own pieces (for example, big creatures and artifacts when your board is small creatures and enchantments), and reanimation rebuilds afterwards. **Living Death** is a third reset that leaves you the better board when your graveyard is the deep one. Bojuka Bog exiles a graveyard when it enters.

### Mana, lands and utility

Chromatic Lantern lets every land tap for any colour. The mana base is the full set of original duals and on-colour fetch lands: the six fetches (Flooded Strand, Polluted Delta, Windswept Heath, Marsh Flats, Verdant Catacombs, Misty Rainforest) each find any of two basic types, so each one gets any dual or shock with a matching type, and Muldrotha can replay them. **Crucible of Worlds** ({3}) lets you play lands from your graveyard: a fetch land every turn, Strip Mine every turn, and any land Glimpse the Unthinkable or Stitcher's Supplier milled. Strip Mine is optional land destruction. **Phyrexian Tower** taps for {C}, or taps and sacrifices a creature for {B}{B}, so it is a land and a sacrifice outlet. Blood Artist and Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER drain whenever creatures die, so Victimize's sacrifice, Grave Pact, Skullclamp and the loops all become life loss for the table. Viscera Seer is a one-mana free sacrifice outlet, and **Phyrexian Altar** ({3}) sacrifices a creature for one mana of any colour, a third altar beside Ashnod's Altar and Altar of Dementia. Mikaeus, Kitchen Finks, Triskelion, Nim Deathmantle, Ashnod's Altar, Altar of Dementia, Phyrexian Altar and Grave Titan are the combo pieces below. Archon of Cruelty, Rune-Scarred Demon and Sheoldred, Whispering One are bombs to reanimate rather than hard-cast.

### How it wins

Three infinite loops (see Key lines), plus fair lines when the loops are answered:
- Archon of Cruelty rebought every turn (Sheoldred, Whispering One, or Muldrotha recasting Animate Dead).
- Altar of Dementia milling one player out with a big creature.
- Elesh Norn, Grand Cenobite's -2/-2 is a one-sided board shrink. Sheoldred, the Apocalypse and the Arena's draws drain the table slowly.
- Aura Shards and Grave Pact grind down their board and their hand of permanents.

### Weak spots and how to play around them

- **Graveyard and sacrifice hate.** Rest in Peace stops every loop. Counter it with Arcane Denial or Swan Song, or remove it with Aura Shards (once a creature of yours enters), Austere Command or Anguished Unmaking.
- **Torpor Orb and similar** shut off enter-the-battlefield payoffs. Remove them with Aura Shards or Anguished Unmaking first.
- **Exile-based removal and theft** on a reanimated bomb: keep Ephemerate or Restoration Angel up for it.
- **Wipes**: Arcane Denial, Ephemerate on the key creature, or hold the reanimation spell until after the wipe.
- **Fast combo decks.** The deck is slower than the very fastest decks; use the cheap counters (Arcane Denial, Swan Song) and the cheap tutors to win first or stop them.
- **Colour screw.** The commander costs four colours and the combo pieces need black, white and green. Count coloured sources for what you are about to cast and use Chromatic Lantern, Birds of Paradise and the fetch lands (crack a fetch for the dual that gives the colour you are short of).

## Key lines

The deck has **three infinite loops**. The Kitchen Finks loop needs a free sacrifice outlet: **Viscera Seer**, **Ashnod's Altar**, **Altar of Dementia**, or **Phyrexian Altar**. Mikaeus + Triskelion needs only the two cards.

| # | Pieces | Result | Kills alone? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Mikaeus + Triskelion | Infinite damage | Yes |
| 2 | Mikaeus + Kitchen Finks + free sac outlet | Infinite life, ETBs, and deaths | Needs a payoff |
| 3 | Nim Deathmantle + Ashnod's Altar + Grave Titan | Infinite colourless mana, ETBs, and deaths | Needs a payoff |

**Mikaeus + Triskelion.** Mikaeus gives Triskelion undying and +1/+1, so with no counters it's a 2/2. When undying returns it with four counters (a 6/6), ping itself twice and opponents twice: the damage stays marked, and once the last counter is gone it's a 2/2 with 2 damage, so it dies with no counters and comes back again. That's 2 damage to opponents per loop, with no outlet needed. (The first time, with three counters, it's two pings at itself and one at an opponent.) **Elesh Norn, Grand Cenobite** breaks this version: her +2/+2 makes it a 4/4 with no counters, so all four pings have to go to itself. With her out, or Nim Deathmantle on Triskelion, sacrifice it to a free outlet after removing the counters instead: 4 damage per loop.

**Mikaeus + Kitchen Finks.** Persist and undying alternate: each return gives Finks one kind of counter, which leaves the other ability free on the next death. Every loop gains 2 life.

**Nim Deathmantle + Ashnod's Altar + Grave Titan.** Sacrificing Titan gives {C}{C}, Deathmantle's {4} returns it, and its two Zombies sacrifice for {C}{C}{C}{C}. That's net +{2} per loop; the first time round you need two more mana (or sacrifice Zombies first). With Elesh Norn, Mother of Machines each return makes four Zombies.

**Payoffs that turn loops 2 and 3 into kills:**
- **Blood Artist or Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER:** each death drains the table (Sephiroth drains one target opponent per death). Sephiroth flips on the fourth drain in a turn, and his emblem keeps draining if he is removed afterwards.
- **Altar of Dementia as the outlet:** Finks mills a player every loop, so loop 2 wins without a drain creature; opponents lose on their next draw. The Deathmantle loop can use it too, sacrificing the Zombies to mill.
- **Ashnod's Altar or Phyrexian Altar as the outlet:** loop 2 also makes infinite mana (colourless from Ashnod's Altar, any colour from Phyrexian Altar).
- **Aura Shards:** every Finks or Titan return and every Zombie destroys an artifact or enchantment, which clears the table's.
- **Triskelion with loop 3:** the infinite mana keeps paying Deathmantle to return Triskelion for three more pings.
- **Grave Pact:** every creature of yours that dies makes each opponent sacrifice a creature, so any loop also empties their boards.

**Shared weak points.** Mikaeus is in two loops, so the Deathmantle loop is the backup if he is answered, and Triskelion covers you if Finks is. Graveyard and sacrifice hate hurts all three, since every loop relies on creatures dying and returning: Rest in Peace stops them outright, and a Torpor Orb-style card (no ETB triggers) shuts off the payoffs.

**Protect the combo turn.** Resolve **Grand Abolisher** first: opponents can't cast spells or activate abilities of artifacts, creatures, or enchantments during your turn. **Arcane Denial**, **Swan Song**, and **Ephemerate** cover what Abolisher doesn't, and Ephemerate also works on opponents' turns, when Abolisher does nothing.

**Openings.**
- Turn 1: Stitcher's Supplier, Entomb, Birds of Paradise, or Sol Ring. Turn 2: Sol Ring plus a Talisman or Signet puts Reanimate or Animate Dead in range for turn 3.
- A fast line: Entomb (or Buried Alive) for Archon of Cruelty or Grave Titan, then Reanimate or Animate Dead the same turn or the next. With Dark Ritual, a Swamp pays for Entomb and Reanimate on turn one.
- Cast Demonic Tutor into Entomb on turn two to set up Reanimate on turn three.

**Mulligans.** Keep hands with two or three lands (or a land plus Sol Ring), a way to fill the yard or find a reanimation spell, and a play before turn 4. A hand that is all reanimation spells with no way to fill the yard is slow; a hand that is all big creatures needs mana. Don't keep one-landers.

## Tutor targets by board state

Four tutors: **Demonic Tutor** (any card to hand), **Vampiric Tutor** ({B} instant, any card to the top of your library, you lose 2), **Gifts Ungiven** (four different cards, opponent splits them between your hand and graveyard) and **Rune-Scarred Demon** (any card to hand when it enters, so every reanimation or blink of it tutors again).

- **One combo piece away → tutor it.** With Mikaeus out, Demonic or Vampiric Tutor for Triskelion (no outlet needed), or for Kitchen Finks if a sac outlet is out too. With Ashnod's Altar and Grave Titan out, tutor Nim Deathmantle. Resolve Grand Abolisher first if you can.
- **Empty yard, reanimation spell already in hand → tutor Entomb (or Buried Alive).** Demonic Tutor into Entomb on turn two sets up Reanimate on turn three.
- **Yard loaded, no way to cheat it out → tutor a reanimation spell.** Demonic Tutor leaves the most mana over to cast it the same turn.
- **Want both halves at once → Gifts Ungiven** for a reanimation target plus reanimation spells, or for Mikaeus, Triskelion, and two reanimation spells — any split between hand and graveyard still leaves both pieces in reach.
- **Loop assembled, need to close → tutor the payoff.** Blood Artist or Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER (or Altar of Dementia as the outlet) turns loops 2 and 3 into a kill; Yawgmoth's Will once the yard is deep.
- **Long game, combo answered → Muldrotha.** She replays the loop pieces, Aura Shards, and a reanimation spell from the graveyard every turn.
- **Under pressure → tutor interaction.** Toxic Deluge or Austere Command to reset, or Swords / Path / Anguished Unmaking for one problem.

Vampiric Tutor puts the card on top, so it costs your next draw; cast it at the end of the opponent's turn before yours. **Open the Armory** ({1}{W}) finds an Aura or Equipment: Animate Dead, Skullclamp or Nim Deathmantle. Entomb and Buried Alive also work as combo tutors here: they put a creature piece in the yard for Reanimate, Animate Dead, Necromancy, or Victimize.

## Mana and consistency

The list is exactly 100 cards. 37 lands: 24 nonbasic plus 5 Swamp, 3 Forest, 3 Plains, 2 Island (13 basics). The nonbasics are the six original duals (Tundra, Underground Sea, Bayou, Savannah, Scrubland, Tropical Island), five shocks (Hallowed Fountain, Overgrown Tomb, Temple Garden, Watery Grave, Godless Shrine; pay 2 life to enter untapped), two pain lands (Caves of Koilos, Underground River), the six on-colour fetch lands (Flooded Strand, Polluted Delta, Windswept Heath, Marsh Flats, Verdant Catacombs, Misty Rainforest), Command Tower, Bojuka Bog (tapped), Strip Mine, Karn's Bastion and Phyrexian Tower. Every pair of the deck's four colours has a dual, so a fetch finds whichever two colours the hand is missing; only Bojuka Bog enters tapped. The fetches and shocks cost life (1 per fetch, 2 per untapped shock), which Sheoldred, the Apocalypse, Kitchen Finks and the drains give back. Karn's Bastion is a colourless land that proliferates; Phyrexian Tower is a colourless land that sacrifices a creature for {B}{B}.

Mana: four rocks (Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Talisman of Hierarchy, Chromatic Lantern), two creatures (Birds of Paradise, Sakura-Tribe Elder), Dark Ritual, and sacrifice mana from Phyrexian Altar and Phyrexian Tower. The deck cheats its threats in, so it has less ramp than a fair deck; Nim Deathmantle + Ashnod's Altar is mana only as part of the combo.

**Graveyard fill.** Stitcher's Supplier, Buried Alive, Entomb, Glimpse the Unthinkable, Tortured Existence and Gifts Ungiven fill the yard. Displacer Kitten and the flicker cards replace some of the filling with value.

**Reanimation count is six** (Reanimate, Grave Researcher // Reanimate, Animate Dead, Necromancy, Victimize, Dread Return) plus Living Death (mass), Sheoldred, Whispering One, Yawgmoth's Will, and Muldrotha (Animate Dead or Necromancy again from the graveyard every turn). Every reanimation spell can take a legendary creature (Victimize takes two creature cards from your own graveyard, Dread Return one).

**Card draw.** Rhystic Study, Phyrexian Arena, Skullclamp, Sheoldred (gain life per draw), Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER's sacrifice-to-draw, and Atraxa's own enter trigger.

**Interaction.** Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile and Anguished Unmaking for spot removal, plus two destroys from Summon: Bahamut; Aura Shards for repeatable artifact/enchantment removal; Austere Command, Toxic Deluge and Living Death as resets; Arcane Denial and Swan Song as counters; Ephemerate, Grand Abolisher, Restoration Angel and the Teleportation Circle / Conjurer's Closet flickers as protection.

## Bracket and Rule 0

**Bracket 4.** Five Game Changers: **Demonic Tutor**, **Vampiric Tutor**, **Gifts Ungiven**, **Aura Shards** and **Rhystic Study**, more than Bracket 3 allows. The deck would be Bracket 4 anyway because of its three infinite loops, including the two-card Mikaeus + Triskelion, all tutorable. Displacer Kitten, Grand Abolisher, Nim Deathmantle, Triskelion, Kitchen Finks, Crucible of Worlds, Living Death, and Muldrotha are off-list.

Disclose before the game:

- **Three infinite loops:** Mikaeus + Triskelion (infinite damage, no outlet needed); Mikaeus + Kitchen Finks + a sac outlet (infinite life and deaths); Nim Deathmantle + Ashnod's Altar + Grave Titan (infinite mana, ETBs, and deaths).
- The five Game Changers above, especially **Demonic Tutor**, **Vampiric Tutor** and **Gifts Ungiven** (tutors for the loop pieces).
- **Grand Abolisher**, which shuts off their interaction on your turn.
- **Yawgmoth's Will**, which can produce a long turn out of nowhere.
- **Austere Command**, a sweeper that can hit your own board too.
- **Living Death**, which swaps every board for every graveyard.
- **Strip Mine** as land kill: one-shot, or **every turn with Crucible of Worlds or Muldrotha** replaying it from the graveyard.
- **Muldrotha** recasts a creature, an artifact, and an enchantment (Animate Dead, Necromancy) from your graveyard each turn.
- The deck reanimates out of *any* graveyard, so opponents' creatures dying is relevant information for them.

Re-check the official list at https://commanderbrackets.com/faq before an event.
