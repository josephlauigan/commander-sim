# Tymna the Weaver & Kraum, Ludevic's Opus — Four-Colour Partners (W/U/B/R)

**Added 2026-10-13.** The comparison build for the Tymna deck (`tymna-thrasios-partners.md`): the same idea with
Kraum instead of Thrasios. **40 of its 100 cards are Jodah leftovers** (Tymna among them), against 47 for the Thrasios
build: red adds Terror of the Peaks, Razia, Caparocti Sunborn, Marchesa, Sol'kanar and six red lands, and loses the
green ramp, Worldly Tutor, Dromoka, Tolsimir, King Darien, Mirri, Lagrella and Privileged Position. Desertion and
Dissipate (Jodah cards cut on 2026-10-09) come back from the binder. The rest are 46 bulk cards, 11 basics and
**Kraum, the one buy**.

**Not in the simulator yet:** no deck key, not modelled, not measured. Built to be measured against the Thrasios
build; JD's choice between the two is pending.

## Strategy

**Tymna the Weaver** ({1}{W}{B}, 2/2 lifelink): *at the beginning of each of your postcombat main phases, you may pay
X life, where X is the number of opponents that were dealt combat damage this turn. If you do, draw X cards.*
**Kraum, Ludevic's Opus** ({3}{U}{R}, 4/4): *flying, haste; whenever an opponent casts their second spell each turn,
draw a card.*

- **Hit everyone, draw cards.** As in the Thrasios build: cheap creatures that are hard to block (The Wasp, Falcon and
  Redwing, Nightwind Glider, Abigale, Moseo, Aven Mindcensor, Elemental Mascot, Firebird, Photon, Nightveil Predator,
  Sphinx of New Prahv; Exit Specialist; menace from Lightning, Security Sergeant and Black Widow; Spectral Lynx), and
  Kraum himself, a hasty 4/4 flier. Reconnaissance Mission and The Aetherspark draw off the same hits.
- **Both commanders draw without mana.** Kraum draws off the opponents' second spells, Tymna off combat, so the mana
  goes to creatures and interaction. Laelia, Lightning and Expressive Iteration add cards from the top of the library.
- **Finish.** Terror of the Peaks (damage whenever your creatures enter), Lyra, Elenda, Razia, Caparocti, Marchesa,
  Sol'kanar and Dakkon Blackblade; Firebird pumps the other attackers.
- **Interact.** Sixteen removal spells (Toxic Deluge the only sweeper), five counterspells (Force of Will, Arcane
  Denial, Mana Leak, Dissipate, Desertion) and three protection cards (Bastion Protector, Ultimate Magic: Holy,
  Shardmage's Rescue).

## Key lines

- **Turns 1-3:** a two-drop flier, then Tymna on turn 3. Talismans or Potioner's Trove toward Kraum on turn 4.
- **Kraum, then attack at once:** he has haste, so he triggers Tymna the turn he lands.
- **Attack every opponent you can, then draw;** pay Tymna's life only as far as your life total allows.

## Consistency

36 lands and only five mana rocks (Talisman of Hierarchy, Talisman of Conviction, Chromatic Lantern, Star Compass,
Potioner's Trove): no green ramp, and a five-mana commander. 16 lands are Jodah's, 9 come from bulk (Glacial Fortress,
Port Town, Clifftop Retreat, Furycalm Snarl, Spectator Seating, Rugged Prairie and three Temples), 11 basics (3
Island, 3 Plains, 3 Swamp, 2 Mountain). Unclaimed Territory: name **Human**.

## Bracket and Rule 0

**Bracket 3,** with one Game Changer: **Force of Will**. No two-card infinite combos, no mass land destruction, no
extra turns. Disclose Aven Mindcensor.
