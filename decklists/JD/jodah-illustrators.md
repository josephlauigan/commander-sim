# Cards illustrated by Donato Giancola and Richard Kane Ferguson

printing by Donato Giancola (DG) or Richard Kane Ferguson (RKF), from Scryfall (`a:"<artist>" game:paper`), fetched

lists those printings. **In list** marks the cards in Jodah's list now. Commander: legal unless noted; GC = Game Changer.
Lands are exempt from the theme (any land can go in the deck).
