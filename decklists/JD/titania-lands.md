# Titania, Protector of Argoth — Mono-Green Lands to the Graveyard

**Added 2026-10-11.** An alternative to the Bristly Bill deck, built from JD's bulk and binder around Titania,
Protector of Argoth (borderless Secret Lair printing, about $2.46) with **no other cards bought**, apart from
Arachnogenesis and Blossoming Defense (already bought 2026-10-11). Measured 22.4% over all tiers (see "Simulator status"). It shares
most of its cards with the Bill deck; only one of the two would be built.

## Strategy

Whenever a land you control goes from the battlefield to the graveyard, Titania makes a 5/3 Elemental. When she
enters, she returns a land from your graveyard to the battlefield.

- **Lands that go to the graveyard.** Only three in the collection: Evolving Wilds, Terramorphic Expanse and Fabled
  Passage sacrifice themselves to find a basic. Each is a 5/3, and the basic it finds is landfall too. Titania's own
  enter trigger brings one back. This is the deck's weak point without buys: the cheap fixes (Myriad Landscape,
  Escape Tunnel, Harrow, Springbloom Druid, Splendid Reclamation, about $1.80) were left out on purpose, to see the
  deck on what JD already owns.
- **Fill the graveyard and the land drops.** Satyr Wayfinder, Malevolent Rumble, Something Worth Saving, Scout the
  City, Pothole Mole (returns a land to hand) and the land ramp.
- **Cash it in.** Garruk's Uprising draws a card for each 5/3 (power 4 or more) and gives them trample; Overwhelming
  Stampede and End-Raze Forerunners turn a pile of 5/3s into a lethal attack. Big green creatures (Ghalta, Hornet
  Queen, Paladin of Predation, Yavimaya Wurm) carry the deck when the engine is slow.
- **Stay alive.** Arachnogenesis (a fog plus a Spider for each attacker), Blossoming Defense and Royal Treatment to
  protect Titania, and bite and fight spells as removal.

## Consistency

38 lands: 35 Forest plus Evolving Wilds, Terramorphic Expanse and Fabled Passage. **An exception to JD's
basics-over-tapped-lands rule:** the basics they find enter tapped, but each sacrifice is a 5/3, the deck's engine
(unlike the Bill deck, where the same lands measured no better than Forests). Ramp: Llanowar Elves, Noxious Newt,
Hardbristle Bandit, Topiary Lecturer, Silhana Starfletcher, Greenhouse Propagator, Bugenhagen, plus land ramp.

## Simulator status

**Measured 2026-10-11** (look-ahead AI, loose profile, 960 games per tier, the same settings as the other decks;
`decklists/pool/pool-results.md` section 0d, results in `audit/titania/`): T1 20.7%, T2 26.9%, T3 24.5%, T4 23.0%,
T5 17.1%; **22.4% over all tiers**, 7.4 points above the Bill deck (15.0%) and near Veyran (23.7%). Wins come late
(turn 13.9 on average); she is often removed and recast, and each recast returns a fetch land for another 5/3.

Known simplifications: several lands going to the graveyard at once make one 5/3, not one each; day and night
(Infestation Expert) is modeled only as far as it changes that card.

## Where the cards come from

The commander and the two cards bought on 2026-10-11 (Arachnogenesis, Blossoming Defense) are new. The 35 Forests are
your own. Every other card came off JD's lists on 2026-10-11, one copy each: Llanowar Elves and Evolving Wilds from the
binder, the other 60 from bulk.
