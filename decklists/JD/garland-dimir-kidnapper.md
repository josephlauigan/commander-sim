# Garland, Royal Kidnapper — Dimir Monarch Kidnapper

**Added 2026-10-13.** JD's Dimir deck around Garland, Royal Kidnapper (owned: one of the retired Jodah deck's cards).
Built entirely from the bulk and binder lists, with no buys: **20 of its 100 cards are Jodah leftovers** (Garland
among them). A rarely played commander (EDHREC rank about 12,000) with a political play style none of JD's other decks
has.

**Not measured yet:** the overnight run of 2026-10-13 is its first.

## Strategy

**Garland, Royal Kidnapper** ({2}{U}{B}, 3/4): *When Garland enters, target opponent becomes the monarch. Whenever an
opponent becomes the monarch, gain control of target creature that player controls for as long as they're the
monarch. Creatures you control but don't own get +2/+2 and can't be sacrificed.*

The monarch draws a card at the end of each of their turns, and whoever deals combat damage to the monarch takes the
crown. Garland's deck never wears the crown itself: it hands it to an opponent and profits each time it moves.

- **Crown an opponent, take their best creature.** Garland gives the crown to the opponent whose creature is worth
  most (often their commander) and steals it: +2/+2 and unsacrificeable while you have it.
- **Let them fight over it.** The other opponents attack the monarch for the extra card. Each time the crown moves to
  another opponent, Garland triggers again: the steal hops to the new monarch's best creature.
- **Never hit the monarch.** Combat damage to the monarch would make you the monarch and end every steal: stolen
  creatures attack the other opponents.
- **Re-crown on demand.** Each time Garland enters, you choose the next monarch: return him to your hand (Rescue,
  Pepper Potts; Matterbending Mage; Peerless Ropemaster; Specimen Freighter; Whoosh!; Unsummon) or copy him (Relm's
  Sketching, Echocasting Symposium) and recast. The steal lasts as long as the crown stays with that player, even if
  Garland leaves.
- **Permanent steals:** Desertion (counter a creature or artifact spell and take it) and Coerced to Kill; both get
  Garland's +2/+2.
- **Control shell:** eight counterspells (Force of Will, Arcane Denial and friends), ten removal spells, Fact or
  Fiction, Mordenkainen, Urza; evasive threats (Nightveil Predator, Soul of the Rapids, Szadek, Xande, Ultimecia,
  Professor Hulk); Crawlspace for defence.

## Key lines

- **Turns 1-3:** mana, counterspells, card draw; stay quiet.
- **Turn 4: Garland,** crowning the opponent with the best creature to take.
- **Middle game:** stolen creatures and fliers attack the players who aren't the monarch; recast or copy Garland when
  the crown stalls or a better target appears.
- **Watch the crown's card draw:** the monarch draws an extra card each turn; keep it moving or close the game.

## Consistency

36 lands: Underground River, Command Tower, Exotic Orchard, Path of Ancestry, Unclaimed Territory (name Human: Garland
is one) and Vivid Creek from Jodah; Dimir Guildgate, Sinister Hideout, Temple of Deceit, Treno, Vivid Marsh and
Evolving Wilds from bulk; 12 Island and 12 Swamp. Ramp is light (Chromatic Lantern, Star Compass, Potioner's Trove,
Silver Deputy, Strixhaven Skycoach, Hydro-Channeler): JD owns no Dimir Signet or Talisman.

## Bracket and Rule 0

**Bracket 3:** one Game Changer, **Force of Will**. No two-card combos, no mass land destruction, no extra turns.
Explain the monarch and Garland's steal at the start; it changes how the table attacks.
