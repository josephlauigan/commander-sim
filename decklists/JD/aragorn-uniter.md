# Aragorn, the Uniter — Four-Colour Spell Burn

**Built 2026-11-06 (JD).** This deck replaces Kefka and Sauron, which are retired the same day, and is built from those
two lists plus `binder-cards.txt`; `documents/aragorn-merge-swaps.md` is the physical swap guide. Eleven cards are
bought: Aragorn and ten upgrades (see "Where the cards come from"). Deck key `aragorn`, tiers t4-t5.

A four-colour spell deck. Every spell you cast triggers Aragorn, and red spells are the clock: 3 damage to an
opponent each, doubled by Veyran on instants and sorceries and tripled by Fiery Emancipation. White spells make
chump-blockers, blue spells smooth draws, and green spells pump. It keeps Kefka's Underworld Breach storm finish and
its pingers. The black half of both decks (reanimation, black removal, the discard engine) is gone. White and green
ramp, protection and creature defence from the binder replace it.

## Strategy

### The commander
**Aragorn, the Uniter** ({R}{G}{W}{U}, 5/5) reads: *Whenever you cast a white spell, create a 1/1 white Human
Soldier creature token. Whenever you cast a blue spell, scry 2. Whenever you cast a red spell, Aragorn deals 3 damage
to target opponent. Whenever you cast a green spell, target creature gets +4/+4 until end of turn.*

- **Cast triggers only.** Copies (Grapeshot's storm copies, Twinned Vision's copy) don't trigger him. Flashback and
  escape and rebound casts do, so Underworld Breach, Ephemerate and Mystic Sanctuary repeat triggers.
- **A multicolour spell triggers once for each of its colours.** Twinned Vision is blue and red (scry 2 and 3 damage).
  Kitchen Finks is green and white (a +4/+4 and a token). Growth Spiral, Third Path Iconoclast and Expressive Iteration also have two
  colours.
- **Veyran doubles all four triggers on instants and sorceries.** A red instant is 6 damage, a white one makes two
  Soldiers.
- **Fiery Emancipation triples his damage:** each red spell is 9 damage (18 with Veyran on an instant or sorcery).
- **Defence.** The white Soldiers chump-block creature decks, which killed Kefka most (Tergrid, Aurelia and Marwyn
  were about 62% of its eliminations). Skullclamp turns each Soldier into two cards, and Aura Shards turns each into
  an artifact or enchantment kill.

### Game plan
- **Turns 1 to 3.** Mana: Birds of Paradise, Noble Hierarch, Three Visits, Nature's Lore, Growth Spiral, Sol
  Ring, Arcane Signet, Smothering Tithe. Aragorn costs one of each colour; Three Visits and Nature's Lore fetch a Forest dual
  (Taiga, Tropical Island, Savannah, Temple Garden) untapped to fix it. Cheap pingers and token makers (Firebrand
  Archer, Third Path Iconoclast, Young Pyromancer) start the burn and the wall. Rhystic Study draws all game.
- **Turns 4 to 6.** Cast Aragorn, protect him (Lightning Greaves, Heroic Intervention, Teferi's Protection, Blossoming Defense, Deflecting
  Swat), and chain cheap spells. Hold counters (Counterspell, Mana Drain, Swan Song, Fierce Guardianship, Force of Will) for
  the turn that matters.
- **Late game.** Win with a burn turn: Jeska's Will, rituals and Underworld Breach, or Fiery Emancipation with any
  red spells.

### How it wins
1. **Underworld Breach storm.** Breach gives every nonland card in the graveyard escape. Seething Song (+2 mana),
   Desperate Ritual and Rite of Flame (+1 each) and Manamorphose (free, draws) are red spells that make mana when
   escaped, so each escape is 3 damage from Aragorn on top. Brain Freeze, milling yourself, refills the graveyard as
   the storm count rises, and Grapeshot ends it. This is Kefka's old line, with Aragorn adding 3 damage per red
   spell.
2. **Jeska's Will turn.** Exile the top three and play them, plus mana for each card in an opponent's hand, then cast
   cheap red spells with Guttersnipe, Firebrand Archer and Third Path Iconoclast out.
3. **Fiery Emancipation.** With it out, every red spell is 9 damage and every pinger trigger triples.
4. **Grind.** Niv-Mizzet, Parun and Talrand turn every instant into a card or a Drake. Elspeth and Restoration
   Angel hold the board.

### Weak spots
- **Mana.** Aragorn needs {R}{G}{W}{U}, and Triple Triad and Fiery Emancipation each need {R}{R}{R}. The base is 35
  lands (all six original duals, 6 fetches, 4 three-colour lands), 3 green mana creatures, 2 land fetchers, 3 rocks and
  Smothering Tithe.
- **Aragorn is the engine.** Without him the deck is a pile of cheap spells with pingers. It has seven ways to protect
  him, and Talrand and Niv-Mizzet keep the spell plan going without him.
- **No graveyard recursion beyond Breach and Mystic Sanctuary.** Graveyard hate hurts the storm line.

### The ten upgrades (bought instead of binder or Kefka filler)
| Buy | Replaces | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Swords to Plowshares | Austere Command | 1-mana removal instead of a 6-mana wipe, still a white trigger |
| Teferi's Protection | Lagrella, the Magpie | Protects Aragorn and the combo turn |
| Smothering Tithe | Paradise Druid | Treasure from every opponent's draw |
| Noble Hierarch | Llanowar Elves | Makes three of Aragorn's four colours, plus exalted |
| Three Visits | Rampant Growth | Fetches a Forest dual (Taiga, Tropical Island, Savannah, Temple Garden) untapped |
| Nature's Lore | Sakura-Tribe Elder | The same as Three Visits; the land comes in untapped, where Sakura-Tribe Elder's comes in tapped |
| Growth Spiral | Coiling Oracle | Instant ramp and a card: green and blue triggers |
| Fierce Guardianship | Arcane Denial | Free with Aragorn out, no cards for opponents |
| Third Path Iconoclast | Kessig Flamebreather | Red and blue on cast, and a Soldier token for every noncreature spell |
| Rhystic Study | Think Twice | Card draw all game |

All ten are Commander-legal and inside Aragorn's colours. New Tymna already runs Swords, Tithe, Rhystic Study, Fierce
Guardianship and Noble Hierarch, so these are new copies. **Optional:** Lion's Eye Diamond for Rite of Flame would make
Breach + LED + Brain Freeze a real combo (not chosen yet; the New Tymna copy is the only one).

**If Sauron stays a deck,** only the six "Sauron only" cards conflict, plus whichever of the nine shared cards the
physical Kefka copy doesn't cover.

## Main cuts from Kefka and Sauron (legal but left out)
- **Discard and looting:** Rummaging Goblin, Garrison Excavator, Stadium Tidalmage, Harmonic Prodigy, Blazing
  Firesinger, Birgi (the deck no longer wants to discard).
- **Expensive or off-plan:** Niv-Mizzet the Firemind and Psychosis Crawler (the Kefka draw-to-damage win needed the
  flip), Jin-Gitaxias, Inferno Titan, Gilgamesh, Godo, Bribery, Solve the Equation, Chandra's Ignition, Nibelheim
  Aflame, Not of This World.
- **Small value:** Reality Shift, Price of Progress, Seize the Spoils, Palantír of Orthanc, Vandalblast (Aragorn's
  own rocks), Propaganda, Slip Out the Back, An Offer You Can't Refuse, the Marvel creatures, Mind Stone, Fellwar
  Stone, the Talismans.
- **Karn leftovers:** none besides Tezzeret and Skullclamp. Aragorn's triggers need coloured spells.

## Bracket and Rule 0
**Bracket 4,** with 11 Game Changers: **Underworld Breach**, **Jeska's Will**, **Force of Will**, **Cyclonic Rift**,
**Mystical Tutor**, **Worldly Tutor**, **Aura Shards**, **Teferi's Protection**, **Smothering Tithe**, **Fierce
Guardianship** and **Rhystic Study**. That is well past Bracket 3's limit of three, so the deck is Bracket 4 only.
There's no two-card infinite combo. The Breach line needs the graveyard to fill first, as in Kefka (Lion's Eye
Diamond would change that).
