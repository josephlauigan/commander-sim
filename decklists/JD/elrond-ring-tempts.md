# Elrond, Lord of Rivendell — Mono-Blue Ring Tempts

**Added 2026-10-12.** JD's candidate for a last deck with no black or green (no Swamps or Forests left). Built from the
bulk and binder lists with no buys; Elrond and Gandalf come from the binder. In the simulator as deck key `elrond`.

## Strategy

Elrond, Lord of Rivendell ({2}{U}, 3/3) reads: *Whenever Elrond or another creature you control enters, scry 1. If this
is the second time this ability has resolved this turn, the Ring tempts you.*

**Two creatures a turn.** Every second creature entering in a turn (yours or an opponent's turn) makes the Ring tempt
you. The deck is built to get there: cards that make two bodies at once (Coral Barrier, Phantom Interference,
Unfathomable Truths), token spells (Muse's Encouragement, Retrieve the Esper, Relm's Sketching, Echocasting Symposium),
flash creatures for the other players' turns (Divining Duelist, Hope-Ender Coatl, Rescue, The Wasp, Gandalf), and
warp creatures that enter twice (Starbreach Whale, Starwinder).

**The Ring.** Each tempt grows the Ring-bearer: legendary and unblockable by greater power; loot when it attacks; a
blocker is sacrificed; each opponent loses 3 when it hits. Gandalf, Friend of the Shire draws a card each time the Ring
tempts you and you choose another creature.

**Re-buying enter effects.** Matterbending Mage, Peerless Ropemaster, Exit Specialist, Rescue, Fear of Isolation,
Unsummon and Whoosh! return your own creatures so Ultimecia, Orysa, Mnemonic Wall, Deluge Virtuoso and the rest enter
again (and count toward Elrond's second trigger).

**Interaction.** Mana Leak, Essence Scatter, Dissipate, Dismiss, Statute of Denial, Desertion (a countered creature
joins you), Amazing Acrobatics, Icy Reception; bounce and tuck (Sudden Setback, Vanish from Sight, Banishing Betrayal,
Void Snare); Kasmina's Transmutation; Crawlspace.

## Where the cards come from

**From the binder:** Elrond, Lord of Rivendell, Gandalf, Friend of the Shire, Crawlspace, Desertion, Dismiss,
Dissipate. **Everything else:** bulk, plus 32 Islands of your own.
