# Yoshimaru/Tymna rebuild: physical swap guide

This guide turns the Tymna/Thrasios Doomsday box into the Yoshimaru/Tymna blink deck and moves the Doomsday and
Thassa's Oracle package into the Y'shtola box. Both new lists contain exactly 100 cards.

- **New Yoshimaru/Tymna blink deck** (Bracket 4): `decklists/JD/yoshimaru-tymna-blink.md`
- **New Y'shtola** (Esper drain with the Doomsday package): `decklists/JD/yshtola-esper-drain.md`

This guide knows only what the deck lists say. It does not know what else you own, so every card that has to come from
outside the two boxes is listed under "Cards to find" for you to tick off.

## 0. Before you start

**What this guide assumes.** It was built from the deck lists, not from the boxes. It assumes the Tymna/Thrasios box
holds exactly its current list (including Doomsday, Brainstorm, Preordain, Ponder and the Swamp), and that the Y'shtola
box holds her list as it stood before 2026-11-14, which includes Disdainful Stroke (swapped in for Dovin's Veto on
2026-11-13). If your Y'shtola box still has Dovin's Veto, add Disdainful Stroke to "Cards to find" and put Dovin's Veto
in the Spare pile. If any other card is missing from a box, add it to "Cards to find".

1. Make three piles: **New Yoshimaru/Tymna**, **New Y'shtola** and **Spare**. Spare means cards that neither new deck
   needs from these two boxes; it is a place to put them, not a record of what you own.
2. Do the Tymna/Thrasios box first, then the Y'shtola box. The Y'shtola box gives 4 cards to the new
   Yoshimaru/Tymna deck.
3. Get a second copy of **Silence** and **Grand Abolisher** for Y'shtola (your decision, 2026-11-14): the Tymna/Thrasios
   box has one copy of each and both new decks play them. The Tymna/Thrasios copies stay in the Yoshimaru/Tymna deck.
4. Find the cards in section 3.

## 4. Check the counts

- **New Yoshimaru/Tymna:** 22 from the Tymna/Thrasios box + 1 Swamp already in it + 4 from the
  Y'shtola box + 56 to find + 17 basics to find = 100, including both commanders (Yoshimaru, Ever Faithful and
  Tymna the Weaver).
- **New Y'shtola:** 88 in the box + 10 from the Tymna/Thrasios box + 2 second copies = 100.
- **Spare:** 67 from the Tymna/Thrasios box + 8 from the Y'shtola box = 75 cards.
- Check each pile against the import list at the end of its deck document. Every card appears once, except basic lands
  (Yoshimaru/Tymna: 11 Plains and 7 Swamp; Y'shtola: 6 Plains, 5 Island and 8 Swamp).

## 5. Cards both new decks play

- **Silence and Grand Abolisher:** one copy of each is in the Tymna/Thrasios box. They stay there for Yoshimaru/Tymna;
  Y'shtola gets second copies (section 3).
- **The 9 cards in "Second copies of cards that Y'shtola's box also holds":** each deck keeps its own copy.
- **The 9 cards that both boxes already hold:** nothing moves. Arcane Signet, Command Tower, Demonic Tutor, Godless Shrine, Marsh Flats, Orcish Bowmasters, Scrubland, Sol Ring, Vampiric Tutor.
