# Doomsday Tymna and artifact Thanos rebuild: physical swap guide

This guide turns four built decks (Tymna/Thrasios, Thanos, Karn and The Ur-Dragon) into two new decks plus binder
cards. Both new lists contain exactly 100 cards.

- **New Tymna/Thrasios Doomsday** (cEDH): `decklists/JD/tymna-thrasios-doomsday.md`
- **New Thanos Artifacts and Dragons** (High Bracket 4): `decklists/JD/thanos-artifacts-and-dragons.md`

Where two old decks each hold a copy of the same card, the guide names which copy goes into the new deck. The other
copy goes to the binder (listed under "Spare copies" in each step).

## 0. Before you start

1. Make three piles: **New Tymna**, **New Thanos** and **Binder**.
2. Do one deck box at a time, in the order below. Empty the box completely before starting the next one.
3. Buy these four cards for New Tymna. None of them is in the binder or bulk lists:
   - Doomsday
   - Brainstorm
   - Ponder
   - Preordain

| New Tymna | 82 | 14 | 0 | 0 | 4 | 100 |
| New Thanos | 6 | 0 | 67 | 27 | 0 | 100 |

## 5. Check the counts

- **New Tymna:** 82 from Thanos + 14 from Tymna/Thrasios + 4 bought = 100, including both commanders
  (Tymna the Weaver and Thrasios, Triton Hero).
- **New Thanos:** 6 from Thanos + 67 from The Ur-Dragon + 27 from Karn = 100, including the commander.
- Check each pile against the import list at the end of its deck document. Every card appears once; there are no
  basics except one Forest and one Wastes, both in New Thanos.

## 6. Cards both new decks wanted

- **Fierce Guardianship and Deadly Rollick** (free while you control a commander) go to New Tymna; there's only one copy
  of each. New Thanos has Deflecting Swat and Jeska's Will instead.
- **Basalt Monolith, Rings of Brighthearth and Walking Ballista** are in both new decks: New Tymna keeps the
  Tymna/Thrasios copies, and New Thanos takes the Karn copies.
- **Lion's Eye Diamond** goes to New Tymna. Without it, Underworld Breach and Brain Freeze go to the binder.
