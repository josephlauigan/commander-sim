# Ur-Dragon and Sauron rebuild: physical swap guide

This guide moves the compact Godo package from The Ur-Dragon to Sauron, then rebuilds The Ur-Dragon around Dragon
entry triggers, token creation and copies. Both final lists contain exactly 100 cards.

## 1. Move these five cards from The Ur-Dragon to Sauron

- Combat Celebrant
- Godo, Bandit Warlord
- Hammer of Nazahn
- Helm of the Host
- Peter Parker's Camera

These cards are transferred between decks; do not buy second copies for this rebuild.

## 2. Remove these five cards from Sauron

- Phyrexian Arena
- Sauron, the Necromancer
- Sephiroth, Planet's Heir
- Triple Triad
- Urza, Lord High Artificer

Put these with the cards removed from The Ur-Dragon below. They do not move into the rebuilt Ur-Dragon list.

## 3. Remove these fifteen additional cards from The Ur-Dragon

- Brass Squire
- Dualcaster Mage
- Embercleave
- Gilgamesh, Master-at-Arms
- Grand Abolisher
- Imperial Recruiter
- Kaldra Compleat
- Open the Armory
- Puresteel Paladin
- Shadowspear
- Sigarda's Aid
- Steelshaper's Gift
- Stoneforge Mystic
- Sword of Feast and Famine
- Sword of Fire and Ice

Together with the five transferred cards, this opens twenty Ur-Dragon slots.

## 4. Add these twenty cards to The Ur-Dragon

- Astral Dragon
- Atarka, World Render
- Cursed Mirror
- Dracogenesis
- Dragon's Hoard
- Dragon Tempest
- Ganax, Astral Hunter
- Garth One-Eye
- Garruk's Uprising
- Kindred Discovery
- Lathliss, Dragon Queen
- Maelstrom of the Spirit Dragon
- Miirym, Sentinel Wyrm
- Panharmonicon
- Sarkhan, Soul Aflame
- Scion of Draco
- Scourge of Valkas
- Spark Double
- Temur Ascendancy
- Utvara Hellkite

Panharmonicon and Kindred Discovery are also used by the current Galadriel list. Buy separate copies unless spare
copies are already available; do not dismantle Galadriel as part of this rebuild.

Garth One-Eye and Scion of Draco replace Dragonborn Champion and Wrathful Red Dragon from the initial shopping list.
Garth adds a flexible five-colour toolbox; Scion remains a Dragon and supplies team-wide keywords. The purchase count
therefore stays at twenty.

## 5. Check the physical decks

For The Ur-Dragon:

1. Count 100 cards including The Ur-Dragon.
2. Confirm the deck contains 35 lands; Maelstrom of the Spirit Dragon is the new land.
3. Confirm all twenty cards in section 4 are present.
4. Confirm none of the twenty cards removed or transferred in sections 1 and 3 remain.

For Sauron:

1. Count 100 cards including Sauron, the Dark Lord.
2. Confirm Godo, Helm, Hammer, Combat Celebrant and Camera are present.
3. Confirm the five cards in section 2 are absent.
4. Keep Lightning Greaves and Whispersilk Cloak in Sauron; Godo can find either when Helm is unavailable.

## 6. Update the Rule 0 description

The Ur-Dragon remains Bracket 4 because of its sixteen Game Changers, but it no longer contains an intentional
infinite combo. Describe it as a Dragon entry, token and copy deck led by Astral Dragon and Miirym.

Sauron becomes Bracket 4. Disclose the three Game Changers—Cyclonic Rift, Orcish Bowmasters and Force of Will—and the
two infinite-combat combinations: Godo plus Helm of the Host, and Combat Celebrant plus Helm of the Host.

## Simulator notes

Every transferred card is Commander-legal and fits Sauron's blue-black-red identity. Every Ur-Dragon addition is
Commander-legal and fits its five-colour identity. The simulator rates most additions Full or Full-auto. Astral
Dragon, Cursed Mirror, Sarkhan, Soul Aflame and Peter Parker's Camera are Approximate. Garth One-Eye is Partial: the
simulator has his 5/5 body but not his tap ability or the six spells it can create. Scion of Draco is Full. Consult the
deck documents for the real paper interactions rather than treating simulation output as complete for those cards.

No simulation result is claimed for either rebuilt list. Measure Sauron only against its matched T3 and T4 tiers and
The Ur-Dragon only against its matched T5 tier, using the loose interaction profile.
